# MaxGPT-Planck idea ledger

- **What this is:** every idea from the MaxGPT-Planck research in one list: REPORT.md, the 10 lanes, 3 diagnoses, 7 follow-up tracks, 2 brainstorms and the planning chat. Each idea sits in one place only: baseline, ranked bet, parked, or tried and failed.
- **Max's rule:** test as much as possible that has not been tested at this size. Each idea is one change against a well-tuned baseline, run on the same test with 2-3 seeds. Ideas that work join the baseline recipe. Ideas already tried elsewhere are labeled honestly and kept, never dropped for that reason.
- **How to read it:** each bet has one of three novelty labels:
  - **untested-at-this-size:** no test found at 10-150M.
  - **tweak-of-known:** a known method with a new axis, size or domain.
  - **known-apply:** done at or near this size, so apply it.

  Suffixes on the labels: "(relabeled)" means the novelty audit changed the label, "(audit holds)" means the audit confirmed an untested label, and "(not audited)" means nobody checked it. Hours with a ~ are this ledger's estimates from similar entries; hours without one come from the source. A win condition that starts with "Default:" was not set in the source and should be pre-registered before the run.
- **Limits (REPORT s5.1):** #1 updating after a correction, #2 binding under same-type interference, #3 chat training signal and reply policy, #4 tokens per parameter and capacity allocation, #5 degeneration on own history, #6 instruction persistence beyond 3 turns, #7 stored world knowledge, #8 teacher signal, #9 embedding share, #10 output-head rank and depth. D1-D8 are the decisions in REPORT s8. dN is the distance between a fact and the question about it.
- **Date and counts:** 2026-09-23. 29 baseline entries (built from 84 input ideas), 185 ranked bets (14 untested-at-this-size, 126 tweak-of-known, 45 known-apply), 57 parked, 0 tried and failed. Each of the 331 input ideas maps to exactly one entry. No model was loaded or trained to build this ledger.

**Why the top 10 are ranked where they are**

**1. P-001, repaired ft_test.py.** This is the cheapest decisive test on the two top limits, #1 (updating after a correction) and #2 (binding). It asks whether a few hundred fine-tuning steps can install latest-wins updating and binding at a 106M body. A pass raises the odds for Level A to about 30-40%. The two-slot, no-update and held-out-wording controls close the "copy the most recent weekday" shortcut that the original script allowed. The method is not new (bAbI dialog Task 2). It is ranked first because every later #1 and #2 bet depends on its answer. It needs Max to allow Mac model runs, through the single-process guard runner.

**2. P-002, battery controls.** Without these controls the #1 results cannot be read. The main update items are lexically confounded, four sub-1B models fail even at d0, and primacy is not yet separated from position. The work is 4-8 hours of likelihood scoring, on the 8 probed models plus LFM2-700M and 1.2B.

**3. P-003, the sealed RC-12/MTB-150 battery.** Every Planck claim is scored on this battery, including the headline curve (P-054). No programmatic battery below 300M goes past 3 turns; MobileLLM's MT-Bench is judged and 2 turns. The headroom rule stops families that sit at the floor from passing non-inferiority trivially. It is coding only (40-80 h), so it can proceed under the current no-model rule.

**4. P-004, decide on log-prob margins.** Two hours that set the cost of every later decision. Generative pass rates have a minimum detectable effect of 3-4 points. At 60M-1.3B, bpb raised decision accuracy from 68.3 to 95.3. If margins agree with pass rates across the E3 seeds, later arms need fewer seeds and fewer generation runs.

**5. P-005, state card as a scaffold condition.** This is inference only, 2-6 hours, and it diagnoses what kind of limit #1 is. If corrections pass when the current facts sit near the end of the context and fail otherwise, then #1 is about interference or access, not capacity. That points toward notes (P-010) and recency attention (P-020). The state card is reported as a separate scaffold condition under D4, never as the model.

**6. P-006, re-inject the system prompt.** A free inference test on #6, the second Level A limit. At about 100M, one system line stops mattering after turn 1. Two hours on the persistence family shows whether distance alone explains the decay. The only prior art is at 70B.

**7. P-007, perspective and role-swap trap data.** The only top-10 bet the audit confirmed as untested at this size. Perspective swings from 0.03 to 1.00 across recipes at a fixed ~300M body, and role capture appears in 7-8 of 8 small models, so the recipe drives it more than size does. "Am I Me or You?" shows role capture persists at 2.7B. The fixes there were reranking, unlikelihood and attention changes, never trap data. The test is 6-12 hours on the Mac.

**8. P-008, binding-dense dialogues.** Attacks #2 through P-001's harness at low cost. Small models fall back on whichever value was mentioned first. SmolLM2's SFT/DPO moved second-mention accuracy from 0.31 to 0.65, so the recipe can move it, and a 37.8M transformer learns 4-hop binding from synthetic programs. The win line is concrete: second-mention >=0.9, and two-hop at d10 from 0.09 to >=0.8.

**9. P-009, interference-balanced correction dialogues.** REPORT lever 1 on #1, the limit that gates the project. Every chat model up to 0.6B prefers the stale value. Balanced data (latest wins, no-update twins, two-slot, stale traps, varied original position) removes both the first-wins and the last-wins shortcut. Stage 1 reuses the ft_test harness on SmolLM2-135M-I, and only a pass justifies the 5M/30M pretraining arms.

**10. P-010, one-line structured note.** REPORT lever 2, which attacks #1, #2 and #6 at once. It turns binding, two-hop references and updates into a short copy from one recent place, so primacy stops mattering. Small-model dialogue state tracking supports it at 82-247M (SimpleTOD, UBAR, Diable, Self-Notes), but nobody has tested it in open-domain chat at or below 150M. The note counts as the model under D4, because the model writes and reads it itself.
