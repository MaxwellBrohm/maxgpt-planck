## E003: where does the E002 recipe stop working?

Files: [notes](../experiments/E003_correction_floor/notes.txt), [queue log](../experiments/E003_correction_floor/logs/queue.txt), [results](../experiments/E003_correction_floor/results.json), [tables](../experiments/E003_correction_floor/logs/tables.txt), [final reading](../experiments/E003_correction_floor/logs/final_e003.txt)

**Question.** At what body size does E002's recipe stop teaching the skill? Models of 30M total parameters and under come first, because they are the main target of the project.

**What was run.** Eight public base models on two ladders: Pythia 14M, 31M, 70M and 160M (same data, tokenizer and context length, so the clean ladder) and TinyStories 1M, 3M, 8M and 33M (narrow-domain text, pretrained on 512-token sequences, a secondary ladder). Bodies run from 0.4M to 85M. Items, pass rule and recipe as in E002 (400 steps, effective batch 16), plus a per-model learning-rate search on a separate dev draw with seed 0 (5e-5, 3e-4, 1e-3, and 3e-3 for the three narrowest models, with one extension if the top rate won and still failed), then 3 fresh seeds at the picked rate. The queue finished on Sep 27 at 21:01 after 34 restarts: 33 memory-guard stops and one stop by the old guard. Each stopped job re-ran from scratch with the same command, and a check confirmed that every record read comes from a job's one completed run.

**Pre-registered rule.** E002's rule per seed (at least 0.8 on the correction, two-slot and no-update items at d = 10), 2 of 3 seeds to pass. The floor is the smallest passing body. A failure at every learning rate reads as "not learnable in 400 steps with this recipe at this size", explicitly not as proof of a capacity limit.

**Result.** All eight models fail, 0 of 3 seeds each. No searched learning rate reached 0.8 on the dev draw for any model (best 0.69, Pythia-160M; one seed-0 dev run per rate). For five of the eight models the winning rate was the lowest one tried (5e-5, E002's rate), and nothing lower was tried, so this is weaker than "no learning rate works". So there is no floor inside either ladder: no Pythia model up to an 85M body passes with this recipe, and no TinyStories model up to 28M. Since no size passes, it is not known that any Pythia size would, and the cells do not rise steadily with size, so there is no trend to extrapolate. The only pass at any size is still E002's SmolLM2-135M-Instruct (106M body), which is a different family, and the E002 audit showed that its pass can come from a wording shortcut. So 85M to 106M is not a floor estimate.
- TinyStories (1M to 33M): mostly near chance (mean on the correction items 0.35-0.52); TinyStories-33M is partly above it (correction items 0.58, 0.53 and 0.45 by seed, where chance is 0.39). The models also fail on the items that fit their 512-token pretraining context, so context length does not explain it.
- Pythia-14M: chance. It and TinyStories-3M were scored at 1e-2, a near-divergent rate (training loss over the first 10 steps 12.0 and 7.2, against 2.1 and 4.2 at 5e-5). At 5e-5 Pythia-14M's dev run reached 0.91 on the correction items but 0.19 on no-update, below chance, so the pre-registered min rule picked 1e-2. The FAIL holds either way. Pythia 31M, 70M and 160M learn the main correction items (0.95-0.99) but fail two-slot (0.57-0.74) and no-update (0.38-0.53). Every scored seed has a failing cell whose 95% interval lies below 0.8, so none is a near miss.
- Added after the fact, so a description and not a verdict: the Pythia failures sit in one item family. On weekday items, 8 of 9 seeds score 0.94-1.00 on all three cells. On colour items they take the latest colour even when it belongs to another object (no-update 0.00-0.16). One Pythia-160M seed takes the latest value on every no-update item.
- Closed-book knowledge, read only where the untouched model is above 0.6: Pythia-160M lost 11-15 points (every interval excludes zero), Pythia-70M lost 2-5 and Pythia-31M lost 0-4. Pythia-14M (untouched 0.585, so uninformative by the rule) lost 7-10 points with every interval excluding zero; it was trained at the near-divergent 1e-2, so that drop is read as a cost of the rate rather than of the task.

**Deviations.** The three were all infrastructure: E002's newer memory guard, which kills a job on swap growth only when free memory is also low on 2 samples in a row; a wrapper that restarts the queue after a memory stop; and that wrapper's cap raised from 20 to 400. None of the three could change a verdict. They decide only when a job is restarted, never what it computes. The one choice a restart could have changed is TinyStories-1M's learning rate, where all four rates sit at chance.

**Audit.** One independent audit (AUDIT.md, 2026-10-02) re-derived the reading from the raw records with its own code: all eight models FAIL, 0 of 3 seeds each, and no searched learning rate reaches 0.8 on the dev draw. Its corrections are applied here and in the notes: two rounded knowledge figures, the scope of "every learning rate", the floor wording, the scope of two after-the-fact statements, and the TinyStories "near chance" wording. E002's audit also applies: these items can be passed by a wording shortcut, so even a pass here would not have shown general updating.

**What it changed.** Even the easy version of the updating test does not train into any of the eight tested models (bodies 0.4M to 85M) in 400 steps with this recipe at the searched learning rates. This is consistent with E004's near-chance tiny ladder on the harder test. The 1-30M targets get no support from short fine-tuning of public base models, and E003 gives no size at which to aim the from-scratch budget. It does not show a capacity limit: more steps, a curriculum, a learning rate below 5e-5 or a state mechanism are untested.
