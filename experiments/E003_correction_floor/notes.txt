Reading (fixed now):
  - The floor = the smallest body at which a model passes. Within the Pythia ladder this is a clean size effect
    for this recipe (400 steps, this data). The TinyStories ladder is read with its caveats above.
  - Secondary label, not the verdict: "per-object tracking" = pass AND crossed pair at d10 >= 0.8 on most seeds.
    A pass without it means the model may use "a correction marker wins, else the first value" (E002 reading).
  - A model that fails at every LR: not learnable in 400 steps with this recipe at that size. That is NOT proof of
    a capacity limit (more steps, curriculum or a state mechanism could still work).
  - Non-monotone results across sizes are reported as they come; no model or seed is re-run to smooth them.
  - Knowledge: when a model's untouched kbig accuracy is near chance (< 0.6), its knowledge change is reported but
    read as uninformative.
  - A pass here would make a 1-30M body a candidate for Planck's correction behavior; a floor between two sizes
    says where to spend the from-scratch budget.
