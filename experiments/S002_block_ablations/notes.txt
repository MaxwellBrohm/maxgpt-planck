WHY
  The baseline block (PLAN 5) carries four switches from Max's 124M A/B: per-head gated attention, normalized value
  residual, 1/sqrt(depth) norm scaling and QK-norm. That A/B measured 4.5% (0.134 nats) of paired training loss for
  the block and NorMuon together, at about 9 tokens per parameter, with an under-tuned AdamW arm (LEDGER row 19;
  REPORT s4.1). REPORT s4.1 lists the other evidence: value residual +16% parameter-equivalent at 82M-468M
  (ResFormer); gate and value residual inside noise at 1.2B. No switch has been tested alone, at 5M, or at 50
  tokens per parameter. Row 19's own check is at 30M; this screen is the cheap 5M pre-screen for three of the four
  switches, each alone against the full block.

QUESTION (decided per arm, C7)
  Does turning off ONE of value residual, QK-norm or norm scaling change held-out chat bits per byte at 5M, 250M?

SEEDS AND PAIRING
  Seeds 101..100+k, each arm paired with the shared BASE run at the same seed (C4). Class: SIA (same-init
  architecture), which is a LOOSENING of E3 rule 1: E3 puts "architecture or shape" in the new-init row, and its
  KNOWN LIMITS say bets that move the model more may be noisier than the LR-only contrast that measured SD_d. Why it
  is looser here: model.py reset_parameters draws only nn.Linear and nn.Embedding weights; RMSNorm gains are
  torch.ones and the value-residual scalars are fixed tensors (blocks.py), so none of the three switches changes a
  single draw: at a seed, every shared parameter starts bitwise equal to BASE's (tested, below), and the init-noise
  term of E3's new-init row is absent. A larger training-path noise may remain, so SD_d is not used bare:
  SD_ref = u x SD_d, u = 1.353 at df 7 (E3's 80% upper bound; C4), df_ref = SD_d's df. The noise check is C4's
  POOLED SIA check (a per-arm SD has df 1 at k = 2): if it fails, every SIA arm is re-read with sqrt(2) *
  sigma_init (sqrt(2) * sigma_seed without E3 Part 3) and labelled "underpowered" where its k was chosen for the
  smaller SD. k = max(2, k_needed(1%)) for u x SD_d, at most 5; one k for all three arms (they share seeds and BASE
  runs).

METRIC AND DECISION RULE
  C5 and C7, per arm against BASE: F(CHAT) decides, F(PROSE) guards, thresholds in E3 MDE units. The arm is the
  ablation, so:
    NOMINATE      removing the switch lowers chat bpb: a candidate to drop it, confirmed at 20M before any change.
    REJECT        the switch earns its place on loss at 5M. What changes: the switch stays in the baseline, and no
                  30M arm that removes this switch alone is queued under row 19 (row 19's 30M check of the block
                  and optimizer still runs as the ledger states it).
    TIE           loss-neutral at 5M/250M: the baseline keeps it (nothing is adopted on a tie); row 19's 30M check
                  decides whether it stays.
    GUARD-FAIL, INCONCLUSIVE  as C7.
  Divergence (C7 DIVERGENCE, E2 RULES): an arm run that goes non-finite at a screen seed scores d_s = +inf on every
  metric for that seed and is never dropped. The arm is labelled UNSTABLE and can never NOMINATE, TIE or GUARD-FAIL;
  it reads REJECT if every other d_s > 0, else INCONCLUSIVE. no_qknorm is the arm most likely to diverge. Three
  contrasts share one BASE, so their d's are correlated; each is read alone.

DIAGNOSTICS (C6; reported)
  Final IND, HD, SINK per arm; IND onset only at matched LR (C6: read if the arm's pick is g = 1, else "LR-confounded,
  not read"). The training-loss trace near the end of warmup (steps 60-160) is printed per arm and seed: QK-norm and
  norm scaling act on early stability, and a stability effect that washes out by step 7,630 would otherwise go
  unseen. The clip rate per arm and seed (C5: share of logged steps whose gnorm exceeds the 1.0 clip; harness/
  trainer.py clip_grad_norm_ at grad_clip 1.0): a stabiliser removed may show up as more clipped steps before it
  shows up in bpb.

KNOWN RISKS
  - Row 19's evidence is a bundle (block plus optimizer); a TIE here is about one switch at 5M, never about the
    bundle.
  - Per-switch effects at 5M may be under 1% and read TIE while mattering at 30M-150M (the ResFormer gain was
    measured at 82M-468M). PLAN 3's SCALE-DEPENDENT label covers a later flip.
  - no_qknorm may only work at a lower LR; the g-check covers a factor 2 each side plus up to two extensions.
  - SIA is argued from the code and the init test, not from a measured noise; C4's pooled check falls back to the
    new-init SD when the screen's own spread says the class is wrong.
  - Leave-one-out cannot see redundancy: if QK-norm and norm scaling each cover for the other (both act on early
    stability), each arm alone can read TIE while removing both would hurt. A fourth arm with all four switches off
    (testutil "plain_block") would see it; it is not run in this batch (about 1.9 h at k = 3 against the 25 h cap,
    SCREENS ORDER), so a TIE on either stabiliser says nothing about removing both.
  - Starter mix and tok_v0_8k only (C1 scope).
