WHY
  Canon layers are residual, trainable causal 1-D convolutions with kernel 4, inserted before attention (A), inside it
  (B), before the MLP (C) and inside the MLP (D) (arch.md 1.6, Physics of LMs 4.1). In-scale synthetic results:
  reasoning depth +200-400% at 8L512D/12L768D; real pretraining at 1.3B "shows high noise and limited resolution";
  cost under 0.45% extra parameters for GPT2-small, and Canon-AC +5.8% forward time at 1.3B (naive code). Their
  claimed mechanism is faster formation of the local token-to-token circuits that induction and associative recall
  are built from (arch.md), which REPORT s6 ties to binding (limit 2: multi-token keys need a previous-token path).
  arch.md's first recommended A/B is Canon-AC or Canon-ACD at kernel 4. PLAN 3d tests P-148 at 30M after a 2-layer
  toy. This 5M screen is the cheap first look at the loss cost or gain and at induction timing, before 30M.
  Must-run status is conditional: PLAN's list names "P-022 or P-148 (whichever the toy favors)". If the PLAN 3d toy
  favours P-022, this screen feeds no must-run bet; it then ranks as an ordinary bet in SCREENS' order and cut rule
  (the cut rule protects S004 only while P-148 is the must-run pick).

THE CHANGE (one flag)
  model.canon: "AC" (new field; default "" = off) with model.canon_kernel: 4 (new; used only when canon is on).
  At every block: h = attn_norm(x) * norm_scale, then h = h + conv_A(h) before attention; h = mlp_norm(x) *
  norm_scale, then h = h + conv_C(h) before the MLP. conv_* is depthwise (one kernel per channel), causal, kernel 4,
  no bias, and never reaches across a document start in packed rows (taps at in-document position < j are zero).
  Init: zero, created without drawing from the torch RNG (a registered choice: the arm then starts as the exact
  BASE function, and every BASE parameter keeps its draw; the Canon paper's init is not recorded in the repo).
  Parameters: 2 sites x 8 layers x 192 channels x 4 taps = 12,288; total 5,022,421 vs 5,010,133 (+0.25%, arithmetic
  from budget.py's BASE count), inside the 2% rule, so the shape is not re-solved. The config generator's C2 check
  allows exactly these two keys for this arm, model.canon and model.canon_kernel (a test: a third key is refused).

WHAT STAYS IDENTICAL
  Everything in C1, including the engine (the convolutions sit outside attention, so doc_attn varlen still
  applies). LRs move only by the arm's g pick (C3). The conv weights go to the "scalar" group (AdamW at scalar_lr, no
  weight decay), routed by name: harness/optim.py split_params would otherwise put any 2-D tensor in the NorMuon
  "matrix" group, and orthogonalizing a 192 x 4 kernel bank is not what that group's LR was tuned for. The conv LR
  is therefore the embedding LR: E2 sets optim.embed_lr = optim.scalar_lr = r x eta (E2 notes, "Both AdamW groups
  move together"), so the kernels train at g x r_5 x eta_5, a value tuned for embeddings and norm gains, not for a
  new module. No separate multiplier is registered (it would be another selection axis); the weight-size
  diagnostic below shows whether the kernels moved.

KNOWN RISKS
  - A convolution that reaches across a document start leaks between packed documents; one that reads t+1 leaks the
    future. Both are what harness/selftest.py's leak tests look for, and both get mutants below.
  - Zero init may leave the convolutions little used in 7,630 steps; the weight-size diagnostic shows it.
  - Throughput cost at 5M is unmeasured (arch.md: +5.8% forward at 1.3B for Canon-AC, naive); the smoke run times
    it, and the report gives F(CHAT) per GPU-hour beside F(CHAT).
  - Real-data evidence exists only at 1.3B and above, and is noisy (arch.md 1.6).
  - Starter mix and tok_v0_8k only (C1 scope).
