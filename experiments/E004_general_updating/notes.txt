WHY
E002 taught SmolLM2-135M-Instruct to pass its rule on 5/5 seeds, but its audit (E002_ft_test/AUDIT.md) found every
scored STRUCTURE in training (item types, value lists, d 0-10, candidate counts, plain format). Two cheap rules pass
the rule and the crossed control: "copy the value after the last occurrence of the prefix's last 3 words" and
"turn with most words in common with the question, latest wins". Where the correction is worded differently from
the question (E001 keyorig, uprobe U_neutral with 3 corrections) several seeds fall BELOW chance. Owner, two-hop and
perspective gains came from direct training. The bare "Value." answer format was collateral damage.
E004's aim: a test that only a genuine updating skill passes, i.e. "the answer is the latest statement ABOUT THE
ASKED OBJECT", whatever its wording, position, marker or distance, with every scored structure absent from training.

DEFINITIONS
  statement      a user turn that gives a value of the item's value type (an original, a correction, an incidental).
  object phrase  the words naming an object ("piano lesson"); head noun = its last word ("lesson").
  content words  lowercased words minus a fixed stopword list, minus values, markers and the object-free verbs list;
                 the list is fixed in code before any item is built.
  frame echo     the question or answer prefix shares a run of >= 3 consecutive words with a statement after the
                 object phrase is replaced by <o> and the value by <v>, and the run holds >= 2 words that are not
                 <o>/<v>/determiners (e.g. "<o> is on", "the color of <o>").
  d              filler exchanges after the last value statement (as E002).   k = corrections of the asked object.
  chance         1 / (number of candidates in the item).

(c) PASS RULE (plain "User:/Assistant:" render, every model; SmolLM2's chat-template render reported, not ruled)
  Likelihood (as E002, lik.py): an item is right only if the gold's summed log-prob after the forced eval prefix is
  strictly above every other in-context candidate; ties, NaN or a missing score fail.
  Free generation: greedy, no prefix, at most 48 new tokens, stop at newline or "User:". HARDENED STRICT grader
  (code/grade_e004.py), right only if ALL hold:
    1 the reply is non-empty and generation stopped before the 48-token cap (a capped reply fails: loops)
    2 no word 3-gram occurs twice and no word occurs 3 times in a row
    3 the gold is mentioned (whole word, case-insensitive)
    4 no OTHER value of the item's whole value pool is mentioned (in-context or not: guesses fail)
    5 no negation cue anywhere (not, n't, no longer, never, instead, rather than, no,)
    6 no question mark and no hedge (I think, maybe, probably, might, perhaps, not sure, I don't know, I believe)
    7 not the user's voice: no "my" within 3 words before a word of the asked object's phrase, head noun or alias,
      and the reply does not start with "User:" or "Assistant:"
    Lenient (first value mentioned is the gold) is reported as a diagnostic only. Every clause gets fixtures and a
    mutant that must be killed (mutation_e004.py) before any model is scored.
  Seed passes <=> for EVERY family f in {H1..H7, C_noupd, C_twoslot}: LIK_f >= 0.8 AND GEN_f >= 0.8 (n = 64 each).
  Model passes <=> a strict majority of its seeds pass (3 of 5 for SmolLM2-135M-Instruct, 2 of 3 for tiny models).
  Also reported per seed: every family at LIK and GEN, the H1/H2 reference-form split, ID, the dev and probe
  scores, lock-in step (first probe step from which min over the 9 probe families stays >= 0.8), and a BELOW-CHANCE
  flag for any family where a seed scores under chance (a sign of an active wrong rule, as E002's keyorig).
  Reading (fixed now):
    PASS       the model passes: general updating at this size under this recipe, within the tested axes.
    PARTIAL-G  a majority of seeds pass every LIK cell but not every GEN cell: it ranks the latest value but does
               not reliably say it. Named with the failing GEN families.
    PARTIAL-X  a majority of seeds pass every cell (LIK and GEN) except those of one or two of H3 (k45), H4 (d20),
               H5 (obj3), H6 (newtype), H7 (newfill): updating with a named limit; the limit is the finding.
    FAIL       anything else. A LIK failure on H1, H2 or a control is never partial: that is the E002 shortcut
               signature. (Labels are checked in the order PASS, PARTIAL-G, PARTIAL-X, FAIL; first match wins.)
               Sub-readings: ID >= 0.8 but H1/H2 < 0.8 = learned the training task by a rule that does not
               transfer; ID < 0.8 = did not learn even in distribution (recipe or capacity); U families pass but
               controls fail = a recency rule.
    Untouched models are scored first; an untouched model that passes needs no fine-tune (reported as such).
    TinyStories caveat (E003): positions 512+ had no pretraining gradient and 768+ get no fine-tuning gradient.
    A TinyStories model failing only H4 (d20) is PARTIAL-X with the positional confound named, not a size finding;
    the Pythia ladder is the clean one for the floor.
