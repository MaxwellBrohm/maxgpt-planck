WHY
  P-108 (LEDGER): "Sequence-length curriculum (T=1024 then 2048)"; prior art Dataset Decomposition (1B); limit #4;
  cheapest test "Two 5M arms"; "Default: same final loss and battery in less wall time". The bet is speed at no loss
  cost. At 5M with document-masked varlen attention, attention cost already follows document lengths, so the time saved
  is only what documents longer than 1,024 tokens cost; this screen measures both the loss and that saving.

QUESTIONS
  Q1 (decided, B7) Does the curriculum change held-out chat bpb at the final checkpoint (scored at 2,048, as every arm)?
  Q2 (reported, the bet's own reading; fixed after critique R12) Training speed: the median tok_per_s of each stage
  from log.jsonl (steps 1..3,814 and 3,815..7,630; the trainer logs tok_per_s per log interval), and from them the
  training hours T = stage-1 tokens / median_1 + stage-2 tokens / median_2, arm against its BASE at each seed. The
  saving is reported as a magnitude: per-seed T_BASE - T_arm in hours and in % of T_BASE, their mean and the seeds
  per sign. Lock-held hours (which include scoring) are reported beside it, not used for the reading. Every GPU
  process seen during the run that does not hold gpu.lock (the queue samples nvidia-smi each log interval) is
  recorded against that seed.

THE CHANGE (one flag)
  train.span_curriculum: {short: 1024, until_step: 3815} (new key; default absent = off). For steps 0..3,814 (the first
  half of 7,630) the trainer splits every packed document at the row's column 1,024: tokens at columns >= 1,024 get a
  new document id, so attention and RoPE positions (restarting there) treat the two halves as separate documents, as
  two 1,024-token rows would; from step 3,815 the rows are BASE's. The loader, the rows, the token order, the
  targets and the loss mask are BASE's at every step (the same token stream as BASE: common random numbers hold), and
  the init is BASE's. Choices fixed here: the split at one fixed column (Dataset Decomposition buckets documents by
  length, which would change the stream and the packing); the switch at half the steps (the ledger names no point).
  Parameters: BASE's (5,010,133). Engine varlen: the split only relabels doc ids, which varlen reads.

WHAT STAYS IDENTICAL  B1 and the token stream. LRs by the g pick (B3).
BUDGET  B1: the same 250.0M slots; only the attention span of the first half changes.

  TIE           P-108's default win if Q2's training hours T are lower on every seed: "same final loss in less wall

KNOWN RISKS
  - At 5M the saving is small if few documents exceed 1,024 tokens; Q2 reports it as measured.
  - The token after each split sees only its own half as context in the first half of training (as any shorter-context
    stage); the targets are still scored.
  - Wall time on a shared GPU varies with the other jobs on the card; Q2 reads logged tok/s per stage (lock-held time
    includes scoring and is exposed to jobs that do not take the lock), and records any such job seen.
  - Starter mix and tok_v0_8k only.
