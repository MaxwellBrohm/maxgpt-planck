WHY
  arch.md 1.10 (Gloeckle et al., arXiv 2404.19737): on children's stories, 2-token prediction "vastly improved"
  induction for models of 30M non-embedding parameters and below, with the advantage gone at 100M and above; on code
  at 300M-13B it was worse than baseline at small sizes; exact MTP hurt at 340M (TOP paper); a forward NTP-to-MTP
  curriculum helps small LMs (arXiv 2505.22757). Induction is the in-context copying mechanism behind multi-turn
  recall (loop.md 4.2). Planck's screening size, 5M (3.44M body), is inside the band where the induction gain was
  seen. LEDGER P-105's win: "earlier induction and better binding at 5M". Induction is measurable now without RC-12
  (C6 IND); binding waits for the lock. research/followup/loop.md idea 5 specifies 2-4 MTP heads and a cheapest test
  of "MTP=0 vs 2 extra heads"; this arm has ONE extra head (t+2), so it is a narrower form than loop.md's, and a
  null here says nothing about 2 or more heads.

THE CHANGE (one training switch; the deployed model is unchanged)
  train.mtp: 1 (new; default 0 = off) with train.mtp_weight: 1.0 (new; used only when mtp > 0). Registered form:
  z_t = the final hidden state after the model's final norm; aux logits_t = lm_head(RMSNorm_mtp(W_mtp z_t)), the
  tied output head shared; W_mtp (192 x 192) initialised to the identity and RMSNorm_mtp's gain to ones, neither
  drawing from the torch RNG (so the aux head starts as a copy of the next-token head). Target of position t: the
  token at t + 2, only where positions t, t+1 and t+2 are in one document and the t+1 target is supervised (not
  -100). Loss per step = (sum of next-token losses + w_t x sum of aux losses) / n_sup, n_sup = the step's
  next-token supervised count (harness/trainer.py normalisation), so at w_t = 1 each aux token weighs as a
  next-token token.
  WEIGHT (registered): w_t = mtp_weight x lr_factor_t, mtp_weight = 1.0, where lr_factor_t is the schedule factor
  at step t (harness/schedule.py factor, which trainer.py applies to every LR and logs as lr_factor): (t + 1)/76
  over warmup, 1 through the stable phase, then linear from 1 at step 6,104 down to 1/1,526 on the last step. So the
  aux weight fades to nearly 0 by the final checkpoint, where F(CHAT) is read. Why: research/lanes/arch.md 1.10
  records the speedrun's "MTP with weights decaying to 0" and an NTP-to-MTP curriculum (2505.22757) that helps
  small LMs; a constant weight would keep pulling the tied embedding toward t+2 up to the scored checkpoint. (An
  earlier draft said the repo records no weighting: wrong.) The forward curriculum (aux weight rising from 0) is a
  later arm only if this one reads BETTER on IND at a bpb cost.
  Parameters: W_mtp 36,864 + gain 192 = 37,056, training only. Deployed model: 5,010,133 (BASE's count, unchanged);
  the aux module is saved in checkpoints for resume, never scored, and never counted in the model's total (it is
  reported as "training-only parameters"). W_mtp goes to the NorMuon matrix group, the gain to "scalar" (AdamW at
  the embedding LR, g x r_5 x eta_5: E2 sets embed_lr = scalar_lr = r x eta). W_mtp must not be an nn.Linear
  among the model's modules: model.py reset_parameters normal-inits every nn.Linear, which would overwrite the
  identity (the init-isolation test below catches it). The config generator's C2 check allows exactly two keys for
  this arm, train.mtp and train.mtp_weight (a test: a third key is refused).

                  fades by 100M non-embedding, so a SCALE-DEPENDENT reading at 20-30M is expected and would keep it
                  out of the recipe (PLAN 3: anything that flips between sizes is left out).
    REJECT        higher chat bpb at 5M. If Q2 reads an earlier onset, the ledger row says "earlier induction at a

    TIE           loss-neutral: Q2 and, after the lock, Tier 0 decide whether it goes to 20M.
    GUARD-FAIL, INCONCLUSIVE  as C7.

KNOWN RISKS
  - The weight, its decay and the normalisation are choices; a different weight could flip the bpb reading. The
    ledger's 3-arm test (NTP, MTP, curriculum) is cut to 2 arms here; the curriculum arm is conditional (above).
  - One extra head where loop.md idea 5 asks for 2-4 (above).
  - Extra compute per step (a second pass through the 8,192-way head at every position): tokens are matched, FLOPs
    are not. The report gives F(CHAT) per GPU-hour beside F(CHAT).
  - IND is an out-of-distribution random-token probe; an earlier IND onset need not mean better conversational
    recall. Tier 0 after the lock is the check.
  - Starter mix and tok_v0_8k only (C1 scope).
