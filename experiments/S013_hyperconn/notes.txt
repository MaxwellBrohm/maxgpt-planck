WHY
  P-112 (LEDGER): "Add hyper-connections or DenseFormer averaging to a non-looped 10-20M model"; prior art
  hyper-connections (>= 1B), DenseFormer (378M); limit #10 (head rank and depth); "Default: loss and battery gain
  beyond seed noise". archfp.md L15: hyper-connections are measured at >= 1B, and at small scale only inside loops
  (the looped law's phi 0.46 to 0.65 with 2 lanes). Max's own sweep found hyper-connection-style residual variants
  strongly negative at 1.2B (AttnRes z -29.47) and diverging at 3B (arch.md); this screen tests the two named forms at
  5M with their papers' identity inits. The ledger names two forms; each is one change, so each is its own arm (as
  S002's three), and each its own contrast against BASE.

THE CHANGES
  Arm s013_dwa: model.dwa: true (new; default false). DenseFormer's form: X_0 is the embedding, X_j block j's output;
  after block i a DWA gives Y_i = sum over j = 0..i of alpha_ij X_j, and block i + 1 reads Y_i; the final norm reads
  Y_8. Dense (dilation 1, period 1: 8 layers is short of the paper's 24-48 blocks, so no sparsification). Init
  alpha_ii = 1, others 0 (the paper's; each DWA starts as the identity, so the arm starts as the exact BASE function).
  Parameters: 8 x 11 / 2 = 44; total 5,010,177.
  Arm s013_hc4: model.hyper_connections: 4 (new; default 0 = off). The paper's dynamic hyper-connections (DHC), its
  default and best-reported rate n 4 ("n = 1 does not improve"): the residual becomes H (4 x d), H_0 = 4 copies of the
  embedding; each of the 16 sublayers (attention and MLP of 8 blocks) is wrapped: h_in = A_m(H)^T H, out = sublayer(h_in)
  (the sublayer keeps its own pre-norm and norm scaling), H <- B(H)^T out + A_r(H)^T H, with H_bar = norm(H) and
  B(H) = s_beta . tanh(H_bar W_beta)^T + B, A_m(H) = s_alpha . tanh(H_bar W_m) + A_m, A_r(H) = s_alpha . tanh(H_bar W_r)
  + A_r. Init (the paper's Pre-Norm-equivalent init): W_beta, W_m, W_r zero, s_alpha = s_beta = 0.01 (its pseudocode),
  B = ones, A_m = e_(k mod 4) for sublayer k, A_r = identity; norm a parameter-free RMS norm. The final norm reads the
  MEAN of the 4 rows (the paper's row sum divided by 4; fixed 2026-10-11 while writing the code, before any value: the
  two are identical after a scale-free norm, but RMSNorm's eps would act at eps/16 on the sum, which moved the tiny
  test model's step-0 logits by about 1%; with the mean, the 4 rows equal at init give BASE's step-0 logits exactly,
  a test). Parameters (the paper's count per hyper-connection, d(n + 2) + n(n + 2) + 2 =
  1,178): 16 x 1,178 = 18,848; total 5,028,981 (+0.38%), inside 2%. The value residual still reads effective layer 0's
  values (unchanged).
  Optimizer, both arms: every new parameter goes to "scalar" (AdamW at scalar_lr, no decay), routed by name (the 2-D
  W_beta, W_m, W_r too: they start at zero and are mixing weights, not block matrices). None draws from the RNG.

KNOWN RISKS
  - Both papers' smallest language models are 378M (DWA) and 1.2B (HC); at 8 layers DWA has little depth to average.
  - Max's 1.2B and 3B results were negative for this family; a divergence scores +inf, never dropped.
  - The mHC constraint (arXiv 2512.24880) is not applied: one form per arm; it is the next arm if hc4 is UNSTABLE.
  - hc4 keeps 4 residual copies: activation memory rises; the smoke's mem_fit decides micro 16, else 8 x 2 with its own
    BASE (B4 (4)).
  - Starter mix and tok_v0_8k only.
