(b) INDIRECT CORRECTION FOLLOWED BY THE OTHER OBJECT (at most 2 objects; 3 objects stay held out for H5)
  Read as the audit's failure case: an ellipsis or pronoun correction of one object, AFTER which the other object's
  statements follow (the other object's own indirect correction included). An indirect correction placed right after
  the other object's statement would refer to that object, so that literal reading is not used.
  Every IND example: objects X then Y of one value type (uniform over the 4). X original, X corrected k = 1-3 times;
  X's LAST correction is ellipsis (2/3) or pronoun (1/3), directly after X's previous statement; then 0-2 fillers;
  Y original; Y corrected 1-2 times, Y's last correction indirect (ellipsis 2/3, pronoun 1/3, directly after Y's
  previous statement) in 2/3 of examples, else full / head (3:2, E004's far weights). Earlier corrections follow
  E004's rules. Ask X 3/4 (gold = X's indirect latest), ask Y 1/4 (gold = Y's latest). E004 pools (pron, ell,
  markers, acks, questions, answers, fillers), pre 0-3, d 0-10, distinct values. No aliases in IND.
