WHAT IS TUNED (harness/optim.py groups. Fixed, not tuned: weight decay 0.1 cautious, momentum 0.95 Nesterov, AdamW
betas (0.9, 0.95), eps 1e-8, clip 1.0)
  eta  optim.lr, the NorMuon matrix group (update RMS 0.2, so AdamW-scale LRs apply).
  r    AdamW ratio: optim.embed_lr = optim.scalar_lr = r * eta. Both AdamW groups move together; a separate scalar
       sweep is not worth its runs (diagnostic D2 looks once).
  g    global multiplier: eta and both AdamW LRs times g, ratio kept. The one knob the 20M grid moves.
  All grids are factor 2.
  Stage A (5 arms, r = 1): eta in {0.75, 1.5, 3, 6, 12} x 1e-3. The harness default 3e-3 is the middle; Max's
          Ultra 124M NorMuon A/B used lr 6e-4 (its configs/ab/normuon_arch.yaml).
  Stage B (4 new arms): at eta_A (stage A's pick), r in {0.5, 2, 4, 8}; r = 1 is stage A's pick, reused.
  Stage C (2 new arms): at (eta_A, r_B), g in {0.5, 2}; g = 1 is stage B's pick, reused.
  Extensions: when a stage's pick sits at an edge of its grid, one more factor-2 point is added beyond that edge, at
          most 2 per stage. Stage C is also extended while its argmin at the 125M branch is at an edge (Q2 needs it
          bracketed), at most 2. Extensions follow this rule, so they are not deviations; each is logged when queued.
  20M grid (3 arms): g in {0.5, 1, 2} around the final 5M pick (eta_5, r_5); g = 1 is the 5M LRs unchanged. It is
          extended by the same rule while the 20M argmin at 500M is at an edge, at most 2.
  Divergence: a run whose loss goes non-finite (trainer.py raises) scores +inf on every metric and is never resumed
          or rerun (change 8).

SCHEDULE: WSD trunks with decay branches (harness/schedule.py modes trunk and branch, the P-186 mechanics)
  Batch 16 x 2048 = 32,768 token slots per step at both sizes (5M micro 16 x accum 1; 20M micro 8 x accum 2; both
  shapes were mem_fit ok in harness/notes.txt's docmask bench). Warmup 76 steps at both sizes (1% of the 250M run at
  5M; the same tokens at 20M, so width and depth are the only differences in Q2). Every config writes
  schedule.warmup_steps: 76; the harness default (1% of total_steps) would give 61 on the 5M trunk, 122 on the 20M
  trunk and 153 on a 20M full run (change 6). Each branch decays linearly to 0.
  5M arm: trunk total_steps 6,105 with branch_points 1,526 / 3,052 / 6,104 (change 5: a trunk of exactly 6,104 steps
      is in its decay phase at step 6,104, and trainer.py asserts a stable phase at every branch point, so that save
      would crash the trunk at its last step; the one extra step is discarded). Decay branches from those stable
      checkpoints with decay_steps 382 / 763 / 1,526 give runs of 1,908 / 3,815 / 7,630 steps = 62.5M / 125.0M /
      250.0M slots (12.5 / 25 / 50 tokens per parameter). Each branch's schedule equals schedule.full for its length
      with warmup 76 and 20% decay (checked with schedule.py 2026-09-25 and 2026-09-26). One arm = 8,776 steps.
  20M arm: trunk total_steps 12,208 with branch_points 6,104 / 12,207; decay_steps 1,526 / 3,052 give 7,630 /
      15,259 steps = 250.0M / 500.0M slots (12.5 / 25 tokens per parameter). One arm = 16,786 steps = 550.0M slots.
  Why these lengths: every 5M screen will run 250M, so the LR is tuned at the screens' own length, and the two short
  branches add the length trend for 15% more compute. At 20M, 500M is the equal tokens-per-parameter point for Q2
  (matching 5M at 125M) and 250M the equal-tokens point, at about 3 h per arm. Slots include row padding; the real
  token count is logged per run (log.jsonl "tokens").
  Checkpoints: train.ckpt_every = round(2% of the run's total steps) (38 / 76 / 153 at 5M; 153 / 305 at 20M) with
  keep_last 2, plus final; stable ones kept; train.log_every 10. Rolling saves fall at multiples of ckpt_every, so
  the two before the final sit at different points of the decay per branch (LR factors 0.19 and 0.09 at 5M 250M,
  0.12 and 0.02 at 5M 62.5M, 0.10 and 0.003 at 20M 500M). This is one reason F, not M, decides (METRIC).

RULES (fixed now)
  Stage pick: the argmin of F(CHAT) at the 250M branch on that stage's axis. Guard: if that point's F(PROSE) is more
    than 1% (relative) above the best F(PROSE) on the same axis, the pick moves one grid step toward the PROSE best
    and the report says so. Exact ties (equal to 4 decimals) go to the lower LR.
  Final 5M pick (Q1) = stage C's pick: eta_5 = g_C * eta_A, r_5 = r_B. Runner-up = the pick's neighbour on the
    stage C axis with the lower 250M F(CHAT); it is E3's arm B. E3 overrides the pick: if E3's paired seeds 2..8
    show the runner-up lower on F(CHAT) with two-sided p < 0.05 (paired t, df 6), the runner-up becomes the 5M LR.
    Seed 1 is left out of that test because seed 1 chose the pick (change 9).
  Vertex: along the axis that moves all LRs together at ratio r_5 (stage C at 5M, the 20M grid), a parabola in
    log2(eta) through the F(CHAT) argmin and its two neighbours; v = log2(eta) at its minimum. If the argmin is still
    at a grid edge after the allowed extensions, v is only a bound (at or beyond that edge).
  Transfer (Q2), primary at equal tokens per parameter (25): s = v20(500M) - v5(125M).
    HOLDS  |s| < 1, within one factor-2 grid step. 20M and larger runs use the 20M argmin (a measured grid point);
           the report says whether it is g = 1.
    MOVES  |s| >= 1, 2x or more. PLAN's branch: E2b, a new pre-registration (a harness patch adding muP width
           multipliers and CompleteP depth scaling with the 5M shape as base, so 5M results stay valid), before any
           cross-size claim. Until E2b reads, 20M runs use the 20M argmin.
    UNRESOLVED  a vertex is only a bound and the bound does not by itself give |s| >= 1. Reported as such; 20M runs
           use the 20M argmin, and no cross-size claim is made (change 7).
    Secondary, reported: s at equal tokens, v20(250M) - v5(250M). If primary and secondary disagree on HOLDS vs
    MOVES the report says so; the primary reads.
  Length trend (Q3, no rule): v at 62.5M, 125M, 250M (5M) and 250M, 500M (20M), and v's change per doubling.
  After E3: each stage decision is labelled "resolved at one seed" when its best-vs-second difference exceeds E3's
    paired MDE at k = 1 (for F(CHAT)), else "not resolved". Picks stand either way, except by the E3 override above.
