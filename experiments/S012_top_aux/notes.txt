QUESTION (decided, B7)
  Does adding TOP's listwise ranking loss through a training-only head change held-out chat bpb (read from the
  next-token head alone) at 5M, 250M slots?

THE CHANGE (one flag; its parameters are fixed here)
  train.top: true (new key; default false). As the paper (Algorithm 1, its loss):
  - Target at position t, window W: for each vocabulary token v whose next occurrence after t is at distance d, 0 < d
    <= W, the score is W - d; every other token scores -inf. W = 2,048, the sequence length (the paper sets W to its
    sequence length, 4,096). Planck's additions, needed by packing and the chat loss mask: only occurrences in t's own
    document count (doc ids; doc=None: the row), and position t has a TOP target only where its next-token target is
    supervised (not -100), the rule S006 used. OCCURRENCES (critique R8): written on the target array, an occurrence
    of v is a position t' >= t in t's document with tgt[t'] = v and tgt[t'] supervised (not -100), at distance d =
    t' - t + 1 (d 1 is the next-token target itself). So a token whose next appearance is inside a later user or
    system turn (no loss under chat loss assistant) does not count there; its first supervised appearance after it
    does, if within W. Reason: the chat loss never trains the model to predict user or system text, and TOP would
    otherwise train it to rank that text.
  - Loss: ListNet, L_TOP = - sum over targeted t of softmax(y_t) . log_softmax(U_TOP(z_t)), z_t the final hidden state
    after the final norm (the hidden state the next-token head reads). Total per step = (sum of next-token losses + sum
    of TOP losses) / n_sup, n_sup the step's next-token supervised count: the paper's unweighted sum, weight 1 at every
    step (no schedule; S006's decaying weight is not used, since TOP's main results use none).
  - Head: U_TOP, one bare (8,192 x 192) matrix, not a module of the model (as S006's head): the model's state_dict,
    model_cfg and deployed count stay BASE's (5,010,133), 1,572,864 training-only parameters (reported, as S006's).
    Init: a copy of the tied embedding at step 0 (draws nothing: the paper does not state its init, and a copy keeps
    every BASE draw and starts TOP's scores at the next-token logits). Optimizer: the "embed" group (AdamW at embed_lr,
    the group of the tied head it copies), routed by name.
  The target never needs the dense T x V tensor: softmax(y_t) is nonzero on at most W distinct tokens; the loss is
  computed from log_softmax(U_TOP z_t) gathered at those tokens (a test against the dense form on small sizes).
  BAND (fixed 2026-10-11 while writing the code, before any value): p_v = exp(-(d_v - 1)) / Z_t, and exp(-(d - 1)) is
  below fp32's smallest subnormal for d > 104, so in fp32 those weights are exactly 0; the code sums distances up to
  min(W, 128), equal to the full W = 2,048 window in fp32 (tests: the full window and a brute-force fp64 reference).

KNOWN RISKS
  - The paper's smallest model is 340M on 52B tokens; at 5M the head is 31% of the deployed parameter count again in
    training-only weights, and the representation it shapes is small.
  - W = 2,048 makes softmax(y_t) nearly all on the next one or two distinct tokens (scores fall by 1 per position), so
    TOP is close to a softened next-token target; the paper's window ablation (4 to 4,096) found every W better than
    NTP at 340M. One W is tested.
  - Starter mix and tok_v0_8k only.
