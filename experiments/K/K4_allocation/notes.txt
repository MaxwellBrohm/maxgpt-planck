PRE-REGISTRATION WRITTEN 2026-09-27 01:20 (before any K code, data, config or run exists. Run for this entry, no

WHY
  If skills live in attention and facts in MLPs, a Planck model should shift parameters toward attention. The fact
  side is mostly known: Physics 3.3 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05405, Results 5-7) finds a 1/4-size MLP has
  "negligible impact" and removing all MLPs leaves capacity unchanged at 1000 exposures but costs more than 1.5x at
  100 exposures, the regime K runs in (capacity.md). Attention stores facts too (arch.md F3: context-constrained
  in-weights learning in 2-layer attention-only models, https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05631). Loss is U-shaped in the
  MLP/attention ratio r with an interior optimum near 1.03-1.06, fitted on 80M-297M and applied at 1B/3B (Bian et
  al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18245). TinyStories reports, anecdotally, that facts lean on width and context
  tracking on depth (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07759). No published work scores the ratio on in-context skill and
  fact accuracy at matched total parameters (arch.md U4; localize.md 3.2 (e)).

KNOWN RISKS
  - Facts are also stored in attention (arch.md F3), so shrinking the MLP cannot starve fact storage by itself;
    a FACT-EQUAL at H7 is possible and would say where facts go at 5M.
  - Init scale moves with the shape [K4-b]: model.py draws every linear at one init_std and W_o and W_down at
    init_std / sqrt(2 x depth), so the attention sublayer's output variance at init grows with its fan-in h x 64
    (7/3 of BASE at H7) and the MLP's shrinks with its width (144/488 at H7). Part of the arm, like the width.
  - More heads also means a wider attention (h x 64 > d): the arm changes attention width and head count together;
    a head_dim change instead would move RoPE and QK-norm geometry, a worse confound.
  - At about 100 exposures the MLP-sensitive regime inflates any fact cost relative to the 1000-exposure ceiling.
  - 125M g-checks applied to 250M runs (LENGTH-RISK).
  - df 6 from three arms' residuals mixes noise across shapes; a shape much noisier than the others inflates every
    threshold (each arm's own residual SD is reported).
  - bpb is not behaviour (SCREENS, citing research/lanes/arch.md 1.4); Bian's optimum is on loss, at 1B/3B.
