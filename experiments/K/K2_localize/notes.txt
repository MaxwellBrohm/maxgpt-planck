K2: localization. Where do facts and in-context skill sit in a 5M model whose facts are known exactly?
RTX 5070 (PC) only, one GPU job at a time; inference and mask fitting on K1's checkpoints, no model training. The Mac
runs no model. World, metrics, rule KR and hours: ../SPEC.txt (SPEC n); models: ../K1_capacity/notes.txt.

PRE-REGISTRATION WRITTEN 2026-09-27 01:20 (before any K code, data, config or run exists; no model run for this
entry. Nothing of K is queued or committed.)

WHY
  Max asked whether we can find which parameters hold facts and which hold skills. Published localization runs on
  pretrained web models of 117M and up (arch.md U2; localize.md 3.2 (a) lists fact recall and in-context skill
  localized on one from-scratch LM with known facts as untested at 5M). K1's FH models give ground truth: every
  fact is known, and skill items are generated. What the literature says to expect and to guard against:
  - Pruning-style masks (HardConcrete, Slimming) localize memorized data best; activations and single-unit zero-out
    do poorly, and single-unit effects do not predict joint dropout (Chang et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09060).
  - Tracing strength does not predict where an edit works (Hase et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04213).
  - Patching results depend on the corruption; symmetric token replacement and logit difference are recommended
    (Zhang and Nanda, https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042).
  - Self-repair hides unit importance and zero ablation is off-distribution (McGrath et al., the Hydra effect,
    https://arxiv.org/html/2307.15771), so mean ablation is reported beside zero.
  - Facts are not only in MLPs (Physics 3.3 Results 5-7, capacity.md); later-layer "memory heads" and "context
    heads" arbitrate at 1B+ (https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18154, Findings of ACL 2024), untested at 5M.
  - At 26M, data-attribution signs flip across seeds (capacity.md, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24460): 3 seeds, unit-
    set agreement reported, methods judged by what the ablation does.

QUESTIONS
  Q1 Is there a set of MLP units whose removal takes out at least half of fact recall while in-context skill drops no
     more than 3 points (SEPARABLE), or does every such set take skill with it (ENTANGLED)?
  Q2 (reported) Which heads and blocks carry facts, skill and the memory-vs-context choice? Do facts displace skill
     units (FH vs F0 skill maps)? How stable are the unit sets across data splits and seeds?

MODELS
  K1 FH final checkpoints, seeds 201-203 (primary). K1 F0 final checkpoints, seeds 201-203 (the skill map with no
  facts; fact probes on F0 are the floor). Weights sha256 in every record.

UNITS
  N1 MLP neurons: 8 x 488 = 3,904 SwiGLU hidden units (unit j of layer l: row j of W_gate and W_up, column j of
     W_down).
  N2 attention heads: 8 x 3 = 24 (the head's output zeroed before W_o), all 24 singly and all 276 pairs.
  N3 sublayers: the 8 attention and 8 MLP sublayers (skipped: the residual stream passes unchanged).

PROBE SPLITS (draw seed 7601; fresh items, disjoint from every SPEC 8 file)
  SPLIT A and SPLIT B, each: 512 FB_high entities (half FB_low) x 6 attributes, BIO prompts in 2 orders; 512 skill
  items in K-EVAL-ID proportions; 128 CONFLICT items. Masks and unit rankings are fitted on A only; every reading
  is measured on B only, so selection and measurement never share an item or an entity.
  Metrics on a split [K2-a]: F = F_LIK minus F0's F_LIK, where F_LIK = argmax over C_F (the FB value plus seeded V
  distractors, |C_F| = round(mean |C|) of the split's skill questions) equals the FB value, so facts and skill are
  read on the same candidate scale; S = LIK accuracy minus chance (SPEC 9); CE_C beside S. Greedy BIO_acc over the
  vocabulary is reported beside F and decides nothing. Normalized CE, for masks: g = (CE - CE_unablated) /
  (CE_floor - CE_unablated), facts on C_F, skill on C (CE_floor: F0 for facts, chance for skill).

METHODS (per seed)
  M1 Masks (primary; Chang et al.'s best family). A HardConcrete gate per N1 unit, trained with the weights frozen,
     Adam on SPLIT A with an L0 penalty lam x relu(E[units dropped] - b) / b that holds the mask to a target budget
     b (lam per budget unit [K2-f]: lam prices an overshoot as a share of the budget, so an overshoot of 10% of b
     costs the same at every b and one unit over costs lam / b), then hardened to the top b by
     gate score (lowest log alpha first). Defaults [K2-f]: 2,000 steps, lam 6, gate init log alpha 0.0 (P(closed)
     0.17 at step 0), lr 0.05, Adam eps 1e-6 (K2-e: 1,000 steps, lam 1 per unit; build: 200-500 steps, init 1.0).
     [K2-h] 2 restarts (gate noise seeds s and s + 7919), the kept one the hardened set with the better objective on
     SPLIT A with its units ablated (a run can leave a saturated unit stuck open).
     Four objectives:
       KF  kill facts: maximize g_fact                        SF  selective facts: g_fact minus g_skill
       KS  kill skill: maximize g_skill                       SS  selective skill: g_skill minus g_fact
     Budgets b in {2, 5, 10, 20}% of N1 (78, 195, 390, 781 units). Fixed hyperparameters, set on seed 201's SPLIT
     A before any B reading and then frozen for every seed (logged in FIXED); lam from the grid {4, 6, 10} [K2-f].
     Fixture check of the pick [K2-f]: the planted-set sweep (code/tests/mask_sweep.py: 10 cases, both floor
     conventions, 20 seeds never used to choose, 32-51) is rerun at the fixed steps, init, lam and restarts before
     any B reading, and its table logged beside them. The pick is frozen only if that sweep finds every planted set;
     otherwise the grid value nearest the pick that does is used, logged as a deviation. [K2-h] With 2 restarts lam
     6 found 400 of 400 on seeds 160-179; single runs missed 6 of 2,080 (seeds 12-31, 60-139 and 160-179), 3 of
     them MX-KF on the default floors (3 of 140 runs); lam 4 and 10: K2-h's grid. At those rates, with the 2 runs
     independent, the 400-fit check misses by chance with probability about 1% (about 0.6 with one run per fit).
  M2 Random sets: 5 draws per budget, matched per layer to SF's layer counts (seeded 7611 + draw).
  M3 Single units: zero ablation of every N1 unit (and mean ablation, the unit's mean activation over K-DEV) on
     SPLIT A, giving (dF, dS) per unit; every N2 head and head pair; every N3 sublayer. If the seed-201 run projects
     the single-unit map over 0.4 GPU-h per seed, it is replaced by gradient x activation attribution with exact
     ablation of the top 200 units only (a registered fallback, not a deviation).
  M4 Causal tracing (descriptive; it chooses no units): on BIO prompts, corrupt the three name tokens by swapping in
     another FB entity's name (symmetric replacement), restore one (layer, position) residual at a time, measure the
     logit difference of the true value; on U items, swap the latest statement's value and trace the answer.
  M5 Positive control: the 2 heads with the highest induction score (SCREENS C6's IND file, copy-2 minus copy-1
     NLL) ablated together must lower S by 10 points or more on every seed; if not, the head-level readings carry
     "copy circuit not found by the head probe".

READINGS (per seed first, then the model reading = the label held by 2 of 3 seeds)
  b* = the smallest budget whose SF set lowers F on SPLIT B to at most half of the unablated F.
  SEPARABLE    b* exists and, at b*, S on SPLIT B drops by at most 3 points (SPEC 10's delta), and the SF set's F
               drop exceeds the largest F drop of the 5 random sets at b* [K2-b].
  DIFFUSE      b* exists with S within 3 points, but a random set of that size drops F as much: facts are fragile
               to any removal of that size, not held by particular units [K2-b].
  ENTANGLED    no budget up to 20% reaches half of F, or at b* S drops by 10 points or more.
  PARTIAL      anything else.
  Scope [K2-c]: every label is about the MLP-neuron basis. Under superposition a fact can live in directions spread
  over many neurons that also carry skill; ENTANGLED says neuron sets do not separate facts from skill at 5M, not
  that no parameter-level or direction-level separation exists, and SEPARABLE says a neuron set exists, not that
  facts are stored only there. Reports and handoffs use these words.
  Beside the label, at b*: KF's skill cost (the unconstrained overlap), the random sets' F and S drops (the floor a
  targeted set must beat), Jaccard(SF set, SS set) against the random expectation b / (2 - b), and the per-layer
  counts of SF and SS units.
  Heads: a head is FACT-ONLY if its ablation lowers F by 5 points or more and S by under 1, SKILL-ONLY the mirror,
  SHARED if both drop by 5 or more; CONFLICT arbitration: the change in context-follow rate per head ablation on
  SPLIT B's CONFLICT items (the memory-head vs context-head reading of 2402.18154, exploratory at 5M).
  Stability: split-half Jaccard of the SF top-5% set fitted on A vs fitted on B (seed 201 only, one extra fit);
  across seeds, the correlation of the per-layer fact-effect profiles (units differ between seeds, layers do not).
  FH vs F0: the per-layer profile of SS units in FH against F0 (do facts push skill units into other layers?).
  Everything per slot frame as well (BIO order, matched vs crossed skill frames): head roles are template-sensitive
  (arch.md F10, the Dotsinski et al. replication).
  What each means: SEPARABLE says fact storage and in-context skill use different MLP units at 5M, so K3's surgery
  has a target; ENTANGLED says they share units, so K3's P-F arm is expected to cost skill and K3-R (routing at
  training time) or K5 (not storing at all) are the better routes; DIFFUSE says K3's P-F and P-R should behave
  alike; PARTIAL is reported with the numbers.

HANDOFF TO K3 (per seed, written to results.json before K3 starts)
  P-F set = SF at b* (or, if ENTANGLED with no b*, the top 390 units, 10% of N1, by SF gate score); P-S set = SS at
  the same count; P-R = random non-P-F units matched to P-F's count in every (layer, skill-effect quintile) cell
  [K2-d], the quintiles of M3's single-unit dS on SPLIT A pooled over layers (M3's fallback attribution if M3 fell
  back); an empty cell borrows from the nearest quintile of the same layer, logged. All three have the count n_p.

KNOWN RISKS
  - Every unit at 5M is polysemantic; "fact units" also carry format and language (localize.md 3.6).
  - Masks optimize on SPLIT A and can find adversarial, non-local sets; SPLIT B and the random sets are the guard.
  - Zero ablation is off-distribution; mean ablation beside it; a small drop does not show a unit is unused (Hydra).
  - Facts may sit partly in heads and in the embedding rows of FB name tokens (Physics 3.3; the embedding is 31% of
    the model). N1 masks can then never reach half of F: that reads ENTANGLED or PARTIAL, and the head and sublayer
    maps say where the rest is. Embedding rows are not masked in K2 (reported as a limit).
  - Likelihood and generation disagree (localize.md 2.2); S is LIK, and GEN is reported beside it.
  - The fixed mask hyperparameters are set on seed 201's SPLIT A, so seed 201's A-fit is slightly favoured.
  - A unit whose removal matters only once its gate is nearly shut (a saturated unit, or one backed up by another)
    gives its HardConcrete gate almost no gradient [K2-e]. Masks can find it late or not at all: the saturated
    skill unit S1 turned late (R-2's defaults missed it in 4 of 120 fits on seeds 6-11; K2-f's reached only
    P(closed) 0.438 at 2,000 steps on one of 40 fresh seeds and 0.032 at 1,500, K2-g), and the saturated fact unit
    F1 can be pushed open before its gate explores closing and stay there (REVIEW 4b M-1: MX-KF on fit's default
    floors, 2 of 100 fits, 4,000 steps or lam 10 did not free it) [K2-h]. The 2 restarts keep the run whose set
    scores better with its units ablated (400 of 400 fixture fits on fresh seeds, K2-h), but a unit neither run
    closes is missed; M3's single-unit zero ablation does not miss it, so a unit M3 ranks in the top b that the SF
    or KF set leaves out is listed, the stated backstop for any mask miss.

CHANGES AFTER CRITIQUE (2026-09-27, adversarial critic; no model loaded, nothing run for these edits)
  K2-a F was greedy exact match over 8,192 tokens while S was argmax over about 6-10 candidates. A small logit
     perturbation breaks greedy recall long before it breaks a small-set argmax, so "half of F gone, S within 3
     points" was easier to reach for facts by the metric alone: a bias toward SEPARABLE. F now uses a candidate
     set of the skill size, masks use range-normalized CE for both sides, and greedy BIO_acc is reported only.
  K2-b SEPARABLE did not have to beat the random sets. If any 5-10% of units halves fact recall (facts fragile and
     spread), a selective mask also does, and the old rule read SEPARABLE. Now it must beat all 5 random sets;
     otherwise DIFFUSE.
  K2-c Neuron-basis scope stated (superposition): labels speak of MLP-neuron sets only.
  K2-d P-R handoff stratified by skill effect (reason: K3 notes, K3-a).
  Suggested, not applied (should): a per-parameter attribution (squared-gradient importance for fact loss vs skill
  loss on SPLIT A, top-k overlap on SPLIT B) answers Max's "which parameters" in the weight basis at a few backward
  passes per seed; SPLIT B skill items 512 -> 1,536 so the 3-point SEPARABLE margin sits above paired noise; head
  labels on a paired test over SPLIT B items rather than the fixed 1-point and 5-point cuts.
