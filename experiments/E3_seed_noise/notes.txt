WHY
  Every Phase 3 decision reads a difference between arms against noise. Without a measured seed SD a 2-seed screen
  cannot say whether a 1% bits-per-byte gain is real (loop.md 5.2: the seeds needed run from 1 to 11 depending on
  sigma, and Planck's sigma at 5M and 20M is unmeasured). E3 gives, per metric and size: the seed SD; the SD of a
  paired arm difference under common random numbers (CRN); the parts due to init, data order and GPU
  nondeterminism; the minimum detectable effect (MDE) at 80% power for 1-5 seeds; and the seeds a screen needs. It
  also re-checks E2's single-seed LR pick.

STATISTICS (per metric and size; fixed now)
  a_s, b_s the arms' values at seed s; d_s = b_s - a_s; N = 8 pairs at 5M, 3 at 20M.
  sigma_seed   pooled SD of A and B across seeds, df N - 1 (7 at 5M, 2 at 20M): under CRN a_s and b_s are
               correlated, so the two variance estimates are not independent and pooling does not add df.
  SD_d         SD of d_s (df N - 1: 7 at 5M, 2 at 20M); rho = corr(a, b).
  sigma_rep    SD over A1, R2, R3 (df 2): GPU nondeterminism at a fixed seed. Share of the paired noise it explains:
               2 * sigma_rep^2 / SD_d^2, reported.
  sigma_init, sigma_data   SD within each Part 3 family (df 4).
  MDE, two-sided alpha 0.05, 80% power, the reference SD borrowed from E3 (so df is E3's):
    paired    MDE_p(k) = (t(0.975, df) + t(0.80, df)) * SD_d / sqrt(k); the factor is 3.261 at df 7, 5.363 at df 2
    unpaired  MDE_u(k) = (t(0.975, df) + t(0.80, df)) * sigma_seed * sqrt(2 / k); the same factors (df 7 and 2)
    for k = 1, 2, 3, 5, in bpb and as % of A's mean. Sensitivity column, not read: the same with each SD at its
    one-sided 80% upper bound (x1.353 at df 7, x2.117 at df 2).
  k_needed(delta) = the smallest k with MDE_p(k) <= delta, for delta = 0.5%, 1% and 2% of A's mean.
  CRN ratio = sqrt(2) * sigma_seed / SD_d. loop.md's rule: a ratio of 2 or more makes CRN mandatory for every
    screen. Below 2, CRN stays the default (PLAN 5) and a screen may use unpaired seeds without a deviation.
  Averaging (loop.md ledger idea 15): SD_d of M vs SD_d of F, per metric and size. Later screens may decide on M only
    where M's SD_d is at least 30% below F's at 5M, and then only with its checkpoints at fixed fractions of the run
    (a harness change; E2 showed that rolling saves land at different decay points per run length).
  Descriptive: over the 28 two-seed subsets at 5M, the share in which both d_s carry the sign of the 8-seed mean d
    (how often a 2-seed screen agrees with 8 seeds on this contrast).
  Seed 1 chose A over B in E2, so d_1 is selected. The noise table uses all 8 seeds and prints the seeds 2..8 values
    (N = 7, df 6, factor 3.353) beside them.
  E2 re-check: a paired t-test on F(CHAT) d_s over seeds 2..8 (df 6). If B is lower with two-sided p < 0.05, the 5M
    LR becomes B (E2's rule), and screens use B; the noise numbers are unaffected. At 20M the same test over seeds
    2..3 is reported (df 1) and moves nothing.

HOW LATER SCREENS USE IT (defaults; a bet's pre-registration may be stricter, and says why if it is looser)
  1 Reference SD, by what the two arms share (each bet's pre-registration names its class):
      same init, same stream   LR, optimizer, schedule, weight decay, clip, loss masks        SD_ref = SD_d
      same stream, new init    architecture or shape, anything that changes the init draws  sqrt(2) * sigma_init
      same init, new stream    mix, shares, filtering, curriculum, templates                sqrt(2) * sigma_data
      both differ                                                                           sqrt(2) * sigma_seed
    Without Part 3, the middle two use sqrt(2) * sigma_seed. SDs travel as a fraction of A's mean; df_ref is the
    df of the SD used. A new mix keeps each source's internal order at the same seed, so its arms share part of the
    stream; sqrt(2) * sigma_data is the conservative end.
    Decay-branch screens (PLAN 5: data and post-training bets as branches off a shared trunk): a seed is an
    independent trunk. Two branches off one trunk share their whole trunk, so their difference is not a seed pair,
    and branch replays off one trunk are never counted as seeds.
  2 Seeds: k = max(2, k_needed(delta_bet)), where delta_bet is the bet's stated minimum effect (default 1% bpb). A
    bet whose k_needed exceeds 5 runs as registered with the label "underpowered", and a null reads "not resolved",
    never "no effect".
  3 Default decision: WIN if the mean paired improvement over k seeds exceeds t(0.975, df_ref) * SD_ref / sqrt(k)
    and every seed pair improves; NULL only if the 95% interval (same SD_ref) excludes delta_bet; else INCONCLUSIVE.
  4 20M confirmations use the larger of the 20M and 5M relative SDs, with the df of the one used. These are
    continuous metrics only; generative pass rates stay reserved for effects of 5 points or more (PLAN Phase 3).
  5 Refresh: when the engine flags, tokenizer version, training mix, corpus build or eval sets change, A and B are
    rerun at 5M for seeds 9, 10 and 11 (6 runs, about 3 h; the seed-9 pair also serves E2's refresh g check). Part 1
    reruns under the new setting before the next screen decision if the mean of the 3 d's falls outside E3's 95%
    prediction interval for a 3-pair mean, or if their SD exceeds SD_d by more than the chi-square 95% bound for
    df 2 (x1.731). One pair, as first written, could not show a change in the noise, which is what a refresh is for.
