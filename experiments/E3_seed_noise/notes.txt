WHY
  Every Phase 3 decision reads a difference between arms against noise. Without a measured seed SD a 2-seed screen
  cannot say whether a 1% bits-per-byte gain is real (loop.md 5.2: the seeds needed run from 1 to 11 depending on
  sigma, and Planck's sigma at 5M and 20M is unmeasured). E3 gives, per metric and size: the seed SD; the SD of a
  paired arm difference under common random numbers (CRN); the parts due to init, data order and GPU
  nondeterminism; the minimum detectable effect (MDE) at 80% power for 1-5 seeds; and the seeds a screen needs. It
  also re-checks E2's single-seed LR pick.

ARMS (defined by E2; no E3 run starts before "E2 5M DONE")
  A        E2's final 5M pick (eta_5, r_5).
  B        E2's runner-up: the pick's neighbour on E2's stage C axis (all LRs x0.5 or x2 together) with the lower 250M
           F(CHAT). A and B differ only in their LRs: a real but small change, like the ones screens test.
  A20, B20 E2's 20M argmin at 500M, and its neighbour on the 20M grid with the lower 500M F(CHAT).
  Model, optimizer, engine flags, data (E2's pinned shares), tokenizer, eval sets and scorer are exactly E2's FIXED
  block; the analyzer checks every recorded hash against it. E3 adds no engine or data setting.
  Schedule, full WSD, batch 32,768 slots per step, schedule.warmup_steps: 76 written in every config:
    5M   7,630 steps (250.0M slots, 50 tokens per parameter), decay from step 6,104 to 0: E2's 250M branch schedule.
    20M  15,259 steps (500.0M slots, 25 tokens per parameter), decay from step 12,207: E2's 500M branch schedule.
  Checkpoints as E2: ckpt_every = round(2% of total steps) (153 at 5M, 305 at 20M), keep_last 2, plus final.

DESIGN
  CRN: the harness seeds init and data order from one seed, so A and B at the same seed start from identical weights
  and read an identical token stream. Seed s is one pair (A_s, B_s).
  What a data seed changes in this loader (read from harness/data.py and sources.py, 2026-09-26): the order of each
  source's shards (so which documents of a multi-shard source a run reaches) and the row shuffle inside each packing
  window. Documents inside a shard keep the pretokenize order (seed 0), and dolly and oasst2 are one shard each, so
  every seed reads them in the same order. sigma_data below is the noise of this loader, the one screens use.
  Part 1, 5M, 18 runs: A_s and B_s for s = 1..8 in the order A1 B1 A2 B2 ... A8 B8 (a stopped queue leaves whole
    pairs), then R2 and R3: A1 again twice (config bytes identical except name and out_dir).
  Part 2, 20M, 6 runs: A20_s and B20_s for s = 1..3, in the order A1 B1 A2 B2 A3 B3.
  Part 3, 5M, 8 runs of arm A, needing one harness change (below):
    INIT  data seed 1 fixed, init seeds 2, 3, 4, 5;
    DATA  init seed 1 fixed, data seeds 2, 3, 4, 5.
    With A1 (init 1, data 1) each family has 5 runs. If the change is not in the committed harness when Part 3 is
    reached, Part 3 is not run and the report says "not run"; nothing substitutes for it.
  Part 4 (no training; each item gets its own addendum here, with its scorer and items, before it runs). Scored on
    the final checkpoints of Parts 1-2, which are kept until Part 4 closes (E4 reads the same series, LEDGER P-046):
    a Tier 0 log-prob margins (P-004), after the RC-12 lock;
    b RC-12 dev pass-or-fail families by generation (PLAN decoding), after the lock: the seed spread of pass rates
      at 5M (8 seeds) and 20M (3 seeds). This is the MDE in points that P-053's gate reads ("E3's minimum detectable
      effect at or below 5", PLAN Phase 2) and E3's only lock-in reading; PLAN's 30M lock-in rule is not decided by
      E3. Pass rates near chance (E004 saw that at 1M-31M) are reported as uninformative, not as zero noise;
    c CORPUS 6.1 (a) assistant turns of dev renders and (c) planted-fact answer spans, once those sets exist;
    d the induction score and history dependence (LEDGER row 39), once they exist.
  Harness change for Part 3 (patch file only; this workflow does not edit harness/): e2e3/data_seed.patch in the
    workflow scratchpad, 4 lines in harness/train.py. data_seed = int(cfg.get("data_seed", seed)) goes to
    build_loader in place of seed and into the checkpoint meta; a config without data_seed behaves exactly as now.
    It was written against train.py sha256 0d7597b4...; the parallel workflow has since changed train.py (sha256
    8d2a9302... on 2026-09-26), and a dry run of the patch on a scratch copy of that file applied cleanly. Before
    Part 3 it needs a test (two runs with equal data_seed and different seed read identical batches and start from
    different weights), watched failing on a mutant.
  A free extra pair: E2's seed-1 run at the pick (trunk + branch) vs A1 (full run), the same seed and schedule by two
    code paths; reported beside the replays.

METRICS (E2's definitions: the built sets v0, data_prep/bpb.py, CHAT = oasst2, PROSE = cccc + gutenberg + wikimedia)
  F(CHAT) and F(PROSE), final-checkpoint bpb, are the primary metrics (every rule reads F, as in E2). Each of these
  also gets its own MDE: M(CHAT) and M(PROSE) (mean of the last 3 checkpoints); CHAT user turns and assistant turns;
  PROSE per source; stackexchange, irc and dolly; and the mean training loss over the last 2% of steps (paired
  training loss on an identical stream, LEDGER row 39).

STATISTICS (per metric and size; fixed now)
  a_s, b_s the arms' values at seed s; d_s = b_s - a_s; N = 8 pairs at 5M, 3 at 20M.
  sigma_seed   pooled SD of A and B across seeds, df N - 1 (7 at 5M, 2 at 20M): under CRN a_s and b_s are
               correlated, so the two variance estimates are not independent and pooling does not add df.
  SD_d         SD of d_s (df N - 1: 7 at 5M, 2 at 20M); rho = corr(a, b).
  sigma_rep    SD over A1, R2, R3 (df 2): GPU nondeterminism at a fixed seed. Share of the paired noise it explains:
               2 * sigma_rep^2 / SD_d^2, reported.
  sigma_init, sigma_data   SD within each Part 3 family (df 4).
  MDE, two-sided alpha 0.05, 80% power, the reference SD borrowed from E3 (so df is E3's):
    paired    MDE_p(k) = (t(0.975, df) + t(0.80, df)) * SD_d / sqrt(k); the factor is 3.261 at df 7, 5.363 at df 2
    unpaired  MDE_u(k) = (t(0.975, df) + t(0.80, df)) * sigma_seed * sqrt(2 / k); the same factors (df 7 and 2)
    for k = 1, 2, 3, 5, in bpb and as % of A's mean. Sensitivity column, not read: the same with each SD at its
    one-sided 80% upper bound (x1.353 at df 7, x2.117 at df 2).
  k_needed(delta) = the smallest k with MDE_p(k) <= delta, for delta = 0.5%, 1% and 2% of A's mean.
  CRN ratio = sqrt(2) * sigma_seed / SD_d. loop.md's rule: a ratio of 2 or more makes CRN mandatory for every
    screen. Below 2, CRN stays the default (PLAN 5) and a screen may use unpaired seeds without a deviation.
  Averaging (loop.md ledger idea 15): SD_d of M vs SD_d of F, per metric and size. Later screens may decide on M only
    where M's SD_d is at least 30% below F's at 5M, and then only with its checkpoints at fixed fractions of the run
    (a harness change; E2 showed that rolling saves land at different decay points per run length).
  Descriptive: over the 28 two-seed subsets at 5M, the share in which both d_s carry the sign of the 8-seed mean d
    (how often a 2-seed screen agrees with 8 seeds on this contrast).
  Seed 1 chose A over B in E2, so d_1 is selected. The noise table uses all 8 seeds and prints the seeds 2..8 values
    (N = 7, df 6, factor 3.353) beside them.
  E2 re-check: a paired t-test on F(CHAT) d_s over seeds 2..8 (df 6). If B is lower with two-sided p < 0.05, the 5M
    LR becomes B (E2's rule), and screens use B; the noise numbers are unaffected. At 20M the same test over seeds
    2..3 is reported (df 1) and moves nothing.

HOW LATER SCREENS USE IT (defaults; a bet's pre-registration may be stricter, and says why if it is looser)
  1 Reference SD, by what the two arms share (each bet's pre-registration names its class):
      same init, same stream   LR, optimizer, schedule, weight decay, clip, loss masks        SD_ref = SD_d
      same stream, new init    architecture or shape, anything that changes the init draws  sqrt(2) * sigma_init
      same init, new stream    mix, shares, filtering, curriculum, templates                sqrt(2) * sigma_data
      both differ                                                                           sqrt(2) * sigma_seed
    Without Part 3, the middle two use sqrt(2) * sigma_seed. SDs travel as a fraction of A's mean; df_ref is the
    df of the SD used. A new mix keeps each source's internal order at the same seed, so its arms share part of the
    stream; sqrt(2) * sigma_data is the conservative end.
    Decay-branch screens (PLAN 5: data and post-training bets as branches off a shared trunk): a seed is an
    independent trunk. Two branches off one trunk share their whole trunk, so their difference is not a seed pair,
    and branch replays off one trunk are never counted as seeds.
  2 Seeds: k = max(2, k_needed(delta_bet)), where delta_bet is the bet's stated minimum effect (default 1% bpb). A
    bet whose k_needed exceeds 5 runs as registered with the label "underpowered", and a null reads "not resolved",
    never "no effect".
  3 Default decision: WIN if the mean paired improvement over k seeds exceeds t(0.975, df_ref) * SD_ref / sqrt(k)
    and every seed pair improves; NULL only if the 95% interval (same SD_ref) excludes delta_bet; else INCONCLUSIVE.
  4 20M confirmations use the larger of the 20M and 5M relative SDs, with the df of the one used. These are
    continuous metrics only; generative pass rates stay reserved for effects of 5 points or more (PLAN Phase 3).
  5 Refresh: when the engine flags, tokenizer version, training mix, corpus build or eval sets change, A and B are
    rerun at 5M for seeds 9, 10 and 11 (6 runs, about 3 h; the seed-9 pair also serves E2's refresh g check). Part 1
    reruns under the new setting before the next screen decision if the mean of the 3 d's falls outside E3's 95%
    prediction interval for a 3-pair mean, or if their SD exceeds SD_d by more than the chi-square 95% bound for
    df 2 (x1.731). One pair, as first written, could not show a change in the noise, which is what a refresh is for.

DEVIATIONS POLICY
  E2's, unchanged (a non-finite loss is a divergence and is never resumed). Also: an E3 run is never rerun because
  its value looks odd. Only a crash (one resume from its checkpoint) or a recorded engine or data mismatch reruns a
  run, and the reason is logged before the rerun. A diverged E3 run is a GAP and its pair is dropped from every SD.

KNOWN LIMITS
  With 8 pairs SD_d is known to within about a factor of 1.35 (80% upper bound); at 20M, with 3 pairs, much less.
  The A/B contrast is an LR change; bets that move the model more may be noisier, which is why the classes above
  fall back to sigma_init, sigma_data or sigma_seed. Lock-in at 30M (PLAN: 5 seeds if seed pass rates spread by more
  than 0.3 at 30M) is not measured here; Part 4b gives the 5M and 20M spread only. The numbers hold for tok_v0_8k,
  the pinned starter mix and this loader until a refresh (rule 5) says otherwise.
