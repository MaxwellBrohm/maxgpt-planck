WHY
  P-173 (LEDGER): "Learnable per-row/column multipliers"; prior art Velikanov 2601.04890 (Falcon-H1-Tiny, 90M); limit
  #4; "Default: loss gain beyond paired noise". The paper's form: W_bar_ij = r_i x W_ij x c_j, a learnable
  generalisation of muP multipliers that frees each matrix's scale from the weight-decay/noise equilibrium norm. It
  reports gains with Adam and with Muon (Table 2: downstream average +1.10 with Muon) at 0.5B and 30-240 GT, and TII
  uses it in every Falcon-H1-Tiny model (arch.verify.md 72). Planck's matrices train with NorMuon and cautious weight
  decay, the setting the paper names. Nothing at 5M.

QUESTION (decided, B7)
  Do learnable row and column multipliers on every block matrix change held-out chat bpb at 5M, 250M slots?

THE CHANGE (one flag)
  model.multipliers: true (new field; default false). In every block, for each of the seven matrices q_proj, k_proj,
  v_proj, o_proj, gate_proj, up_proj, down_proj (out x in), a row vector r (out) and a column vector c (in); the forward
  uses W_bar = r[:, None] x W x c[None, :] (the paper's index form, computed each forward; no merge during training).
  Not on: the tied embedding and head (the paper leaves the projector unchanged and its preceding RMSNorm already acts
  as column multipliers; tied, a multiplier on the embedding would also move the head), the attention gate projection
  (192 x 3), norms and scalars. Both vectors on every matrix, as the paper's experiments ("both row and column
  multipliers on all matrices", symmetries handled by weight decay), not its symmetry-free subset.
  Init: ones (constant: no RNG draw), so the arm starts as the exact BASE function and keeps every BASE draw.
  Parameters per layer: 4 x (192 + 192) + 3 x (192 + 488) = 3,576; x 8 = 28,608; total 5,038,741 (+0.57%), inside 2%.
  Optimizer: r and c go to the "scalar" group (AdamW at scalar_lr = r_5 x eta at g 1), routed by name, with weight decay
  2e-3 on them only (the paper: "We converged to applying a small weight decay lambda_lrm = 2x10^-3 to multipliers"; the
  scalar group otherwise has none). NorMuon and its cautious decay on W are unchanged. Fixed 2026-10-11 while
  writing the code, before any value: r and c form their own AdamW group ("mult": scalar_lr, decay 2e-3, decoupled
  and NOT cautious, as the paper's AdamW decay), so no other scalar decays (a test).

KNOWN RISKS
  - The symmetries the paper names: Q and K meet only in their product, so scale can drift between q_proj's and
    k_proj's multipliers, and QK-norm after q and k divides out much of their row scale, so those r vectors are partly
    redundant; redundant multipliers can "lead to NaN values". A divergence scores +inf (C7), never dropped.
  - The multiplier LR is BASE's scalar LR (no own multiplier, as batch 1's new-module rule); the paper tuned LRs per
    configuration.
  - 250M slots at 5M is short of the paper's regime (30-240 GT).
  - Starter mix and tok_v0_8k only.
