WHY
  P-113 (LEDGER): "Raise output-head rank (factorized 128/256/384, mixture-of-softmaxes)"; prior art Godey et al.
  (<= 410M), 2608.16671 (harm not shown); limits #10 and #5; "Default: repetition rate falls at higher rank". At 5M the
  head is tied and d is 192, so the log-probability matrix over 8,192 tokens has rank at most 193 (192, plus one for
  the normaliser: the softmax bottleneck). A factorized 128 head would lower the rank, and an untied 256 or 384 head
  costs 2.1M to 3.1M parameters
  (40-60% of the model), far outside 2%. A mixture of softmaxes (MoS, Yang et al. 2017) with K 2 components is not
  bound at rank d: log(sum_k pi_k softmax(h_k E^T)) is nonlinear in the contexts, so its log-probability matrix can
  be high rank (Yang et al.'s argument; corrected after critique R7, which struck an earlier "rank up to 384"). It is
  not a match to the ledger's factorized 128/256/384 values; it is the head-rank form that fits one change at 2%
  (74,112 parameters).

QUESTION (decided, B7)
  Does a 2-component mixture of softmaxes over the tied head change held-out chat bpb at 5M, 250M slots? The repetition
  rate is reported beside it (the ledger's own reading), no rule.

THE CHANGE (one flag)
  model.mos: 2 (new; default 0 = off). From the final hidden state z (after the final norm): prior pi = softmax(W_pi z)
  over 2 components; component k's context h_k = tanh(W_k z); p(v) = sum over k of pi_k softmax(h_k E^T)(v), E the tied
  embedding. The model returns log p as its logits (softmax(log p) = p), so the loss, bpb.py, the IND hook and decoding
  read the mixture with no change on their side (a test). No biases (Planck's linears have none). Init: W_pi zero (an
  even prior); W_1 and W_2 = identity plus normal(0, init_std), drawn after every BASE draw (registered last), so the two
  components differ from step 0 and every BASE parameter keeps its draw. Parameters 2 x 192 x 192 + 192 x 2 = 74,112;
  total 5,084,245 (+1.48%), inside 2%. Optimizer: W_1, W_2 in "matrix" (NorMuon, as block matrices), W_pi in "scalar"
  (a 2 x 192 prior), routed by name.
  Engine varlen at micro 16 if the smoke's mem_fit is ok (two full logit tensors and their log-softmaxes); else micro 8 x
  accum 2 with its own BASE at that setting at every seed (B4 (4)), logged here before any g-check.

KNOWN RISKS
  - The tanh contexts start compressed against BASE's z, so the arm starts off BASE's function (not an identity
    init); the g-check covers the LR, not that.
  - tanh(W_k z) lies in [-1, 1]^192, so every logit h_k . E_v is bounded by ||E_v||_1: a sharp next-token
    distribution needs the tied embedding rows to grow, which also rescales the input stream (tied). This is part of
    the form tested, named in the REJECT wording (critique R7); per-module, the mean ||E_v||_1 of arm and BASE at the
    final checkpoint is reported.
  - Two softmaxes per position double the output cost and memory; a micro 8 x 2 setting moves the arm to its own BASE.
  - Repetition at 5M under greedy decoding is a crude reading of limit #5.
  - Starter mix and tok_v0_8k only.
