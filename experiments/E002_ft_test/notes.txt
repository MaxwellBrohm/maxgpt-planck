  steps then cosine to 0, grad clip 1.0, fp32 weights and optimizer, loss on answer tokens only,
  effective batch 16.

  Why not 512: at 512 the rejection removed 45-55% of d = 9-10 examples and, worse, removed the long
  examples selectively (twoslot 31% kept vs noupd 81% at d >= 8), which raised the share of
  first-mentioned golds at long distance from 0.36 to 0.47. REPORT 7.4 allows "512 or gradient
  checkpointing"; checkpointing keeps the training mix unbiased. validate.py checks this.
  fp32, not bf16: pure-bf16 AdamW at lr 5e-5 drops most updates (bf16 resolution ~4e-3 relative),
  which would corrupt the measurement; fp32 fits (dry-run peaks below).
