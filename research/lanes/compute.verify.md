# Verification: compute feasibility lane

Checked 2026-09-23. Primary sources I used:

- **Hardware and software:** the NVIDIA RTX Blackwell whitepaper (Table 6 and the 5080/5070 Ti tables, read with pdftotext), the Turing whitepaper, and NVIDIA's archived TITAN RTX product page. The CUDA release notes and forward-compatibility page. The PyTorch cu118 wheel index. vLLM release assets and notes from the GitHub API, and `requirements/cuda.txt` at v0.10.1.1. The SGLang install doc.
- **Papers:** arXiv PDFs for RS-KD (2503.16870), Chinchilla replication (2404.10102), Distillation Scaling Laws (2502.08606) and Strong Teacher Not Needed (2605.23857). Abstracts for DataDecide, Wen et al. and Muennighoff et al. The Qwen3 and Qwen2.5 reports.
- **Model cards and configs:** SmolLM2 model cards, `config.json` and HF API parameter totals.
- **Public benchmarks:** llm.c issue 796 and discussion 481 (via the GraphQL API).
- **Anthropic pages:** the Claude pricing page, the Max plan support article, and the Consumer Terms.
- **Max's local files (read-only):** the four A/B `*.metrics.jsonl`, `configs/`, `eval/harness.py`, `data/loader.py`, `posttrain/sft_data.py`, `data/prepare.py`, `WRITEUP_NOTES.md`, `maxgpt-3/config.py` and the Lambda memory file.

I recomputed every FLOP, parameter, wall-clock and storage figure. I re-ran the lane's Claude usage aggregation and a precise recount of the limit errors, the lane's Mac benchmark (while the Mac was busy, see claim 9), and the Busbridge distillation law at Max's scale. WebSearch was exhausted for this session, so the 2026 discovery below used the arXiv API and GitHub directly. The omissions list may be incomplete for that reason.

**Summary.** The hardware specs, software-version facts, FLOP arithmetic, storage arithmetic and local-code findings hold up almost everywhere. Four things need fixing:

1. **The "NorMuon behind at step 4000, ahead after decay" flip in Max's A/B is an eval-noise artifact.** It is not the Wen et al. phenomenon. All four variants trained on the identical data order: detrended train-loss fluctuations correlate at 1.00. On that paired training loss, NorMuon is ahead of AdamW at every checkpoint, including step 4000 (3.189 vs 3.306 over the 250 steps up to it).
2. **The same paired training loss puts the recipe gain at 0.134 nats (4.5%), not 0.207 nats (6.9%).** This is a zero-cost, low-noise metric the lane missed while diagnosing eval noise.
3. **Personal usage figures:** the recount of Max's own session-limit events is omitted from the public copy.
4. **The Qwen3-0.6B compute figure uses d=1024 in the attention term.** Qwen3-0.6B's attention width is 2048 (16 heads x 128). The corrected figure is about 1.8e23 FLOPs and about 67 years on the 4 edge Titans, not 1.5e23 and 57 years.

## Scale misapplications

1. **Wen et al.'s decay-induced ranking flip (0.1B-1.2B) was read into Max's 124M A/B.** The observed "flip" is unpaired eval noise (claim 20b). The optimizer ranking in Max's run never changed on the paired training loss.
2. **"Distillation is favourable exactly in Max's regime" stretches the Busbridge law.** I evaluated the paper's fitted laws (Eq. 8, constants from its coefficient table: supervised E 1.220, A 3355, B 18186, α 0.408, β 0.431, γ 0.452; distillation c0 2.549, c1 522.6, f1 0.090, d1 1.315) for a 120M non-embedding student with an Ultra-like 1.0B teacher at 100B tokens. At equal student tokens, distillation beats supervised training by **0.042 nats at 100B** and by 0.026 nats at 300B. At 10B student tokens the advantage is 0.128 nats. That 100B gain equals about 3.3x more supervised tokens, a real win for offline RS-KD whose teacher pass is paid once. But it is **neutral when compute is matched** for online KD (+0.001 nats at 100B), and a 3B/1T teacher is already neutral-to-harmful at 300B student tokens (+0.003). The law is fitted on students of 143M and up and extrapolated here to 660-2000 tokens/param, so treat it as indicative. "Modest token budget" in the paper means far fewer tokens per parameter than the 100-300B the lane plans.
3. **RS-KD's "unknown at 150M" is partly answered by the paper itself.** Its student-size sweep (100M to 3B, 8B teacher) shows the smallest gain at 100M. Top-K KD at small students also fell below CE in earlier work (Peng et al., as cited there).
4. **Qwen3's distillation was post-training (off-policy and on-policy), not pretraining KD.** The lane uses it as evidence about pretraining compute and distillation. The lesson that transfers to Max is cheap post-training distillation, not a pretraining-token equivalence.
5. **Chinchilla data terms at 2T-36T tokens are extrapolated** well beyond the fitted range (Chinchilla trained to about 500B tokens). The parameter-term comparison between 150M and 0.5-0.6B is interpolated and is the sturdier half of the argument. The lane's "illustrative" flag covers this.
6. **The MaxGPT-3 anchor (about 87% MFU on a half-rate GeForce accumulate peak)** sits well above anything public: llm.c reaches about 70% on a 5080, and Max's own Titans reach 33-47%. It is likely a rounded log value and should not pull the 5070 estimate upward.

## Omissions

1. **The A/B logs already contain a paired, low-noise metric: training loss on the identical data order.** It gives the real recipe gain (0.134 nats, 4.5%), removes the phantom optimizer flip, and costs nothing to use. For future ablations, log a running mean of paired train loss (single-epoch data is effectively held out) next to a fixed eval set.
2. **The session-limit counts reflect fan-out size, not chronic saturation** (claim 14). A per-day and per-file breakdown should replace "hundreds of session-limit hits in two recent weeks".
3. **nanochat as a compute reference.** It is Karpathy's full pretrain-to-chat pipeline (repo created 2025-10-13). The README calls its speedrun "a 4e19 FLOPs capability model", "a bit like talking to a kindergartener". Its leaderboard reached GPT-2-grade CORE (0.2626) in 1.65 h on 8xH100 by Mar 14 2026. The 150M at 50B tokens is 6.2e19 FLOPs, the same order. It also documents fp16 + GradScaler for SM<80 cards. Useful as a public, measured calibration of what about 1e19-1e20 FLOPs buys in chat quality, although its models are larger (about d24-d26) and compute-optimal rather than over-trained. https://github.com/karpathy/nanochat
4. **Teacher-strength evidence from 2026.** "Strong Teacher Not Needed? On Distillation in LLM Pretraining" (arXiv 2605.23857, May 2026) makes two findings. With proper CE/KD loss mixing, "even small and undertrained teachers improve larger students". And "pushing the teacher further ... can saturate or even reverse the distillation gains". This supports Ultra (a 1.1B at about 91 tokens/param) as a teacher. **Scale flag: students 0.7B-8B trained on 50B tokens only.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23857
5. **Cross-tokenizer distillation is now practical and was not costed.** ALM (arXiv 2503.20083), Byte-Level Distillation (2604.07466, 1B-8B) and Byte-Prefix Marginalization for on-policy distillation (2607.22334, Jul 2026) let Qwen3 or SmolLM2 act as logit teachers without switching Max's tokenizer. That weakens "Ultra is the only logit teacher", and it changes the storage maths: the teacher-side ids need 18 bits for a 151,936 vocab. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20083 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07466 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22334
6. **Initialising from a larger model plus KD saves the most compute at small token budgets.** "Where to Begin" (arXiv 2510.07227) initialises a small model from sub-networks of a larger model and adds KD. It matched a Pythia SLM's perplexity with 5.16x fewer FLOPs at 10B tokens but only 1.26x at 100B. That is the same pattern as the distillation law: large gains early, shrinking at Max's 100B+ budgets. It is also a cheap way to seed a 150M from Ultra's weights. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07227
7. **Max's trainer already has the chunked, checkpointed loss** (`loss_chunk: 2048`) the lane recommends for the 49k-vocab memory wall. The Mac OOM and micro-batch limits in section 3 come from the naive benchmark, not from Max's code.
8. **The Titan throughput baseline mixes configurations.** The 7,997 tok/s solo bench used mb2 with 8-bit AdamW, while the 16.1k DDP figure used mb1 with NorMuon and the arch tweaks. A matched solo bench of the actual 150M config (AdamW and NorMuon) on card 0 would pin sections 1.4-1.5 and the 68k DDP estimate. The 5070 needs the same `bench_micro.py` run the lane already lists.
