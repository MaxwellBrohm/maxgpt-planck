# Verification: census lane (sub-600M chat models)

Checked 2026-09-23 against primary sources: config.json and HF API `safetensors.total` fetched directly, model cards (raw README, or the rendered HF page for gated repos), arXiv full text (LFM2 2511.23404, Danube3 2407.09276, MobileLLM 2402.14905, Physics of LMs 3.3 2404.05405, Falcon-H1 2507.22448, Qwen3 2505.09388, Multi-IF 2410.15553, tau2-Bench 2506.07982), the Falcon-H1-Tiny blog HTML (tiiuae-tiny-h1-blogpost.hf.space), Liquid blogs, the evalchemy repo at TII's pinned commit, HF discussion threads, and Max's local files (read-only).

This file was written in two passes. The first pass ran out of WebSearch budget, so its 2026 discovery used the HF API and arXiv only. The second pass (after the machine restarted) re-fetched every load-bearing number, re-ran discovery with WebSearch, and changed one finding: the first pass said TII's MT-Bench judge was "most likely GPT-4o-mini". At TII's pinned evalchemy commit the command-line default maps to GPT-4, so the judge is undeterminable (details under claim 4 and omission 1).

Summary: every architecture and parameter number I could recompute was right, and the benchmark numbers match their sources. The problems are in the framing. Two "every/only" generalizations are broken by the lane's own table. One TII finding is taken out of its tool-calling context. The knowledge-capacity number is presented as recipe-independent. The lane also missed that TII's "MT-Bench" cites MT-Bench-101 but was run as the original 2-turn MT-Bench. And it missed the one same-tokenizer KD ablation below 400M (MobileLLM, neutral to negative).

### Other numbers in the report that recommendations lean on

- **"Memorization window (~500 GT)"** (section 3a). 500 GT is TII's conservative estimate for FalconMamba-7B. TII itself scales it linearly: "Linear scaling of the conservative 500 GT memorization window estimate for FalconMamba-7B leads to only 5 GT window size" at 100M. At 150M the same rule gives ~10 GT (my arithmetic, not measured). As quoted, the lane's figure is off by roughly 50-100x for Max's scale, and the rescaled figure is what justifies mixing SFT data into pretraining at all.
- **"Learnable multipliers plus Muon gave up to a 20% relative gain"**. The comparison is Muon with vs without learnable multipliers ("up to a 20% relative gain from random values" on MMLU/BBH/GSM8K). It is the gain from adding the multipliers, not from Muon.
- **"Distillation is near universal at the top"**. The lane leaves out the only sub-400M pretraining-KD ablation in the census (omission 3). MobileLLM distilled LLaMA-v2-7B into 125M and 350M students and got "comparable or inferior accuracy" at 2.6-3.2x the training time.
- **Qwen3 on-policy distillation "1/10 of the GPU hours"**. Verified, but it was measured "starting from the same off-policy distilled 8B checkpoint" on "math and code-related queries" only.
- **Generation defaults as a regime signal.** The lane cites LFM2.5's temperature 0.1. The Falcon-H1-Tiny blog makes the same point for its models: "Temperature 0.1 is recommended for all Falcon-H1 models, higher temperatures lead to inconsistent outputs". Both leading sub-250M models are tuned for near-greedy decoding. Pipis et al. (omission 13) find that low temperature is exactly where distilled students loop, so near-greedy decoding and multi-turn chat pull against each other.

## Scale misapplications

1. **Distillation evidence from strong, heavily trained teachers is applied to MaxGPT-Ultra.** Every teacher in the census was trained on trillions of tokens (LFM1-7B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Nemotron-Nano-4B, Apertus-8B, Gemma's large IT teacher, Qwen3 flagships). Ultra is 1.1B on 100B tokens (~91 tok/param), is still training, and learns from the same pretraining data as the planned student. The 7.3x ratio being "inside the census range" says nothing about teacher quality. The one pretraining-KD ablation below 400M (MobileLLM 125M/350M, same vocabulary as the teacher) was neutral to negative, and the LFM2 report has no KD ablation at all.
2. **On-policy KD headline gains (MobileLLM-R1.5) come from 360M and 950M.** At 140M, the size nearest Max's target, GSM8K went only 4.1 to 8.3 and MATH500 6.2 to 16, all on math and code reasoning with a 4B teacher and ~6.5 days on 128 GPUs. The lane's "speculative for chat" label is right, but the lever table should carry the small 140M effect.
3. **"Second-turn collapse steepens with smaller size" is measured on 0.5B to 4B models** from different families and recipes (n=7). Nothing below 0.5B is measured, and at 0.5B the recipe variance (0.58 vs 0.73) equals the size trend.
4. **Qwen3's strong-to-weak distillation, "1/10 of the GPU hours" of RL, was measured at 8B** on math and code. The lane flags the 8B in-line, but it still sits in the lever table as support for a 150M chat plan.
5. **The ~2 bits/param knowledge ceiling assumes ideal training** (1000 exposures, no junk). At ~670 tok/param over a web-heavy mix, a 150M model will sit below 2 bits/param. "2% of a 7B" is a ratio of ceilings, not a prediction.
6. **TII's memorization window (500 GT) was measured at 7B** and quoted without the linear rescaling TII itself applies to tiny models (~5 GT at 100M).
7. **LFM2 to LFM2.5 token scaling (10T to 28T at 354M, confounded with RL)** is used as evidence for the "more tokens" lever. It sits roughly 100-300x beyond Max's compute, so it cannot tell him the slope in the 100B-token regime he can reach. The Falcon-H1-Tiny SFT-fraction sweep at 100 GT is the closest-scale evidence the lane has, and it is not used for this.
8. **TII's "CoT causes loops" was measured on a 90M tool-calling specialist** but is applied to chat SFT data in general (lever "no long CoT"). TII's own CoT-pretrained R-90M is a counterexample.

## Omissions

1. **TII's "MT-Bench" identity.** Cited as MT-Bench-101 (Bai et al. 2024), but the Falcon-H1 report runs "MTBench ... turn 1 & 2 average" through evalchemy pinned at f735e77, whose MTBench task loads FastChat's original 2-turn `mt_bench` questions. The judge is either GPT-4 (evalchemy's command-line default `auto` maps to `gpt-4`) or GPT-4o-mini (the class default, or if passed explicitly); TII does not say which. Affects claims 4, 12 and 16, and the arch and posttrain lanes, which call it MT-Bench-101. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22448 ; https://github.com/mlfoundations/evalchemy/blob/f735e77/eval/chat_benchmarks/MTBench/eval_instruct.py ; https://github.com/mlfoundations/evalchemy/blob/f735e77/eval/eval.py
2. **tau2-Bench numbers for LFM2.5-230M and 350M** are the only published multi-turn dialogue metrics above 3 turns at this scale, and they are in a card the lane already cites. 230M: Telecom 5.26, Retail 13.68. 350M: 18.86, 17.84. The 230M-to-350M jump on Telecom (3.6x) is the clearest many-turn scale signal in the census. https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-230M
3. **MobileLLM's negative KD result at 125M and 350M** (Sec. 3.5, Appendix G, Table 16): LLaMA-v2-7B teacher, full-logit cross-entropy, students on the same 32,000-token vocabulary (full-logit KD requires one). Average zero-shot accuracy 43.9 label-only vs 43.8 with KD at 125M (29h vs 93h), 49.1 vs 48.8 at 350M (42h vs 109h). Scale caveat: exploratory runs of 120k iterations (about 0.25T tokens), measured on commonsense benchmarks, not chat. It is the closest published analogue to Max's same-tokenizer Ultra-to-150M plan. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14905
4. **MobileLLM-R1.5 details**: the small 140M-specific gain, the Nemotron-Nano-4B teacher, the semi-on-policy procedure, and the compute cost. https://huggingface.co/facebook/MobileLLM-R1.5-140M
5. **Pre-LLM open-domain multi-turn chatbots under 400M**: BlenderBot 90M (Roller et al. 2020, with multi-turn engagingness and humanness human evals) and DialoGPT 117M/345M/762M (2019, 147M Reddit exchanges). They set the historical floor for chit-chat coherence at Max's size without instruction following or reliable knowledge, and bear directly on the premise. https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.13637 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.00536
6. **Multi-turn degradation is not a small-model-only effect.** "LLMs Get Lost In Multi-Turn Conversation" reports an average 39% drop from single-turn to multi-turn across top open and closed LLMs. The census's "wall" section should weigh this before reading turn-2 loss at 0.5B as a size effect. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06120
7. **Meta MobileMoE (arXiv 2605.27358, weights June 2026)**: on-device MoE with SFT and QAT variants; MobileMoE-S is ~0.3B active. Outside the census's total-parameter scope, but a 2026 sub-400M-active instruct release. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27358 ; https://huggingface.co/facebook/MobileMoE-S-SFT
8. **Distil Labs fine-tuned LFM2.5-350M for multi-turn task dialogues** and reported "over 95% accuracy throughout the dialog" (vendor-relayed, task-specific, details unverified). https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-5-350m-no-size-left-behind

11. **Independent evaluation of the two headline tiny models.** Second-pass WebSearch and HF discussion threads found no third-party chat or instruction-following replication for LFM2.5-230M or Falcon-H1-Tiny-90M (the Falcon repo has 0 threads). The only community numbers are GSM8K on LFM2.5-230M: 29.7% (llama-eval, one user) and 29.08% avg@10 at T=0.6 (Liquid's mlabonne), roughly equal to the 350M. Replication stays **unverifiable**, not "none exists". https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-230M/discussions/10
12. **TurnWise (Graf, Pyatkin, Dziri, Lambert, Hajishirzi; arXiv 2603.16759, Mar 2026).** Directly addresses the lane's open question 2 (capacity vs SFT data). On Olmo 3 7B, adding 10k synthetic multi-turn conversations to SFT raised TurnWiseEval absolute from 10.0 to 24.1 and shrank the multi-turn minus single-turn gap from -13.9 to -1.0, at a cost of IFEval 81.3 to 76.7 and MMLU 66.7 to 61.4. It also releases a synthetic multi-turn data pipeline (TurnWiseData). **Scale: 7B only**, so it is a hypothesis for 150M, but it says the Danube3 turn-2 drop cannot be read as pure capacity. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16759
13. **"Wait, Wait, Wait... Why Do Reasoning Models Loop?" (Pipis et al., arXiv 2512.12895).** TII cites it: "distilled students loop significantly even when their teachers rarely do", looping is common at low temperature, and larger models loop less. This bears on two census levers at once (distillation from Ultra, and near-greedy decoding defaults) and on the loop failure mode the lane documents. Measured on reasoning models, not 150M chat. https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12895
14. **2026 multi-turn on-policy distillation papers**, which the lane's "untested on multi-turn chat" statement should acknowledge: ReOPD (arXiv 2607.04763, Jul 2026), turn-level curriculum OPD (2606.15912), TCOD (2604.24005). All are multi-turn **agentic** tool-use settings (math with Python, search), not open chat, and student sizes are not in the abstracts. So the lane's statement still holds for chat. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04763 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15912 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24005
15. **Catastrophic overtraining** (Springer et al., arXiv 2503.19206): OLMo-1B pretrained on 3T vs 2.3T tokens is over 2% worse after instruction tuning. This is a counterweight to "more tokens is the largest lever", but it was measured at 1B and 2,300-3,000 tok/param, far above Max's ~670, so it does not bind his plan. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19206
