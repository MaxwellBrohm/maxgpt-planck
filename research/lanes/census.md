# Lane: census of released chat models at or below ~600M parameters (through 2026-09-23)

Scope: every released model at or below about 600M total parameters that ships a chat or instruct variant or is presented as conversational. Architecture numbers were computed from each model's `config.json` (fetched directly from `https://huggingface.co/<repo>/resolve/main/config.json`) and total parameter counts from the Hugging Face API `safetensors.total` field (`https://huggingface.co/api/models/<repo>`), or, where a safetensors file stores a duplicated tied head (Qwen3-0.6B) or a vision tower (Qwen3.5-0.8B), from the safetensors header itself. Everything else carries its own URL. Vendor benchmark numbers are vendor-run unless stated; cross-vendor numbers from different harnesses are not comparable and are flagged where it matters.

---

## Bottom line

1. Max's "400-500M wall" is **partially accurate and mostly outdated**. It describes 2024 (Qwen2-0.5B, Qwen2.5-0.5B, Danube3-500M were the smallest mainstream chat models), but by mid-2026 specialist labs have moved the benchmarked frontier down to **~230M total / ~163M non-embedding** (LiquidAI LFM2.5-230M, June 2026, Multi-IF 37.7 vs 41.7 for Qwen3.5-0.8B and 44.3 for Gemma 3 1B) and even **91M** (TII Falcon-H1-Tiny-90M-Instruct, Jan 2026, MT-Bench 4.33 vs 3.8 for SmolLM2-360M-Instruct in the same harness).
2. What is still true: **nobody publishes evidence of open-domain, many-turn (5+ turn) coherent chat below ~400M.** The only multi-turn numbers at this size are 2-turn (MT-Bench) and 3-turn (Multi-IF) instruction following, and almost every sub-400M vendor explicitly scopes its model *away* from open conversation (Gemma 3 270M: "not designed for complex conversational use cases"; LFM2.5-230M: not recommended for "creative writing"; MobileLLM-R1: "not general-purpose chat models"; Monad: "no support yet for multi-turn").
3. The models that crossed the old wall did it with **brute-force token budgets, not magic**: 8,800 (Falcon-H1-Tiny) to 83,000 (LFM2.5-230M) training tokens per parameter, plus logit distillation, SFT-style data mixed into pretraining, DPO and RL. Max's 100B-token build on a 150M model is ~670 tokens/param, i.e. ~5x less compute than the Falcon-H1-Tiny recipe and ~290x less than LFM2.5-230M.
4. The clearest measured signature of the small-model wall is **second-turn collapse**: under a GPT-4 judge, Danube3-500M scores 4.16 on MT-Bench turn 1 and 2.40 on turn 2 (ratio 0.58), Qwen2-0.5B 0.73, while 1.8B-4B models hold 0.76-0.90. Degeneration (loops) and parroting context are the other recurring failure modes, including in Max's own Daimax measurements (SmolLM2-360M looped in 4/33 runs vs 0/33 for Qwen2.5-0.5B).
5. Two structural trends matter for design: (a) **embedding tax**: Gemma 3 270M is 63% embeddings (only 100M transformer params), MobileLLM-R1-140M 53%, Qwen3.5-0.8B 34%; the strongest sub-250M models keep embeddings at 18-30%; (b) **big-lab floors are rising** (Qwen 0.5B to 0.6B to 0.8B; Gemma 4's smallest is E2B) while **purpose-built tiny models come from specialists** (Liquid, TII, PleIAs, Meta MobileLLM, HF SmolLM2).
6. Factual knowledge is the one wall no recipe removes: measured knowledge capacity is ~2 bits/parameter, so a 150M model holds roughly 2% of what a 7B holds. A 150M chat model that "really chats" has to separate conversational mechanics (reference, instruction retention, turn-taking) from stored knowledge (offload to retrieval/tools).
7. For Max specifically: his previous small-model "wall" (MaxGPT-2/3 confident nonsense, loops) was measured at ~18-85 tokens/param, which is roughly 35x to 4,000x below the chat-capable models in this census (MobileLLM-350M at ~2,900 up to LFM2.5 at ~83,000); that experience says little about what 150M can do. The most budget-reachable frontier reference is Falcon-H1-Tiny (800B tokens at 90M), and the most directly usable asset he has is a **same-tokenizer teacher**: MaxGPT-Ultra (1.1B, same 49,152-token BPE) can supply logits for distillation, which most leading tiny models used in some form (SmolLM2 and Falcon-H1-Tiny are the notable exceptions).

### 3. Model-by-model notes, grouped by why the model exists

#### 3a. Designed for its size (specialist tiny-model programs)

- **LiquidAI LFM2 / LFM2.5 (350M, 230M).** Hybrid: gated short convolutions for most layers plus 6 GQA layers, found by hardware-in-the-loop search ([LFM2 report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23404)). LFM2 pretraining used a "decoupled, tempered Top-K" logit distillation objective with **Top-K=32 teacher logits per token from LFM1-7B**; post-training was SFT on 5.39M samples (26.6% general, 17.1% instruction following, 13.2% RAG, **10.1% real-world chats**, 10.1% tool use, ...), length-normalized preference optimization on ~700k conversations with 5 on-policy samples each, then model merging. LFM2.5-350M extended pretraining from 10T to 28T tokens and added "large-scale multi-stage RL"; at identical architecture, Multi-IF went 32.92 to 44.92 and IFEval 64.96 to 76.96 (confounded: tokens and RL changed together). LFM2.5-230M (June 2026) is "our most compact model yet", 19T tokens, post-trained by "(1) supervised fine-tuning with distillation from LFM2.5-350M, (2) direct preference optimization, and (3) multi-domain reinforcement learning" ([blog](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-5-230m)). Its ~163M non-embedding params are the closest published analogue to Max's 150M target. Liquid recommends both for extraction and on-device agents and says the 230M is not for "creative writing" and the 350M not for "math, code, and creative writing" ([350M blog](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-5-350m-no-size-left-behind)). Generation defaults are temperature 0.1 with repetition_penalty 1.05, which itself signals a narrow, low-entropy operating regime.
- **TII Falcon-H1-Tiny (90M English, 100M multilingual, R-90M, R-0.6B, Tool-Calling-90M, Coder-90M; Jan 2026)** ([blog](https://huggingface.co/spaces/tiiuae/tiny-h1-blogpost)). 24 layers, d=512, parallel attention and Mamba heads, 32,768 vocab chosen as "the smallest vocab size that our tokenizer series support", tied. 800 GT base training (80% STEM-focused mix, 20% FineWeb). Two notable recipe findings: (1) **"anti-curriculum" SFT-pretraining**: 25% pure SFT data mixed into the pretraining mixture for the full 800 GT (Tulu3 repeated "≳100" times) beat a classic pretrain-then-SFT curriculum on IFEval (53.47 to 66.08) and LiveBench (12.4 to 15.69) but **not on MT-Bench** (4.4 vs 4.33); (2) DPO for 1 epoch lifted MT-Bench from ~3.1 to ~4.3. They also report that "chain-of-thought reasoning traces in training data cause repetition loops rather than improved reasoning" for small models, and propose a "memorization window" (~500 GT) beyond which repeating a source is harmless. Learnable multipliers plus Muon gave "up to a 20% relative gain" on MMLU/BBH/GSM8K. No teacher distillation is described. No independent replication of the MT-Bench numbers was found.
- **PleIAs Monad (56.7M) and Baguettotron (321M)** ([blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/Pclanglais/synth-data-frontier)). Trained only on SYNTH, a fully synthetic dataset expanded from 50,000 Wikipedia "vital articles", under 200B tokens, native thinking traces in a compressed notation, "less than 1000 H100 hours" of final training. Extreme depth: 64 layers at d=256 (Monad, custom <8k-token tokenizer) and 80 layers at d=576 (Baguettotron). PleIAs' own table ([image](https://huggingface.co/PleIAs/Baguettotron/resolve/main/figures/table_evaluation.png)): Baguettotron MMLU 40 / GSM8K 39 / HotPotQA 51 vs SmolLM2-360M 25 / 11 / 16, Gemma-3-270M-IT 25 / 3 / 23, LFM2-350M 44 / 34 / 25, Qwen3-0.6B 44 / 40 / 64. Baguettotron "has support for multi-turn" with rolling thinking; removing thinking "significantly decreased performance". Monad "has no support yet for multi-turn". Relevant because Baguettotron's budget (623 tokens/param) is the same order as Max's (670).
- **Meta MobileLLM (125M, 350M; 2024) and MobileLLM-R1 / R1.5 (140M, 360M, 950M; 2025).** The original paper argued "deeper and thinner" beats wide at sub-billion scale, shared embeddings (saving ~16M params, "11.8% of total parameters" at 125M), and immediate block-wise layer sharing (+0.7/0.8 accuracy at 125M/350M) ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14905)). After identical chat fine-tuning, MobileLLM-350M reached MT-Bench 3.28 and 47% AlpacaEval win-rate vs text-davinci-001, above OPT-1.3B (2.24 / 38.8%), i.e. a 2024 demonstration that architecture + 1T tokens let 350M beat 2022-era 1.3B chat. MobileLLM-R1 reversed two of those design choices (15 layers, 128k vocab, so ~53% of the 140M is embeddings), used **logit KD from Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct during 200B tokens of mid-training**, and is explicitly "not general-purpose chat models" ([R1 card](https://huggingface.co/facebook/MobileLLM-R1-140M)). MobileLLM-R1.5 added **on-policy KD as the final post-training stage**: at 360M, MATH 28.4 to 63.4 and GSM8K 24.5 to 52.8 ([R1.5 card](https://huggingface.co/facebook/MobileLLM-R1.5-140M)). MobileLLM-Pro is 1.08B total (API count), out of scope.
- **HF SmolLM2 (135M, 360M; Oct 2024).** Llama-style, 49,152 vocab (same size as Max's tokenizer), 30 and 32 layers. Single-stage pretraining on 2T / 4T tokens ("smaller models benefited from a single-stage training approach with consistently high-quality data"); SFT on smol-smoltalk, which removed "complex instruction-following tasks (e.g., function calling) and hard examples from MagPie-Ultra to better align with the models' capacity", then DPO on UltraFeedback; no distillation ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02737)). HF has released no newer model at this size; its only 2026 sub-200M release is **nanowhale-100m** (Apr 2026), an educational DeepSeek-V4-architecture model trained on ~2.6B tokens whose card says "Generations are often incoherent or factually wrong" ([card](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/nanowhale-100m)).
- **IBM Granite 4.0 Nano 350M / H-350M (Oct 2025).** ~15T tokens in four stages; "supervised finetuning, reinforcement learning, and model merging"; lists "multilingual dialog use cases" ([card](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-4.0-350m)). IFEval average 55.4 / 61.63; Multi-IF 24.21 / 28.70 in Liquid's harness.
- **Google Gemma 3 270M (Aug 2025).** "170 million embedding parameters due to a large vocabulary size and 100 million for our transformer blocks"; "designed from the ground up for task-specific fine-tuning"; "not designed for complex conversational use cases" ([Google blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/introducing-gemma-3-270m/)). Trained on 6T tokens. Gemma 3 family pretraining uses distillation ("We sample 256 logits per token, weighted by teacher probabilities") and post-training distills from "a large IT teacher" plus RL ([Gemma 3 report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19786)); whether the 270M used the identical recipe is unsourced (it was released after the report). FunctionGemma-270M-it (Oct 2025) is a function-calling specialization. There is no Gemma 4 model at this size: Gemma 4's smallest is E2B ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemma-4/)); the 78M "gemma-4-E2B-it-assistant" repos are speculative-decoding drafters (4 layers, d=256), not chat models ([config](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-E2B-it-assistant/resolve/main/config.json)).

#### 3b. Afterthoughts of a larger family (smallest rung of a big ladder)

- **Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct** (18T tokens; >1M SFT examples and ~150k offline DPO pairs family-wide; IFEval 27.9) and **Qwen3-0.6B** (36T family tokens; "Strong-to-Weak Distillation" for all small models: off-policy on teacher outputs then on-policy KL to teacher logits, which Qwen says "achieves significantly better performance than reinforcement learning while requiring approximately only 1/10 of the GPU hours", measured at 8B) ([Qwen2.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15115), [Qwen3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09388)). **Qwen3.5-0.8B** (Feb 2026) is a multimodal hybrid (Gated DeltaNet + gated attention, MTP head, vision tower) whose 248k vocab puts 254M params in the embedding table. Qwen's floor has risen each generation (0.5B, 0.6B, 0.8B) and Qwen3.6 / Qwen3.8 (Apr-Aug 2026) have no small sizes ([Qwen org listing](https://huggingface.co/Qwen)).
- **Hunyuan-0.5B-Instruct** (Tencent, Jul 2025): IFEval 49.7, strong math for size (MATH 48.5), tokens unsourced ([card](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hunyuan-0.5B-Instruct)). **ERNIE-4.5-0.3B-PT** (Baidu, Jun 2025): post-trained 0.36B, tokens and chat numbers unsourced ([card](https://huggingface.co/baidu/ERNIE-4.5-0.3B-PT)). **MiniCPM4-0.5B** (Jun 2025): 1T tokens ([card](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM4-0.5B)). **Apertus-v1.1-0.5B-Instruct** (May 2026): "pre-training distillation" from Apertus-8B with a 90/10 mix of KL and cross-entropy over 1.7T tokens ([card](https://huggingface.co/swiss-ai/Apertus-v1.1-0.5B-Instruct)). **H2O-Danube3-500M-chat** (Jul 2024): 4T tokens, SFT with prompt-loss masking. **OpenELM-270M/450M-Instruct** (Apr 2024): layer-wise scaling, no chat benchmarks. **Falcon-H1-0.5B-Instruct** (May 2025): 2.5T tokens, 36-layer hybrid, the highest vendor MT-Bench at this size (7.06, TII harness).
- **Compressed rather than trained**: Multiverse's LittleLamb 0.3B is Qwen3-0.6B compressed ~50% to 290M with a proprietary method ([card](https://huggingface.co/MultiverseComputingCAI/LittleLamb)); no chat benchmarks.

#### 3c. Research and educational

- **Karpathy nanochat d20** (Oct 2025): 561M params, 11.2B tokens (20:1), $92 on 8xH100; chat ability came from midtraining on SmolTalk (460k) plus SFT; final MMLU 0.315, ARC-E 0.388, GSM8K 0.046 (0.076 after RL), ChatCORE 0.088 ([discussion](https://github.com/karpathy/nanochat/discussions/1)). It is the reference point for a Chinchilla-budget chat model: it talks, but as a demonstration, not a usable assistant. The speedrun has since moved to "time to GPT-2" at depth 24-26 ([leaderboard](https://github.com/karpathy/nanochat/blob/master/dev/LEADERBOARD.md)).
- **TinyStories family** (2023): models "below 10 million total parameters" or with "only one transformer block" produce "fluent and consistent stories with several paragraphs" when the data distribution is restricted to a 3-4-year-old's vocabulary ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07759)). TinyStories-33M's config (4 layers, d=768, 50,257 vocab) implies roughly 68M total params if embeddings are counted, so the "33M" label understates it ([config](https://huggingface.co/roneneldan/TinyStories-33M/resolve/main/config.json)). The dialogue analogue, **TinyDialogues** (~29M words of GPT-4-generated child-centered conversations), was used for GPT-2/RoBERTa learning studies, not to ship a chat model ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.03617)).
- **BabyLM 2025** ([findings](https://aclanthology.org/2025.babylm-main.28/)): 100M-word data limit, GPT-2-small-scale models; a new Interaction track allowed teacher feedback but "None of the submissions in the Interaction track outperformed models submitted to the Strict track" on the main score. The two submissions relevant to chat: ContingentChat (teacher-edited responses improved "contextually contingent and cohesive dialogue turns", and preference post-training "improved multi-turn interaction quality") and a storytelling feedback study where "fewer than one million interactive tokens" gave gains "comparable to a model trained on 100M tokens, particularly in narrative cohesion and entity tracking".

#### 3d. 2026 community tiny chat models (solo builders, consumer GPUs)

A Hugging Face API sweep (text-generation models, top 1,000 by likes, downloads and trending, plus 70 org listings, filtered to <=650M and created after mid-2025) found a wave of hobbyist sub-150M chat models in Jul-Sep 2026: **Supra2-100M-Instruct** (30B tokens, Qwen3 architecture, fine-tuned on one RTX 5060 Ti; 53 likes, ~80k downloads), Supra-50M-Instruct (20B tokens, Alpaca-Cleaned SFT), **PetitGPT** (124.6M, ~13B tokens on one RTX 4090, IFEval prompt-strict 17.19 vs 21.63 for SmolLM2-135M-Instruct in the same harness), GPT-X2.5-135M and cagliostro-v3 (75B tokens each, claimed within ~0.6-2 points of SmolLM2-135M on a community base-model index with ~27x fewer tokens), PicoLM-80M, Surjo-100m, Doge-160M/320M-Instruct (SmolTalk SFT + UltraFeedback DPO). None publishes multi-turn benchmarks. They matter as a feasibility signal: solo builders on one consumer GPU get within shouting distance of SmolLM2-135M on base benchmarks at 13-75B tokens.

### 4. Credible reports of real conversational quality

- **Gemma 3 270M**: a Google researcher on the launch thread said it is "Not designed for conversational or reasoning use cases"; users reported it repeating "Mount Everest" as the second-tallest mountain despite corrections and infinite repetition loops ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44902148)); Simon Willison: basic Q&A like capitals works, SVG generation never did ([blog](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Aug/14/gemma-3-270m/)).
- **Supra2-100M-Instruct's own showcase** is an unusually honest multi-turn transcript at 100M/30B tokens ([card](https://huggingface.co/SupraLabs/Supra2-100M-Instruct)): it stays on topic across a follow-up ("how will it influence work life?") but when the user answers "Sure!" to its offer to expand on "pros and contras" of fast food, it produces bullets on fast food, **contraception** and weight loss, i.e. it resolves the referent by surface-token association ("contras") rather than by discourse. The same card has it date Pearl Harbor to 2001.
- **Max's own Daimax measurements** (in-app, 11 cases x 3 runs, temperature 0.7; [BENCHMARK.md](~/Documents/Projects/daimax/docs/BENCHMARK.md), 2026-09-03b): SmolLM2-360M looped until the token ceiling in 4/33 runs (a 5-gram repetition measure scored a real user transcript 0.914), Qwen3-0.6B 1/33, Qwen2.5-0.5B 0/33; SmolLM2-360M also claimed to be "Daimax-Pro" by parroting text retrieved from earlier conversations, and failed 21+7. Separately, Qwen3.5-0.8B with thinking on spent 1,030 words re-reading its system prompt without answering "name three primary colours" (Daimax project notes). Small n, one harness, but first-hand.
- **Max's MaxGPT-2/3** ([WRITEUP_NOTES.md](~/Documents/Projects/Max's%20AI%20Model/WRITEUP_NOTES.md)): "Bitcoin.com" low-entropy loops, confident nonsense on "What is photosynthesis?", recipe answers to color questions. MaxGPT-2 was 110M on 482M unique tokens (~2B token passes, ~18 tokens/param); MaxGPT-3 235M on 4.6B unique tokens (20B passes, ~85 tokens/param).
- r/LocalLLaMA could not be queried (Reddit blocked the fetch, and search returned no threads), so community evidence here is HF discussions, HN and model-card transcripts only.

### 5. Trends across the census

- **Embedding share.** The best-performing sub-400M chat models keep embeddings at 18-30% (LFM2.5 29% / 19%, Falcon-H1-Tiny 18%, SmolLM2 21% / 13%, Granite 23-29%). Outliers are family afterthoughts or multilingual/multimodal designs: Gemma 3 270M 63%, MobileLLM-R1-140M ~53%, llm-jp-3-150m 67%, Qwen3.5-0.8B 34%. Gemma 3 270M has fewer transformer parameters (100M) than SmolLM2-135M (106M); its "270M" is mostly a 256k-token dictionary.
- **Tokens per parameter.** The sub-600M models with the strongest chat and instruction-following numbers were trained at 8,800-83,000 tokens/param (Falcon-H1-Tiny 8.8k, SmolLM2-135M 14.9k, Gemma 270M 22k, Qwen2.5-0.5B 36k, Granite 43k, Qwen3-0.6B 60k, LFM2.5 79-83k). The lowest-budget model with a respectable published chat score is MobileLLM-350M at ~2,900 tokens/param (MT-Bench 3.28, 2024). Below that sit synthetic-data models (Baguettotron 623) and hobby models (100-555). Chinchilla's ~20 tokens/param (nanochat) produces a talking demo, not an assistant.
- **Distillation is near universal at the top.** LFM2 (top-32 logits from 7B), Gemma 3 (256 sampled logits), Qwen3 (off- and on-policy logits), MobileLLM-R1 (KL to Llama-3.1-8B logits), MobileLLM-R1.5 (on-policy KD), Apertus (KL from 8B), LFM2.5-230M (SFT distilled from the 350M). The notable non-distilled exceptions are SmolLM2 and Falcon-H1-Tiny.
- **Architecture.** The two newest benchmark leaders under 250M (LFM2.5-230M, Falcon-H1-Tiny) are both hybrids (conv or Mamba plus a few attention layers); Qwen3.5 is also hybrid. The depth question is unsettled: MobileLLM (30 layers at 125M), Monad (64), Baguettotron (80) go deep and thin; LFM2.5-230M uses only 14 layers at d=1024; MobileLLM-R1 went back to 15.
- **Scoping away from chat.** Sub-400M releases increasingly target extraction, tool calling, classification or math reasoning and say so. Open-domain conversation is the use case vendors disclaim, not the one they claim.

## Where the frontier is, and the verdict on the premise

**Verdict: partially accurate.**

- *Wrong as stated for 2026:* coherent, instruction-following, short multi-turn chat now exists well below 400M. LFM2.5-230M (~163M non-embedding) scores within 4-7 points of Qwen3.5-0.8B and Gemma 3 1B on a 3-turn benchmark; Falcon-H1-Tiny-90M outscores SmolLM2-360M and Gemma 3 270M on MT-Bench in TII's harness; MobileLLM-350M already matched or beat 1.3B-class 2022 chat models in 2024.
- *Right in spirit:* no one has shown open-domain, many-turn conversation (the "remembers what was said turns ago, handles references, stays on topic" that Max wants) below ~400M, because (a) no benchmark at this scale measures beyond 3 turns, (b) the second turn already loses 27-42% of the first turn's MT-Bench score at 0.5B, and (c) the vendors who reached 90-230M did it by narrowing the target to instruction following and tool use and explicitly disclaiming open chat.
- *Where the practical frontier sits (Sep 2026), by capability:*
  - Fluent, grammatical, on-topic single replies: ~10M in a restricted domain (TinyStories), ~50-100M open-domain (Monad, Supra2, Falcon-H1-Tiny).
  - Verifiable single-turn instruction following at or above Qwen2.5-0.5B's level: ~90M (Falcon-H1-Tiny, IFEval 66 vs Qwen2.5-0.5B's 28-32 across harnesses).
  - 2-3 turn instruction following at 1B-model level: ~230-350M (LFM2.5).
  - Knowledge-bearing, many-turn, open-domain chat that a user would call "really chatting": not demonstrated below ~500M; Max's own Daimax testing still found Qwen2.5-0.5B the smallest model clean enough to ship.

The honest reframing for Max: the wall is not a parameter count. It is the product of (training tokens x distillation quality x how narrowly "chat" is defined), with a separate, genuinely parameter-bound ceiling on stored knowledge.

## What this implies for a ~150M chat model

1. **Budget reality.** Rough 6ND training compute: Max at 150M x 100B = 9.0e19 FLOPs. Falcon-H1-Tiny-90M (800B) = 4.4e20 (~5x), Baguettotron (200B at 321M) = 3.9e20 (~4x), SmolLM2-135M (2T) = 1.6e21 (~18x), Gemma 3 270M = 9.7e21 (~107x), LFM2.5-230M (19T) = 2.6e22 (~290x). The LFM2.5 recipe is out of reach; the Falcon-H1-Tiny and PleIAs recipes are within one order of magnitude. MaxGPT-3's 2.8e19 was ~3x below the planned 150M run.
2. **Target shape.** A 150M model with Max's 49,152-token tokenizer at d=576 spends 28.3M (19%) on tied embeddings, matching SmolLM2-135M's shape exactly (30 layers, 9/3 heads gives ~106M non-embedding). At d=768 it is 37.7M (25%). Either keeps the embedding share in the band the leading tiny models use; a 128k+ vocabulary would not.
3. **Teacher.** Most of the top models distilled from a larger teacher (ratios from 1.5x to ~57x). MaxGPT-Ultra (1.1B, 22 layers, d=2048, tied 49,152 vocab per [TECHNIQUES.md](~/Documents/Projects/Max's%20AI%20Model/maxgpt-ultra/TECHNIQUES.md)) shares the tokenizer, so top-K logit distillation (LFM2-style) and on-policy KD (Qwen3 / MobileLLM-R1.5 style) are possible without tokenizer alignment. A 7.3x size ratio is inside the range the census used (LFM2: 350M from 7B = 20x; MobileLLM-R1: 140M from 8B = 57x; LFM2.5-230M from 350M = 1.5x).
4. **Scope.** To be credible, "real chat" at 150M should be defined and measured as conversational mechanics (reference resolution, instruction retention across N turns, no loops, no parroting, topic maintenance), with factual knowledge offloaded to retrieval or tools. A 150M model will not out-know a 0.5B model; it can plausibly out-converse one on those mechanics, since nobody at this size has optimized for them.
5. **Measurement.** Nothing in the public record measures turn 4+ at this scale. Max needs his own multi-turn suite (with a loop/degeneracy metric, which Daimax already built and mutation-tested) before any claim of "breaking the wall" is testable, and must compare against LFM2.5-230M, Falcon-H1-Tiny-90M, SmolLM2-135M/360M and Qwen2.5-0.5B under one harness, because cross-harness numbers here disagree by 2x.

## Evidence about the "wall" (why chat quality degrades below ~400M)

1. **Second-turn collapse is measurable and steepens with smaller size** (GPT-4 judge, Danube3 report): turn-2/turn-1 ratio 0.58 (Danube3-500M), 0.73 (Qwen2-0.5B), 0.76 (Danube2-1.8B), 0.78 (Danube3-4B), 0.80 (Qwen1.5-4B), 0.81 (StableLM-3B), 0.90 (Phi3-4B). Scale: 0.5B to 4B, 2024 recipes. Whether this is capacity or single-turn-heavy SFT data is not separable from the published data.
2. **Three-turn instruction following still trails 1B models** (Liquid harness): Multi-IF 37.7 at 230M, 44.9 at 350M, vs 44.3 for Gemma 3 1B; Granite 350M 24-29. MultiChallenge 18.9 for Qwen3.5-0.8B vs 33.7 at 2B.
3. **Degeneration / loops**: SmolLM2-360M 4/33 loop rate vs 0/33 for Qwen2.5-0.5B (Max's Daimax); Gemma 3 270M infinite loops (HN, Willison); Qwen3.5-0.8B thinking "doom looping" (Liquid); TII: CoT traces "cause repetition loops" in tiny models; Max's MaxGPT-2 "Bitcoin.com" attractors. Vendors ship tiny models with low temperature and repetition penalties (LFM2.5: T=0.1, rep 1.05; Qwen3.5-0.8B: presence_penalty 2.0).
4. **Referent tracking by surface association**: Supra2-100M maps "Sure!" (to an offer about "pros and contras") onto contraception. Scale: 100M, 30B tokens.
5. **Parroting context as fact**: SmolLM2-360M claimed a retrieved identity ("Daimax-Pro") from its context (Max's Daimax). Small models over-weight copyable context.
6. **Knowledge capacity is parameter-bound**: ~2 bits of knowledge per parameter, "independent of int8 quantization", with a 7B holding more than English Wikipedia plus textbooks ([Physics of LMs 3.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05405), measured on synthetic biographies across sizes). A 150M model gets ~2% of a 7B's store. Observed: Gemma 3 270M "hallucinates wildly", MaxGPT-2 photosynthesis nonsense, Supra2's 2001 Pearl Harbor.
7. **Embedding tax** removes compute from the models that look big: Gemma 3 270M has 100M transformer params; Qwen3.5-0.8B has 498M of 752M.
8. **Undertraining explains most historical "walls"**: MaxGPT-2 (~18 tokens/param) and nanochat d20 (20) vs 8,800-83,000 for the strongest chat models in this census (and ~2,900 for MobileLLM-350M, the lowest-budget one with a respectable MT-Bench). The 2023-2024 impression that ~500M is the minimum was formed when 0.5B models were the ones given 4-18T tokens.
9. **Counter-evidence (the wall is not fundamental)**: TinyStories (<10M, coherent in a restricted domain); Falcon-H1-Tiny-90M beating SmolLM2-360M on MT-Bench in one harness; MobileLLM-350M beating OPT-1.3B in 2024; BabyLM storytelling feedback matching 100M tokens of pretraining on narrative cohesion and entity tracking with <1M interactive tokens (GPT-2-small scale).

## Open questions

1. How does any sub-400M model behave at turn 4-10 (reference resolution, instruction retention, contradiction rate)? No public data exists; this is the gap Max's project could fill first, cheaply, by evaluating existing models.
2. Is the MT-Bench turn-2 drop at 0.5B a capacity effect or an SFT-data effect (single-turn-heavy mixes)? A fixed-model SFT ablation with multi-turn-heavy data would separate them.
3. How much of LFM2.5-230M's Multi-IF comes from 19T tokens vs distillation from the 350M vs RL? Liquid published no ablation.
4. Does on-policy distillation fix multi-turn exposure bias the way it fixed reasoning in MobileLLM-R1.5? Unmeasured anywhere for chat.
5. Which judge did TII use for the Falcon-H1-Tiny MT-Bench numbers, and do they replicate? No independent replication found.
6. Can a 150M model at ~670 tokens/param (Max's 100B) plus Ultra distillation match SmolLM2-135M-Instruct (14,900 tokens/param, no distillation) on chat? Hobbyist data (PetitGPT at 13B tokens: IFEval 17 vs 22; GPT-X2.5/cagliostro at 75B close on base benchmarks) suggests "close", not proven.
7. Gemma 3 270M's official IFEval (51.2) is not reproducible by the community (20-28%); which harness details produce the gap, and do other vendor numbers in this census have similar gaps?
8. Token counts for ERNIE-4.5-0.3B, Hunyuan-0.5B and Qwen3.5-0.8B, and whether Gemma 3 270M was distilled, remain unsourced.
