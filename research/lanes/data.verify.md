# Verification: data lane (data.md)

Adversarial fact-check of `research/lanes/data.md`, 2026-09-23. This run replaces an earlier draft of this file that was written before the session was interrupted. Every load-bearing claim was re-checked in this run against the primary source: the TII blog HTML, arXiv PDFs, Hugging Face cards and config.json files, the datasets-server API, the Anthropic terms page, and Max's own logs (read only). Verdicts: confirmed, corrected (gist holds, but a number, scale, date or attribution is off), refuted, unverifiable.

## Short version

- None of the 12 load-bearing claims is refuted. Seven are confirmed as written. Five need corrections, and three of those change how a recommendation should read:
  1. **Anti-curriculum is an instruction-following lever, not a chat lever.** In TII's own post-DPO table, anti-curriculum wins IFEval (+12.6), LiveBench (+3.3), MMLU (+2.5) and MMLU-Pro (+5.2). It loses MT-Bench (4.33 vs 4.4), AlpacaEval (9.43 vs 10.44) and GSM8K (19.18 vs 20.24). MT-Bench is the table's only two-turn chat metric, and there it is a tie or a small loss. TII itself says anti-curriculum is better "but not with a huge margin". **DPO moved MT-Bench from about 3.1 to about 4.4 in both arms.** At 90M, the headline MT-Bench number comes from DPO, not from the data schedule.
  2. **Parrot's +0.41 to +0.47 MT-Bench includes CaPO** (a DPO-based method). The data-only gain is +0.18 to +0.24, and every model is 13B.
  3. **Instruction Pre-Training: Pythia-1B scores 47.1, not 45.7.** The 45.7 row is GPT-2 774M. The 500M model (46.6) is 0.5 below Pythia-1B. The controlled effect at 500M and equal tokens is +1.5 (45.1 to 46.6).
- The token budget needs a correction: 37.5k tok/s is the end-of-run value for the adopted recipe, and its run median was 29.3k. That gives **2.1 to 2.7B tokens per Titan-day at 150M**, so 100B costs about 37 to 48 Titan-days or about 21 5070-days. The "50 to 200B regime" conclusion holds.
- One attribution is wrong in the lane's own anecdote: `WRITEUP_NOTES.md` documents the "Bitcoin.com" loops and the photosynthesis nonsense for **MaxGPT-2 (110M, TinyStories + OASST)**, not MaxGPT-3 (235M, chat-heavy mix). MaxGPT-2 never saw UltraChat, so the notes' own "spam in UltraChat" explanation does not fit either.
- The biggest omissions for the premise: **LFM2.5-350M** (Multi-IF 44.92, a 3-turn instruction benchmark, vs 41.68 for Qwen3.5-0.8B-Instruct; 28T tokens plus RL) and **ufakzeka-1** (151M chat model, Sep 18 2026). In ufakzeka-1, identity tracking over a long story and multi-turn arithmetic did not move under any data change the authors tried. That is weak evidence, but it is at Max's exact size, and it cuts against "the multi-turn wall is data". **TurnWise** (Mar 2026, 7B) is the main missing multi-turn data pipeline.

## 3. Scale misapplications

1. **Parrot (13B only) is the main evidence behind lever 3**, the "candidate new technique", and the reported gain includes CaPO. Data alone gives +0.18 to +0.24 at 13B. No published result shows that back-reference-dense data moves multi-turn ability at 150M. ufakzeka-1 (151M) weakly suggests some multi-turn tracking did not move with data at this size.
2. **BeyondWeb conversational upsampling** (Llama-3.2-1B, 20B tokens, 5-shot benchmarks, "conversational" = FAQ / Q&A forum / transcripts) is used to set a 15 to 20% chat share for a 150M chat model. It measures neither chat quality nor 150M. **[>=1B only]**
3. **MiniCPM decay-with-SFT** (1.2B, 2.4B) backs lever 8 as "+several points". At 1.2B the effect was already mixed, and MiniCPM still ran a full SFT afterwards. **[>=1B only]**
4. **Meena R^2 = 0.93** (2.6B, social chit-chat) is the mechanism argument in section 3 ("conversational quality follows loss on conversational text") for a 150M assistant-style model. **[>=1B only]**
5. **Physics of LMs 3.3 junk-data result** is applied to real web text and to "knowledge-light" pretraining. In the paper, junk means random rare facts, and repetitive junk is harmless. This is a domain misapplication more than a scale one (synthetic biographies, small-to-mid GPT-2 models).
6. **Instruction Pre-Training** (500M and 1.3B) is presented as "500M matches Pythia-1B". It supports only "+1.5 on a 9-task average at 500M from instruction-formatted pretraining data".
7. **Falcon-H1-Tiny's 800GT result** is the headline, but the evidence closest to Max's 50 to 200B regime is TII's 100GT ablation, where 25% and 50% tied and no numbers are published. The MT-Bench 4.33 at 90M is mostly a DPO effect, measured the same way in both arms.
8. **Rephraser-size saturation at 3B** (BeyondWeb: web rephrasing for 1B to 8B students) is carried over to choosing a teacher that writes multi-turn dialogue. That is a different task, and DeepDialogue shows 4 to 8B generators failing past 6 turns. The lane partly hedges this.
9. **DCLM's OH-2.5 + ELI5 classifier** (1B and 7B, CORE benchmark) is read as "the best filter selects conversational register". Reasonable, but measured on benchmarks at 1B+, not on chat. **[>=1B only]**
10. **The memorization window at 100M** comes from a single TII study (constant LR, one domain, one scale) plus a linear scale-down of a FalconMamba-7B estimate. The lane's "30 passes every 3.3B tokens is safe" rests on that one lab.
11. **Small Model Learnability Gap** (math reasoning, 0.5B to 3B+ students) is used for chat register and teacher size at 150M (lever 5, open question 5). Different domain; the scale is close but above 150M.

## 4. Omissions

1. **LFM2.5-350M (LiquidAI, 2026-03-31).** 354M, 10 conv + 6 GQA layers, pretraining extended from 10T to **28T tokens**, plus multi-stage RL. Vendor: IFEval 76.96, IFBench 40.69, **Multi-IF 44.92**, vs Qwen3.5-0.8B-Instruct 59.94 / 22.87 / 41.68 and Gemma 3 1B IT 63.49 / 20.33 / 44.25. This is the strongest current premise data point: a 350M model at or above 0.8B to 1B models on a 3-turn instruction benchmark. It was bought with about 80,000 tokens per parameter plus RL, far outside Max's regime. It contradicts the lane's "nobody has measured sub-400M multi-turn" for shallow multi-turn. License lfm1.0, not Apache. [card](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-350M)
2. **IBM Granite-4.0-350M and Granite-4.0-H-350M (Oct 2025, Apache-2.0).** Sub-400M instruct models missing from the premise table; Multi-IF 24.21 and 28.70 in Liquid's table. [card](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-4.0-350m)
3. **ufakzeka-1 (arXiv 2609.25081, 2026-09-18).** A 151M (182M with embeddings) Turkish chat model: 13.5B pretraining tokens (about 90 per parameter), about $286 total. Across the last eight data rounds, "identity tracking over a long story" and a multi-turn arithmetic recompute path "did not move with data". The authors read this as a 151M limit but say they "cannot rule out a data change we did not try". Seed variance matched the whole recipe spread (helpfulness 75.2 / 80.2 / 81.2 over three seeds vs 75 to 82 across recipes). A data-repaired gate read 64/64 on its own prompts vs 34/64 on held-out paraphrases. Takeaways for Max: 3 seeds per data ablation at 150M, and held-out paraphrase probes. It is the only published multi-turn chat measurement at his size. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25081)
4. **TurnWise (arXiv 2603.16759, Mar 2026).** A multi-turn benchmark (TurnWiseEval), scored pairwise against the equivalent single-turn setting, plus a synthetic multi-turn data pipeline (TurnWiseData). Its user turns are generated independently from seeds and then stacked, which avoids drift and long contexts. On Olmo 3 7B, adding 10k multi-turn conversations in post-training gave up to 12% on TurnWiseEval. This is the most direct 2026 evidence for lever 3 and a ready generation recipe. **[>=1B only]** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16759)
5. **UserLM-8b / "Flipping the Dialogue" (arXiv 2510.06552, Microsoft).** A user simulator post-trained on real user turns. Finds that better assistants make *worse* user simulators; GPT-4o drops from 74.6% to 57.4% against realistic simulated users. This attacks the lane's own diagnosis (information-complete synthetic users) and fits on the Mac at 4-bit. The card says MIT, but the base is Llama-3.1-8B, so check the Llama terms for derived training data. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06552), [card](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/UserLM-8b)
6. **realsim (arXiv 2605.02624, May 2026)** finds simulated users miss the "communication frictions" real users introduce. **ConsistentChat (arXiv 2506.03558)** does skeleton-guided multi-turn generation over nine intent trajectories (15k conversations) and reports a 20 to 30% gain in chat consistency; I did not check its model scale. Both bear on how to generate the back-reference set.
7. **Nemotron-Pretraining-SFT-v1 (NVIDIA, Aug 2025).** SFT-style data built for use *inside pretraining*; the card lists a General SFT subset of 87.5B tokens. It is a ready-made anti-curriculum source for a 50 to 200B run. Gated, NVIDIA Open Data License; synthetic parts carry third-party model terms. [card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Nemotron-Pretraining-SFT-v1)
8. **SmolTalk2 (HF, Jul 2025).** Successor sources for smol-smoltalk, with per-subset turn and length statistics. It adds a Multi-Turn IF split (28,217 rows, Multi-IF recipe) and 244,736 everyday conversations regenerated with Qwen3-32B. Caveat: the multi-turn IF split is think-mode, about 3,460 tokens per example, so it is too long for a 150M student as is. [card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceTB/smoltalk2)
9. **Existing multi-turn metrics.** Multi-IF (arXiv 2410.15553, 3 turns) is already reported for 350M models, and MT-Bench-101 (arXiv 2402.14762) exists. Both are shallow, but they are free comparison points before building the custom back-reference probe in section 2.3.
10. **TinyHelen / Leaner datasets (arXiv 2501.00522).** A simplified-language pretraining set (71M) and instruct set (7M) for tiny LMs; its genre-preserving simplification includes conversation. Direct tiny-scale evidence for lever 5 (match register to capacity). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00522)
11. **Inside the lane's own source:** the DPO effect at 90M. MT-Bench rose from about 3.1 to about 4.4 in both TII arms, and IFEval by 13 to 16 points. The lane credits the data schedule for numbers that DPO mostly produced. The post-training lane should own this, but the data lane should not claim it.
