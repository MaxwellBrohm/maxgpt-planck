## Scale misapplications

1. **Zoology's recall result is stretched to generative multi-turn recall at 150M.** Zoology measures next-token perplexity on repeated n-grams (copying) in Pile text at 70M-1.4B, attention vs gated convolutions. It does not measure answering a paraphrased question about a fact stated several turns earlier, with same-type distractors, in a chat template. "Remembering user-supplied facts is not expected to be the binding constraint at 150M" is a hypothesis, not a finding. It also cuts against two recommended baselines: LFM2/LFM2.5 (10 or 8 of their blocks are gated convolutions) and Falcon-H1-Tiny (Mamba hybrid) belong to the architecture class Zoology found weak at in-context recall.
2. **2 bits/param is a best-case ceiling from synthetic biographies** at 1000 exposures with no junk data. The lane uses it as the working capacity for a 150M model trained on web text; Physics of LMs 3.3 itself reports 1 bit/param at 100 exposures and up to 20x loss with junk data. This strengthens the lane's "knowledge is the limit" point, but the number should be quoted as an upper bound.
3. **Judge-agreement figures are borrowed from strong-model outputs.** MT-Bench's 85% (6 models, 13B to GPT-4) and MT-Bench-101's 87% (6B+ outputs) say nothing about how a judge ranks 90M-500M outputs, where many answers are near the floor. The lane flags this in open question 5, but the D4 judge plan still cites Prometheus 2 and Selene claims measured on frontier outputs.
4. **IFBench's "overfit to IFEval" finding is measured at 7B+ and frontier.** Using it to discount tiny-model IFEval is reasonable caution, but at 350M the one same-harness data point cuts the other way: LFM2.5-350M's IFBench (40.69) is 53% of its IFEval, a higher ratio than Qwen3.5-0.8B's 38%. Sub-400M overfitting to IFEval is plausible but unmeasured.
5. **MultiChallenge scores are compared across rating schemes.** "Frontier best 41.4%" is human-rated on the paper's set; Qwen3.5-0.8B's 18.9 is Qwen's auto-judged number. GPT-4o's 12.5% in the paper is below the 0.8B's number, which shows the scales differ, not that the 0.8B beats GPT-4o.
6. **LiC recap (+16 to +17.5) and TurnWise (+6.2) are GPT-4o-class and 7B results** that the lane labels correctly as unmeasured at 150M. No further misapplication there.
7. **MT-Bench-101's "RLHF/DPO gave no obvious multi-turn gains"** is 7B-20B InternLM and Mistral pairs (+0.16, +0.10, -0.06). The lane labels it 6B+; fine, but it sits next to Falcon-H1-Tiny's DPO gains at 90M (which are on a single-turn-dominated TII metric suite), and the two should not be read as conflicting evidence about the same thing.

## Omissions

1. **LFM2.5-230M** (Liquid, 2026-06-24; 229.7M params; 14 layers, 8 gated-conv + 6 GQA; 19T tokens, about 83k tokens/param; distilled from LFM2.5-350M plus multi-stage RL): IFEval 71.71, IFBench 38.40, Multi-IF 37.70. It is the only sub-300M model found with a published 3-turn number, it is newer than every model in the lane's table, and it should be a required baseline and a row in A1. https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-230M
2. **Falcon-H1-Tiny-90M-Instruct-Curriculum** (released alongside the Instruct model) beats it on TII's judged chat metrics (MT Bench 4.4 vs 4.33, AlpacaEval 10.44 vs 9.43). "The model to beat at this size" should be both checkpoints, and the "SFT-in-pretraining" lever's evidence is IFEval-specific, not chat-general. https://tiiuae-tiny-h1-blogpost.hf.space/
3. **Pre-LLM small open-domain chatbots with real multi-turn human evaluation.** BlenderBot BST 90M in 14-turn human-bot chats (engagingness preferred over Meena 2.6B, 61 to 39, p < 0.05) and ConvAI2's 4-6 turn human evaluation of PersonaChat models. These are the closest published evidence against "nothing under 300M has been measured in conversation", and the lane's premise check should cite them, with the caveat that they are chit-chat models rated for engagingness, not instruction followers rated for coherence. The Blender paper also names the exact failure modes this battery targets (contradiction, repetition, hallucinated knowledge) and warns that 14 turns is short. https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.13637 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.00098
4. **Degeneration evidence at 0.8B from a vendor**: Liquid's LFM2.5 cards report that Qwen3.5-0.8B (Thinking) MMLU-Pro "could not be completed due to doom looping". This is independent support for the lane's point that looping is a small-model failure mode, and it is from a 2026 model. https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-350M
5. **More harness-gap data the lane could use**: Gemma-3-1B-IT Multi-IF 32.8 (Qwen) vs 44.25 (Liquid), IFEval 54.5 strict (Qwen) vs 63.49 (Liquid); LFM2-350M's own numbers shift by up to 1.8 points between Liquid's two reports. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09388 Table 20 ; https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-350M
6. **MT-Bench-101's own warning about golden context**: the paper shows golden history produces an "illusory improvement" over turns because models copy the style of the reference turns, which is why it takes the minimum over turns. The lane adopts golden vs own history but should also report min-over-turns (or first-failure turn) for F1-F7, not only pass rates. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14762 (Sec. 4.2, 4.5)
7. **Statistical design**: the -3 margin needs a power calculation, not just a CI width (see row 12). This is an omission in D6/D8 rather than a missing paper.

## Resume-pass additions (WebSearch available)

Six targeted searches (multi-turn benchmarks for sub-500M models, SmolLM multi-turn coherence, 2026 multi-turn instruction-following degradation, new sub-300M instruct releases, human evaluation of sub-billion chat, conversational-quality judges) turned up no 2026 multi-turn coherence evaluation of any sub-300M instruction model. The narrowed version of claim 1 (no programmatic recall, reference or instruction-persistence measurement beyond 3 turns below 300M) still stands. What they did add:

1. **Floor-effect risk for F4, from programmatic multi-turn tests at 4B-32B.** "Quantifying Conversational Reliability of LLMs under Multi-Turn Interaction" (arXiv 2603.01423, Mar 2026) uses pass/fail tasks close to F2 and F4 (global constraints kept across topic shifts, tool choice amid interleaved intents, entity tracking under revisions). Multi-turn instruction following dropped from single-turn levels of 84 to 32 (Qwen3-4B), 83 to 27 (Qwen3-8B), 92 to 33 (Gemma-3-12B) and 96 to 63 (GPT-4o), McNemar-significant. Smallest model: Qwen3-4B. If 4B-12B models pass about 30%, Qwen2.5-0.5B may be near 0% on F4, and a -3 point non-inferiority margin on a floored family proves nothing. D8 should require that the bar model shows headroom on a family (for example at least 20% pass) before that family counts toward MTC, or report floored families separately. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01423 (Table 1)
2. **LLM judges are unreliable on realistic multi-turn dialogue even at human quality.** UPHELD (arXiv 2608.26131, Aug 2026): 36,000+ expert per-turn annotations over 30,000+ turns written by professional script writers. Classical metrics and single reference-free LLM judges correlate weakly to moderately with expert ratings; a mixture of judges improved correlation by about 30%. Measured on predicted next turns from GPT-3.5, GPT-4o, GPT-5, Llama-3.x-70B, Kimi-K2.5, Qwen3-32B and DeepSeek v3 inside human-written dialogues, not on tiny-model output. This supports D4's rule that a judge must pass validation and that the final claim needs a blind human test, and it argues against one local 8B-14B judge as the only open-ended signal. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26131
3. **Distillation confound in the token-budget story (claim 9 and "Budget reality").** Gemma 3 (all sizes, knowledge distillation in pretraining), LFM2 (Top-K knowledge distillation), LFM2.5-230M (distilled from LFM2.5-350M) and Qwen3-0.6B (strong-to-weak distillation) all had a teacher. The lane's "too few tokens vs a real wall" dichotomy should be "too few tokens, no teacher, or a real wall". Tier 0 curves over tokens cannot separate the teacher effect. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19786 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23404 ; https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-230M ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09388
4. **Qwen documents looping at 0.8B itself.** The Qwen3.5-0.8B card warns that in thinking mode it "is more prone to entering thinking loops compared to other Qwen3.5 models", and its thinking-mode IFEval (44.0) is below non-thinking (52.1).
