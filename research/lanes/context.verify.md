# Verification: lane "context" (multi-turn coherence mechanics and external memory)

Checked 2026-09-23 against primary sources (arXiv html/pdf, official model cards, config.json, the TII blog source, Max's own BENCHMARK.md). Verdicts: confirmed, corrected, refuted, unverifiable. Scale is stated for every correction. After a machine shutdown, the decisive verdicts were re-checked against the sources (Titans Table 2 raw HTML, LFM2 Table 6, TII blog tables, arXiv 2505.13514 withdrawal, arXiv 2608.18083 sec 4.3) and all held.

Headline: 12 of 17 load-bearing claims hold as written or with small caveats. Three matter for decisions:

1. **The "no multi-turn benchmark exists below 1B" claim is wrong.** LFM2's own technical report runs Multi-IF (a multi-turn, 3-turn instruction-following benchmark) on LFM2-350M: 32.85, against Qwen3-0.6B 45.13 in the same harness. LFM2-350M *beats* Qwen3-0.6B on IFEval (65.12 vs 64.24) but *trails* it by 12.3 points on Multi-IF. That is a measured multi-turn gap between 350M and 600M, which is exactly the wall Max described, and it is already published.
2. **The Titans "340M Transformer++ scores 98% on single NIAH" numbers belong to TTT, a recurrent model.** Titans' NIAH table has no Transformer++ row at all. The claim that single-fact recall is "near-solved at small scale" has no transformer evidence from that source.
3. **Falcon's anti-curriculum win is IFEval-only.** On MT-Bench, the chat metric, the curriculum model scored slightly *higher* (4.4 vs 4.33), and AlpacaEval also favored curriculum. The multilingual 100M variant got "no clear boost". The induction-head-dominance paper used as the loop mechanism was **withdrawn** in Dec 2025.

## 2. Scale (and task) misapplications

1. **Titans NIAH numbers are used as transformer evidence at 340M.** They are TTT (recurrent) passkey numbers at an unstated size. The lane's skill ladder rung "single fact recall inside the window: near-solved (98% at 340M/15B tokens)" has no support from this source.
2. **Entity tracking "human level at 410M" is used as a chat-relevant threshold.** It is a likelihood read-out. In generation, OLMo 2-1B-Instruct scores 55.3% (human 75.3%) and 13B-Instruct 65.6%. The "crosses at ~410M" rung and open question 1 should say "for likelihood scoring; generated answers stay below human level through 13B". The "data moves the threshold" point rests on OLMo 2, which only exists at 1B and above.
3. **The 2-hop failure at 1.3B/100B is presented as a parameter wall.** The same paper shows a GPT-2-small-sized model doing 4-hop in-context retrieval on ideal synthetic data. The binding constraint is data at academic pretraining scale, not capacity. That makes it a data lever at 150M, not a size ceiling.
4. **The induction-head descaling / "induction dominance" lever** comes from a withdrawn paper whose measurements are 1.5B-9B only.
5. **The anti-curriculum lever is labelled evidence for multi-turn chat** ("Falcon 90M +24% IFEval"). It was measured at 90M on single-turn IFEval. The chat metric (MT-Bench, whose citation is multi-turn) went slightly the other way.
6. **TurnWise +12.8 (7B, post-training) is used to support mixing multi-turn chat into pretraining at 150M.** It is a 7B SFT result, and it came with IFEval -4.6 and MMLU -5.3.
7. **Depth-vs-width "Strong at 125-350M" is applied to state tracking, instruction following and multi-hop.** At 125-350M, MobileLLM measured zero-shot commonsense, QA and reading comprehension. Physics 2.1 is synthetic math, and TinyStories is anecdotal at 1-8 layers. It is a task transfer, not a measured chat result.
8. **Daimax "every sub-500M model called zero tools" is used to show tool non-use is a small-model trait.** Six models of 0.8B to 3B also called zero tools. The data points to training and dialect, not size.
9. Already flagged by the lane, and correctly: Laban (8B and up), MemGPT (70B and up), recap strategies (8B and up), code-for-entity-tracking (7B and up), gated-attention long-context gains (1.7B), Pipis (1.5B and up).

## 3. Omissions

1. **Sub-1B multi-turn benchmark results exist** (this reverses the lane's central "unmeasured" point):
   - LFM2 report, Multi-IF: LFM2-350M 32.85, Qwen3-0.6B 45.13, Gemma-3-1B 44.49, Llama-3.2-1B 29.43, all in one harness ([arXiv 2511.23404](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.23404v1) Table 6). Single-turn IFEval ranks LFM2-350M above Qwen3-0.6B, and Multi-IF reverses that by 12 points. This is the cleanest published evidence that a sub-400M model's single-turn score overstates its multi-turn ability.
   - Qwen3 report: Qwen3-0.6B Multi-IF 33.3 (non-thinking) and 36.1 (thinking), Arena-Hard 6.5 ([arXiv 2505.09388](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09388v1) Tables 19-20).
   - TII: MT-Bench (cited as MT-Bench-101) for 90M, 135M, 270M and 360M. The original MT-Bench numbers on the SmolLM2 cards are 2-turn.
2. **Falcon's 100GT SFT-fraction sweep** (0/25/50/75/100% SFT, 2GT SFT mix). 25-50% was best, mostly-SFT mixes were worse, and a follow-up SFT stage added nothing ([TII blog](https://tiiuae-tiny-h1-blogpost.hf.space/), "Anti-curriculum model: final recipe"). This runs at Max's own 100B-token budget.
3. **Instruction Pre-Training** (Cheng et al. 2024): trained from scratch at 500M and 1.3B on about 100B tokens, with 1/5 of the raw texts converted into synthesized instruction-response pairs. Their 500M model on 100B tokens "reaches performance of the 1B model pre-trained on 300B tokens" and gains more from later instruction tuning ([arXiv 2406.14491](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14491)). This is primary evidence for instruction data in pretraining at Max's scale and token budget.
4. **RETRO reproduction** (Wang et al. 2023): 148M to 9.5B trained from scratch. RETRO generates "with considerably less repetition" than GPT at equal fluency and coherence, and its downstream gains are concentrated on knowledge-intensive tasks ([arXiv 2304.06762](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06762)). That makes retrieval pretraining relevant to the loop problem as well as to knowledge.
5. **Distillation Scaling Laws** (Busbridge et al. 2025): students and teachers from 143M to 12.6B. Distillation beats supervised pretraining only up to a compute level that scales with student size, and only when the teacher already exists ([arXiv 2502.08606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08606)). This bears directly on the lane's MiniLLM/on-policy distillation lever, with the 1.1B Ultra as a possible teacher.
6. **Small Models Struggle to Learn from Strong Reasoners** (Li et al. 2025): Qwen2.5-0.5B scores 19.5 after short-CoT fine-tuning vs 14.8 after long-CoT, and mixed distillation helps ([arXiv 2502.12143](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12143)). It independently supports Falcon's "keep long CoT out of tiny models" finding at 0.5B.
7. **The withdrawal of arXiv 2505.13514** should itself be recorded, so the loop-mechanism section does not cite it as settled.
