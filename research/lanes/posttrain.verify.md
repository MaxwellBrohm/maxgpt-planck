# Verification: posttrain lane (post-training a tiny multi-turn chat model)

Checked 2026-09-23 against primary sources: raw model cards (SmolLM2-360M-Instruct, LFM2.5-230M, LFM2.5-350M, unsloth/gemma-3-270m-it, the MobileLLM-R1.5-140M page), config.json files for the LFM2 and LFM2.5 families, the LFM2 report HTML (2511.23404v1), the Falcon-H1-Tiny blog HTML, the Falcon-H1 report (2507.22448, eval harness table), evalchemy's MTBench source, arXiv PDFs or HTML for GKD, MiniLLM, Qwen3, SMOPD, Found in Conversation, Rethinking OPD, Distillation Scaling Laws, MiniMA capacity gap, OXRL, KTO, MobileLLM-R1, the looping paper, "Don't Say That!" (unlikelihood), BlenderBot, DistiLLM, the GOLD space page, the SmolLM3 playbook, the TinyZero README, the nanochat discussion via the GitHub API, the Thinking Machines blog, the alignment-handbook SmolLM2 configs, the ICLR 2026 awards post, and Max's local files (read-only).

Two passes. The first pass ran in the afternoon with WebSearch exhausted. After the session was interrupted, a second pass re-fetched the load-bearing sources (all 17 claims' primary numbers, the TII table and its harness, the Thinking Machines table, SMOPD's abstract, the looping paper's tables, the GOLD page, the playbook quotes, the Daimax table) and reproduced every first-pass verdict. It then used WebSearch, now available, to close open items and search 2026 work. Changes from pass 2 are marked "(pass 2)".

Summary: the numbers are almost all right. Every figure traced to its source except four: the Thinking Machines IFEval figure (45% is the wrong row), SMOPD's "neutral at 1.7B" (a different variant), TII's "MT-Bench-101" label (TII's own harness table says 2-turn MT-Bench through evalchemy), and (pass 2) the claim that LFM2.5 teachers up to 2.6B share the 65,536 vocabulary (LFM2.5-2.6B uses 128,000). The bigger problems are framing. (a) Several levers rest on evidence from other settings: reasoning-model loops, a pruning-based capacity-gap law, math-only preference studies, and tool-calling multi-turn scores. (b) Some effect sizes come from the most favorable scale (MiniLLM +6.1 at 120M shrinks to +0.3 at 340M). (c) The lane missed MobileLLM-R1.5, the one published sub-400M on-policy distillation result (140M and 360M, released Nov 2025), and (pass 2) Guided-OPD (Jun 2026), which measured multi-turn on-policy distillation on a 0.6B student. That breaks the lane's "multi-turn OPD only at >=1.7B" line, though in agent tasks rather than chat.

- **Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct post-training, OLMo-1B >2% worse at 3T vs 2.3T, Instruction Pre-Training 500M/100B matching 1B/300B: confirmed** (first pass). Sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.15115 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19206 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14491
- **Lost in Conversation (-39%, aptitude -15%, unreliability +112%, CONCAT 95.1%, smaller models 86-92): confirmed.** (pass 2) The "ICLR 2026 outstanding paper" label is now **confirmed**: the ICLR blog lists "LLMs Get Lost In Multi-Turn Conversation" (Laban, Hayashi, Zhou, Neville) as one of two Outstanding Papers. Sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.06120 ; https://blog.iclr.cc/2026/04/23/announcing-the-iclr-2026-outstanding-papers/
- **MT-Bench-101 golden history and "neither ... alignment techniques nor chat-specific designs": confirmed** (first pass). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14762

## Scale misapplications

1. **The teacher-size "sweet spot" (2.5x) is carried from a different kind of distillation.** MiniMA's law was fitted on students *pruned from* GPT-2/Pythia teachers and logit-distilled on ~3.9B pretraining tokens. The lane applies it to post-training on-policy distillation from an independently trained teacher. The distillation-scaling-laws paper it also cites frames the capacity gap as a gap in learning capacity and teacher cross-entropy, not only relative size, and shows weaker students gain most. Direct counterexamples at Max's scale: GKD's 77M student gained from a 3B teacher (38x), and MobileLLM-R1.5 gained at 28x and 11x. The 7x worry about Ultra is not supported. Ultra's teacher quality (1.1B, 100B tokens, same data as the student) is the real open risk.
2. **Loop evidence comes from long chain-of-thought reasoning models, 1.5B-32B, on AIME problems at greedy or low temperature, with a 30-gram-repeated-20-times criterion.** It is applied to 150M chat replies at T=0.7 measured by a repeated-5-gram fraction. The same paper says instruction-tuned counterparts (Qwen2.5, Phi-4) barely loop. The "errors in learning" mechanism may transfer; the 76% / 37% / 30% / 4% rates do not. The Daimax 4/33 vs 0/33 comparison is not significant (p = 0.11) and comes from correlated repeats.
3. **Multi-turn *conversational* training levers (SMOPD, FiC, MAIGO, RLAAR, CollabLLM) exist only at 1.7B-14B.** SMOPD's effect (1.0-2.5 pp, single seed, LoRA) is the size of its own run-to-run noise. The lever table still gives "+1-2.5 pp" as if it were a stable effect. The only sub-1B multi-turn OPD evidence (Guided-OPD at 0.6B) is agentic.
4. **OXRL's "loss ~1 pp" hierarchy was measured on GSM8K with GSM8K self-play pairs** (Qwen2.5-Instruct 0.5B-7B). It is used as guidance for chat preference tuning at 150M. The same paper finds all algorithms within 0.47 pp on general benchmarks, a null result for general behavior, not a ranking.
5. **Qwen3's "OPD at 1/10 the RL compute" (8B, math and code) and Thinking Machines (8B) are used to size a 150M plan.** The lane marks the scale in-line, but the lever table's "compute trivial" rests on them plus an unmeasured sampler.
6. **GKD's sub-1B evidence comes from T5 encoder-decoders on single-turn summarization, translation and arithmetic.** MiniLLM's +6.1 at 120M is the most favorable size: +0.3 at 340M, +4.0 at 760M. Both are single-turn. The lever table's "+6.1 GPT-4 score (+16% relative)" should carry the 340M counterexample.
7. **Unlikelihood and BlenderBot results come from ~90M encoder-decoders with persona contexts** and greedy or length-controlled beam decoding (2019-2020). They are applied to a decoder-only 150M instruction model.
8. **The anti-curriculum gain at 90M is an IFEval gain.** On TII's chat metrics (MT Bench, AlpacaEval) the classic arm was slightly ahead after DPO. Recommendation 1 and the lever table present it as a chat-skills lever.
9. **TII's BFCL multi-turn function-calling note is used as evidence about multi-turn chat** (section 1), and SmolLM3's reasoning-mode switching (3B) is generalized to "single-turn data does not produce multi-turn behavior".

## Omissions

1. **MobileLLM-R1.5 (Meta, released Nov 2025): the one sub-400M on-policy distillation result.** "Semi on-policy KD": the student regenerates the 6.2M reasoning SFT prompts (temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95), then trains with forward KL for 4 epochs against Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-4B-v1.1, ~6.5 days on 16x8 GPUs. Gains: 140M, MATH500 6.2 to 16 and GSM8K 4.1 to 8.3 (LiveCodeBench slipped 1.7 to 1.5); 360M, 28.4 to 63.4 and 24.5 to 52.8. Meta: "particularly effective when applied as a final stage in the post-training pipeline for small models". (pass 2) Date correction: the first pass called it 2026; the HF repos were created 2025-11-21. Tokenizer note that supports the lane's tokenizer point: MobileLLM-R1 uses the Llama 3.2 128k vocabulary and the teacher's is 128,256, so this is same-family-tokenizer KD, and a 128k vocabulary at 140M is a design Max's 49k tokenizer avoids. Math and code, not chat. https://huggingface.co/facebook/MobileLLM-R1.5-140M ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.24945 (tokenizer line)
2. **(pass 2) Guided-OPD (Jun 2026): multi-turn OPD on a 0.6B student, and the mechanism the lane's open question 3 asks about.** Under vanilla multi-turn OPD, teacher perplexity on student tokens "grows with the turn index and remains high even after 200 steps": small student errors compound across turns and leave the teacher's familiar distribution, so teacher signal is least reliable where the student needs it. Mixing teacher-written turns into rollouts on a schedule that decays to zero fixed much of it, with gains largest on the smallest student (+35.3% relative Score at 0.6B). Agentic tasks, 30B-A3B teacher. For Max this is a direct design input: early multi-turn OPD rollouts should interleave teacher turns. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15912
3. **(pass 2) MAIGO (May 2026, 7B): history-cleaned on-policy self-distillation for Lost-in-Conversation.** It removes prior assistant replies from the reference context for middle turns ("self-contamination"), raising Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct sharded accuracy 52.8 to 66.1. It is the closest published method to the lane's "dirty history" diagnosis, >=1B only. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27186
4. **(pass 2) ReOPD (Jul 2026): the "prefix trap" in multi-turn OPD.** Making histories more on-policy for the student queries the teacher where its targets are unreliable; a step-decaying prefix schedule favoring early turns kept OPD accuracy at 4x lower rollout cost. Agentic tasks; student sizes not checked in this pass. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04763
5. **DistiLLM (ICML 2024): skew-KL plus adaptive off-policy reuse of student-generated outputs**, tested with a GPT-2 0.1B student and GPT-2 XL teacher, "up to 4.3x speedup" over recent KD methods including MiniLLM. A cheaper route to on-policy benefits for spare Titans. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03898
6. **Distillation Scaling Laws' positive finding for small students** ("weaker students benefit more from distillation"). The lane cites the paper only for the capacity gap. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08606
7. **DialoGPT (2019): decoder-only 117M/345M/762M models trained on 147M Reddit exchanges**, near-human in single-turn human evaluation, with MMI reranking against blandness. The decoder-only counterpart to the BlenderBot premise check, and what Max would actually build. https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.00536
8. **TII's own tool-calling finding** that "CoT is toxic for tool calling models at this scale", causing repetition loops. It is the closest-scale (90M) evidence for the lane's "skip long CoT" advice, and the skip list does not cite it. Falcon-H1-Tiny blog, tool-calling "Key Findings".
9. **The Falcon-H1 report's harness table** (evalchemy MTBench, "turn 1 & 2 average"), which identifies what TII's MT-Bench number measures. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22448
10. **Self-Distillation Fine-Tuning (SDFT)**: the model rewrites SFT targets into its own distribution before fine-tuning, which cuts forgetting. A cheap bridge between off-policy SFT and OPD aimed at the "learnability gap", measured on Llama-2-chat 7B only (>=1B). https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13669
11. **(pass 2) LFM2.5-230M-Base (Jun 2026) is released.** An open 230M base with the 65,536 vocabulary is a possible initialization or same-tokenizer sibling if Max ever adopts that tokenizer. https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-230M-Base
12. Coverage note. Pass 2 WebSearch and HF org listings (HuggingFaceTB, LiquidAI, tiiuae, PleIAs, google, facebook, Qwen, allenai, ibm-granite, microsoft, nvidia; releases since mid-May 2026) found no new sub-400M general chat model after LFM2.5-230M (Jun 2026). Liquid's July-August releases are 1.2B+ or encoders. The 2026 multi-turn OPD literature is almost entirely agentic or reasoning; none has a sub-1B chat student.
