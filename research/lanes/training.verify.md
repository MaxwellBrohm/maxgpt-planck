## 3. Scale misapplications

1. Busbridge's distillation law (students >= 143M non-embedding, <= 512B tokens, unrepeated C4) is evaluated at 120M and up to 1T tokens, and its outputs (-0.02 to -0.04 nats token-matched, -0.01 to -0.02 compute-matched) are placed in the levers table as "expected effect at ~150M" without the extrapolation flag that section 2.7 carries. Its supervised half is run to 18T for the Qwen-like row, 35x past the fit.
2. Wen et al.'s optimizer rankings (<= 8x Chinchilla, a few 16x runs, i.e. <= ~320 tokens/param) are used to predict SOAP/Kron competitiveness "at the 100x+ Chinchilla ratios a chat model trains at". Unmeasured regime.
3. Du et al.'s loss-threshold result (>= 300M, fixed corpus and tokenizer) is carried both down to 150M and across to a different data distribution: two stacked extrapolations (claim 14).
4. Laban et al.'s "multi-turn loss is unreliability, not aptitude" (frontier models) is used as the mechanism by which on-policy distillation would fix multi-turn drift at 150M. No sub-1B multi-turn evidence is cited. MiniLLM's 120M result is single-turn.
5. Qwen3's on-policy-distillation-beats-RL ablation (Qwen3-8B) is flagged [>=1B only] in the text but still anchors the "distill where it pays" recommendation. A 2026 study of OPD mechanics (2604.13016, 1.5B/7B) finds OPD fails unless the teacher offers capabilities the student has not already seen and shares its "thinking patterns", which matters when the teacher (Ultra) was trained on the same corpus as the student.
6. Busbridge's top-k=128 plus lambda=0.7 result (pretraining, 198M-1.82B students) is presented as "within 0.01 nats of full KD" for SFT-stage KD at 150M. Stage and scale both differ.
7. Minitron's "prune a model closest to the target size" (4B-15B) is used to argue against Ultra-to-150M pruning. Directionally reasonable; no sub-1B evidence.
8. Allen-Zhu and Li's capacity law is scale-free but domain-specific (uniformly random synthetic biographies). Treating open-domain chat knowledge as the same kind of bits is a domain extrapolation.
9. Max's own A/B (113M at ~9 tokens/param) is correctly flagged as the wrong regime, but the lane then compares its noisy val numbers to IMU-1's train-loss ablation as if both were precise, and misreads which IMU-1 number is the optimizer-only effect (claim 12).

## 4. Omissions

1. **Falcon-H1-Tiny-90M-Instruct (TII, 2026-01-12).** 90M hybrid Transformer+Mamba (config.json: hidden 512, 24 layers, vocab 32,768, tied embeddings), pretrained on 800B tokens (~8.9k tokens/param). Headline training result: "SFT-pretraining" with 25% SFT data in the pretraining mix and ">=100 repetitions of SFT sources like Tulu3" under a "memorization-aware" repetition rule. In TII's harness: IFEval 66.08, MT-Bench 4.33, AlpacaEval 9.43, vs SmolLM2-135M-Instruct 30.69 / 2.68 / 1.52, SmolLM2-360M-Instruct 38.78 / 3.80 / 2.15, Gemma3-270m-it 27.44 / 3.71 / 4.65. Its classic-curriculum sibling reaches MT-Bench 4.40 at 90M. Caveats: vendor blog, single runs, own harness; the blog cites **MT-Bench-101** (Bai et al. 2024, a multi-turn dialogue benchmark) for its "MT Bench" numbers, so they are not comparable with HF's MT-Bench 3.66/4.16. This is the closest published evidence to Max's target and undercuts the 400M-wall premise. https://huggingface.co/tiiuae/Falcon-H1-Tiny-90M-Instruct ; https://huggingface.co/spaces/tiiuae/tiny-h1-blogpost
2. **LFM2.5-350M (Liquid AI, 2026-03-31).** Pretraining extended from 10T to 28T tokens (~80k tokens/param) plus "large-scale multi-stage reinforcement learning". Card: Multi-IF (multi-turn instruction following) 44.92 vs LFM2-350M 32.92, Qwen3.5-0.8B-Instruct 41.68, Gemma 3 1B IT 44.25; IFEval 76.96 (vendor harness). Direct multi-turn evidence that a 350M model can match 0.8B-1B models. https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-350M
3. **JugnuLM-53M vs 110M (2026-09-13).** The only 2026 ablation ladder I found at Max's exact scale: a 110M deep-thin model (23 x 576, SmolLM2 tokenizer, ~8B FineWeb-Edu tokens). Value residuals and Muon (separately tuned LR: Muon 2e-2, AdamW 1.5e-3) were kept (ARC-Easy 52.5 to 56.1); offline top-16 logit KD from SmolLM2-1.7B (~15x the student) was dropped: heavy KD raised ARC-Easy to 56.99 but blew byte-perplexity from 1.93 to 2.18, light KD lost the gain. Supports the lane's pretraining-KD skepticism and its "teacher 2-3x the student" advice. Single runs, small token budget. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14715
4. **Strong Teacher Not Needed? (2026-05-22).** In pretraining distillation, small or undertrained teachers still improve students with proper CE/KD mixing, and stronger teachers "can saturate or even reverse the distillation gains"; KD helps OOD/downstream more than in-domain loss. Students 0.7B-8B on 50B tokens [>=1B mostly]. Relevant to whether the 124M shakedown model or a partially trained Ultra checkpoint could serve as a teacher. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23857
5. **Cross-tokenizer (on-policy) distillation, 2025-2026.** The lane's tokenizer section assumes only same-tokenizer teachers are usable and proposes adopting the SmolLM2 tokenizer. It misses methods that remove the constraint: byte-prefix marginalization for cross-tokenizer OPD (2607.22334), cross-family OPD with token mapping (2606.09456), SimCT (2605.07711), byte-level distillation through a byte decoder head (2604.07466, 1B-8B), approximate likelihood matching (2503.20083). Max's tokenizer is byte-level BPE, which suits the byte-level methods. All evidence at >= 1B students. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22334 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09456 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07711 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07466 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20083
6. **OPD data and success conditions (2026).** Data-free On-policy Distillation (2026-09-12): eight prompts match a 17k-problem dataset on "the two teacher-student pairings most common in practice" (scale not in abstract, likely >= 1B), so the OPD lever may need little data. Rethinking OPD (2604.13016): OPD succeeds only when teacher and student share compatible "thinking patterns" and the teacher offers genuinely new capabilities (1.5B/7B). https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14193 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13016
7. **Warm-start from a larger model (Where to Begin, 2510.07227).** Sub-network initialization plus KD: ~410M students need 1.71x fewer FLOPs than Pythia-410M; the best (Pythia-2.8B-comparable) model's advantage falls from 5.16x at 10B tokens to 1.26x at 100B. Directly relevant to the "weight inheritance from Ultra" lever: expect the benefit to fade on a long run. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07227
8. **Small-model RL/preference stability (2607.25091, 2026-07-27).** PPO on 70M-500M models (Pythia, SmolLM2-135M/360M): bf16 importance-ratio overflow, silent LoRA freezing, reward-model collapse; proposes a "capacity-headroom" rule (fluent SFT prior with PPL < 20 plus a discriminative reward) rather than a parameter threshold. Relevant to the lane's "post-train gently" item and to Max's fp16-only Titans. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25091
9. **Multi-token prediction hurts small models.** "Multi-token prediction models are worse than the baseline for small model sizes" (0.3B/0.6B worse, code). The lane covers no auxiliary objectives; this is a known do-not-use at 150M. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19737
10. **Over-training scaling laws with downstream prediction (Gadre et al.).** 104 models, 0.011B-6.9B, laws that extrapolate over-training and map loss to downstream error; a second anchor beside Sardana for 150M planning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08540
11. **Measurement quality of Max's A/B (verification omission, not a paper).** The lane took the README's 5.1/6.3/6.9% at face value. Held-out evals on that setup are dominated by eval-sample noise (claim 12). Any future 150M A/B should use a larger fixed validation set or paired single-pass training loss before drawing sub-5% conclusions, and should tune AdamW's LR rather than inherit 6e-4.

## 5. Summary judgement

The lane's citations are accurate: every arXiv number I traced matched its source, and its [calc] tables reproduce to 0.001 nats once the compute-matched convention is recovered. Its substantive problems: (1) it misreads IMU-1's 5.21% as optimizer-only (the optimizer part is 3.85%) and trusts a noisy val metric; Max's real 124M gain is ~4.5% (2.6% optimizer, 2.3% architecture) by paired training loss, and the AdamW arm used the exact LR Wen et al. identify as under-tuned. (2) It is out of date on 2026 models: Falcon-H1-Tiny-90M-Instruct and LFM2.5-350M undercut the 400M-wall premise harder than anything it cites, and JugnuLM-110M adds a same-scale KD negative. (3) Its central "break the wall" inference leans on Du et al. in a way the paper's fixed-corpus condition does not allow; the closest real test (Falcon SFT-pretraining at 90M) improved instruction following but not chat scores. Minor: the 1.7B-teacher crossover is ~350B not ~300B, MiniPLM scoring cost is ~2.4e20 not ~1.1e20, MiniPLM's 2.2x is versus vanilla KD, and SmolLM2-135M-Instruct's MT-Bench is 1.98.
