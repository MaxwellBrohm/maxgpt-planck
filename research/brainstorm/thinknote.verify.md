## 1. The tiny-scale answer

**Report:** no test exists of capped, history-stripped thinking, or of a running memory note, in a general chat model at or under 150M, measured on multi-turn recall or corrections.

**Verdict: the "No" stands, but the nearest-neighbour list is incomplete.** I searched for a counterexample and found none that meets all four conditions (at most 150M, open-domain chat, the model writes the think or note itself, scored on recall or corrections). But I found five items the report missed, and three of them are closer to Planck than several it lists:

| Missed neighbour | Size | What it has | What it lacks | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PENCIL** (ICML 2025) | 10.63M (SAT, QBF) and 25.19M (Einstein puzzle), 2,048 context | Program-generated thoughts that the model learns to **erase** once used, keeping only the answer (`C [CALL] T [SEP] A [RETURN]` becomes `C A`). Trained as a set of shorter cut sequences, with loss only on newly generated tokens. The answer's KV cache is recomputed after erasure, which is the report's re-prefill design. | Chat; multi-turn; recall | [arXiv 2503.14337](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14337), sec. 4 "Training" and "Implementation", App. A |
| **MemoryPrompt** (LREC-COLING 2024) | OPT-125M and OPT-350M, frozen, plus a small LSTM memory module (about 10% extra parameters at 350M) | A learned **running memory**, carried segment to segment. It was tested on (a) a fact-updating task that asks for the latest value of a fact that was overwritten, which is the corrections skill, and (b) Multi-Session Chat. OPT-125M + memory: 89.99 / 92.14 on the short and long no-distractor sets, against 47.39 / 34.97 for full-context OPT-125M. On MSC it reaches 20.60 perplexity, against 20.79 for full history. | Text notes (the memory is 5 soft-prompt vectors); generative chat; recall scoring on MSC (perplexity only). The full-context baselines were not fine-tuned, so the fact-update gap overstates the memory's advantage. | [arXiv 2402.15268](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15268), Tables 2-3 |
| **ConvFill / conversational infill** (v3, Jul 2026) | Talker SmolLM2-135M (also 360M-1.7B); the Reasoner is a frontier LLM | A 135M chat model that reads a stream of **thoughts written by another model** and folds them into its replies across multi-turn conversations (MultiWOZ, 991 graded turns; Everyday Conversations, 119). History lookback is 1 turn. SmolLM2-135M scores 90.0 / 89.5 on the NLI metrics for the sampled multi-turn sets. | The talker writes no thinking or note itself; no recall or correction items | [arXiv 2511.07397](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07397), sec. 6.1.4, Table 2 |
| **trlm-135m** (Sep 28 2025, hobby project) | 135M (SmolLM2-135M-Instruct) | Stage 1 SFT on about 58k "everyday conversations and instruction following" samples, stage 2 on about 78k `<think>` samples, then DPO. Its chat template **keeps full past assistant content**, so old thinking is not stripped. | Any multi-turn measurement (only ARC, BBH, GSM8K, MMLU are reported); no cap. The card calls it a research prototype. | [card](https://huggingface.co/Shekswess/trlm-135m), [chat_template.jinja](https://huggingface.co/Shekswess/trlm-135m/raw/main/chat_template.jinja) |
| **GRGPerDialogue** (PeerJ CS 2025) | BART-base (about 140M) or GPT-2 Medium/Large responder; persona written by Llama-2-13B | A small open-domain responder conditioned on a **text persona memory** retrieved per turn (MSC, Conversation Chronicles) | The small model writes nothing; scored by BLEU/ROUGE plus human "history relevance", not recall or corrections | [PMC12453736](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12453736/) |

Two further nearest neighbours sit just above the range:

- **Think Before You Speak** ([arXiv 2110.08501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.08501); DialoGPT-medium, about 345M). An open-domain chat model generates implicit commonsense knowledge before each reply, and that knowledge is fused into the history "after each turn". It is scored on response quality only. The report downloaded this PDF but did not cite it.
- **MIRROR** ([arXiv 2506.00430](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00430)). An inner monologue kept between turns, run on frontier LLMs.

**What this changes.**

1. The report's best small-scale evidence for its thinking arm should be **PENCIL, not Coconut**. PENCIL is size-matched: 10M-25M, like Planck's 10M and 30M points. It uses program-written thoughts that are erased after use, and it trains on cut sequences with loss only on new tokens. That is the T arm's exact training recipe, validated at Planck's size on synthetic reasoning.
2. **MemoryPrompt is the one prior result at about 125M that measures a running memory on the corrections skill.** It supports the note arm's premise, with the caveats above.
3. The report's sentence "Every reasoning-trained model under 150M either does not claim chat (MobileLLM-R1-140M) or says it lacks multi-turn support (Monad 56M)" needs trlm-135m added. It does chat-style SFT and keeps old thinking in its template, but it publishes no multi-turn number. MobileLLM-R1.5-140M (Nov 2025) repeats "not general-purpose chat models" ([card](https://huggingface.co/facebook/MobileLLM-R1.5-140M)).

| TN10 drop "(was X)" | untested | **stands**, reframed | The prior-art default already has no old value: DST states hold only current values, and Keep Me Updated!'s REPLACE drops the old sentence. | TN11 note placement | untested | **stands** (no placement ablation found in DST or chat) | none found |
| TN12 KV-retaining strip with packed masked training | untested | **overclaim** | The mechanism exists at 7B-8B. ## 5. What to change in the report

1. Add PENCIL, MemoryPrompt, ConvFill and trlm-135m to "Has it been tested at this size?" and to bottom-line item 1. PENCIL should anchor the T arm's small-scale case, ahead of Coconut.
2. Replace "packing ... is cheaper but not exact" with the exact token-duplication method (2504.18246). Keep cut sequences as the simple default, or adopt one-pass if chat tokens become the bottleneck.
3. Diable's teacher-forcing setup is Planck's plan. Promote TN9 (note-noise training), or at least a "train on self-written previous notes for part of stage 2" variant, into the note arm rather than leaving it as a later ledger item.
4. Add the Self-Notes caveat: where the scratchpad fits in context (Boolean Variable), notes and scratchpad tie.
5. Add the Qwen3 decoding confound to the 0.6B thinking vs non-thinking comparison.
6. Relabel TN12 and TN14 as above, and fix the compute range to 30-42 h.
