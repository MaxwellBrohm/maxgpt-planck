RC-12 SPEC: dev split and machinery. Version 0.1, 2026-09-24 (LEDGER P-003; with P-033, P-045, P-082)
Status: draft for the pre-registration (lock Oct 11-14). Every number marked (proposal) becomes final only when
prereg/RC-12.md is pushed, and never changes after. No Planck model is scored on any RC-12 split, dev included,
before that push. This file covers the DEV split and the machinery. The sealed split is written later, in a separate
session, from separate Claude-written templates and slot pools, encrypted at rest (PLAN Phase 0, P-049).
Sources: PLAN.md s1 (Level R/A wording) and Phase 0; research/REPORT.md s3, s5, s8 (D3, headroom rule); LEDGER
P-001..P-008, P-045, P-046; E004 notes and code (oracles, shortcut table, gen_grade.py, mutation tests); E001
battery.py (hardened chat graders: asserted spans, negation, hedges, hypotheticals, guess lists, person binding).

0. SCOPE AND BINDING RULES
  Target: conversational mechanics over the conversation's own context. Stored world knowledge is out of scope.
  Knowledge-free families: every gold is stated in the conversation (a user turn, a supplied passage, or the
    model's own earlier reply). Their scores make the composite. Knowledge-bearing items (family K) are scored and
    reported separately and never enter a composite or a claim.
  L1 Cheaters: every item is run through the fake scripted responders of s5 on the REAL items; they must fail.
  L2 No question, answer prefix or user turn after a statement contains that probe's gold word.
  L3 Every grader fails an empty reply, a loop, a hedge, a negation, a guess list, a reply in the user's voice
    (plus PLAN's list: wrong in-pool value, shotgun list, echo of the question, stale value, hypothetical), and
    passes a correct natural sentence. Each clause is mutation-tested: remove it, watch a fixture go red.
  L4 Knowledge-free and knowledge-bearing scores are never pooled.
  Dev text is Claude-written (D8 allows Claude for gold sets). Dev and sealed share no template or slot pool.
  Dev is never training text: its prompts go into the 13-gram decontamination list. E004 eval items are not
  reused (E004's fine-tunes saw its training streams); E004 and E001 CODE and ideas are reused and extended.

1. CONVERSATION FORMAT
  Every conversation has exactly 12 scripted user turns u1..u12. User text never depends on the model's replies.
  Own history: the harness sends u1, generates reply a1, appends it as the assistant message, sends u2, and so on
    to a12. Nothing from any gold, IDEAL reply or annotation is ever injected. The reply fed back is the decoded
    text after the stop rule (s2), with leading/trailing whitespace stripped; it is stored verbatim with its stop
    reason (eos, end-of-turn, role tag, cap).
  Turn kinds (annotation): S statement of a fact, L same-type lure (a value of the probe's type bound to another
    holder or object), C correction, I standing instruction, O override of an instruction, Q request that makes
    the model commit to an answer (family OWN), T passage or table (family LOOKUP), D distractor filler, P probe
    (graded), X second probe in the same conversation (graded; paired scoring where the family says so).
  Distance: d = index(P) - index(S) in user turns, 1..11 (S at u1 and P at u12 gives d = 11). For corrections,
    dc = index(P) - index(latest C of the asked object).
  Probe placement (unless a family's layout says otherwise): the main probe is at u12 in 3/4 of conversations
    and at u9-u11 in 1/4 (fillers follow), so "the last turn is the test" is not learnable from the format.
  Fillers (D): short knowledge-light how-to, why and small-talk requests; no names, no digits, no value of any
    RC-12 pool; a new dev pool of >= 80 fillers sharing 0 word 5-grams with E001/E004 fillers; each filler used at
    most once per conversation. Filler replies are graded only by the loop and role-leak rules (s4), never for
    content, so knowledge in a filler answer never moves a knowledge-free score.
  Length: user turns <= 35 words (LOOKUP passages <= 70 words). Budget: rendered with every reply set to 50 words,
    a conversation stays <= 1,800 tokens (proxy: 1.35 x words + 4 per turn until a Planck tokenizer exists; then
    measured). If a real rendered history exceeds (model context - 256), the harness drops the oldest whole
    user/assistant pairs, logs it, and any probe whose S, C, I, Q or T turn was dropped counts as failed (PLAN).
  Every conversation record (JSONL): id, split, family, cell, seed, turns [{i, kind, text}], probes [{turn,
    grader, gold or gold_fn, candidates, pool, stale, holder, object_words, pair_id}], knowledge flag.

2. RENDERS AND DECODING
  Chat-template render (primary for every model that ships a template): tokenizer.apply_chat_template(messages,
    add_generation_prompt=True), no system prompt, the model's default system behavior, enable_thinking=False
    for Qwen3-family models. Stop at the template's end-of-turn token(s) or eos; special tokens stripped on decode.
  Plain render (primary for models without a template; reported for every instruct model as a diagnostic, since
    rendering alone moved SmolLM2-135M recall 0.15 -> 0.70, REPORT s3.2):
      User: <u1>\nAssistant: <a1>\nUser: <u2>\nAssistant:
    Generation starts after "Assistant:"; stop at eos or at the first newline followed by a role tag (User:,
    Assistant:, Human:, case-insensitive). Newlines inside a reply are kept and fed back as written.
  Planck is scored in its own shipped template (named in the prereg) and also in the plain render.
  Decoding (PLAN, review 24): T = 0.6, top-p 1.0, repetition penalty off, max_new_tokens 256, 3 sampling seeds for
    every model; greedy reported as secondary. Engines per model are named in the prereg (vLLM or batched HF on
    the 5070, lockstep batches, greedy HF-vs-vLLM parity on 20 conversations per family of models).
  Likelihood rows (diagnostic, P-004): every probe with a closed candidate set also gets a row on GOLDEN history
    (IDEAL replies) with a forced answer prefix from a separate prefix pool: right iff the gold's summed log-prob
    is strictly above every other candidate (E004 lik.py rule; equal candidate token counts checked per tokenizer).
    Tier 0 margins (T0-L) are the per-checkpoint guard of PLAN s5.

3. FAMILIES (dev counts are conversations; knowledge-free unless marked)
  T0 Tier 0 (48). Two facts of DIFFERENT value types, each stated once and asked at d = 1-2; no lures; the rest
    fillers. Score = mean of the 2 probes. A gate, not in the composite (s6).
  RECALL (60). Recall of user-stated facts at distance. Layout: S (gold, bound to the user) plus 2 L (same type,
    bound to named others: "my sister's cat is ...") in u1..u10; gold position first/middle/last in thirds;
    d balanced 1-3 / 4-7 / 8-11 (16 each, 48 conversations). Abstain cell (12): the asked attribute was never
    stated for the user (only for others); an answerable X probe on another fact sits in the same conversation;
    the conversation passes only if the P abstains (s4 G-ABS) AND X is right.
  CORR (64). Corrections applied 4+ turns later. A's original, k = 1-3 corrections, a second object B of the same
    type (before A in half, after A's latest in half, followed by its own indirect correction there: E004 dev.2),
    dc >= 4 always (A's latest correction at least 4 user turns before the probe). Cells: U-diff 16 (the question
    and prefix frame-echo the ORIGINAL or an earlier correction and share no content word with the latest
    correction; the latest correction refers by pronoun, ellipsis or an alias defined in the original: E004 H1/H2),
    U-same 16 (latest correction by full phrase or head noun), C_noupd 16 (A never corrected, B corrected 1-3 times;
    A first in half, in the middle of B's statements in half), C_twoslot 16 (A corrected, then B stated and
    corrected after A's latest). Candidates = every value of the type in the conversation; stale values are named
    for the grader.
  BIND (60 = 30 pairs). Whose-is-whose in both mention orders. Two holders with a same-type attribute stated in
    u1..u6; the paired twin swaps only the mention order; the probe (u10-u12) asks the same holder in both.
    Unit = pair; right only if both twins are right (chance 0.25; "first mentioned wins" scores 0). Cells: owner
    12 (my X vs my sister's X), perspective 10 (my name vs the name I gave you; "What's my name?" and "What did I
    call you?" in alternate pairs), third-party 8 (two named others, neither the user).
  TWOHOP (48). Two-hop references: holder -> key -> value ("My cousin runs the bakery on the corner." ... "The
    bakery on the corner has a green door."), with 2 lure chains of the same shape; the probe names the holder by
    relation only ("What color is my cousin's door?"). A lure value statement frame-echoes the question. Chance 1/3.
    Diagnostic cell COMPOSE (16, P-033, not in the composite): two stated facts compared ("Which is older, my dog or
    my cat?"); the answer names a holder.
  PERSIST (48). Instruction persistence across 6-12 turns. I at u1 or u2: one checkable format rule (all caps;
    start with a given word; end with a given phrase; exactly one sentence; end with a question; include a given
    word). Every later reply is checked; score = compliance share over replies at distance 6-11 from I. Cells: hold
    32; override 16 (I1 at u1, O at u4-u5 replaces it with an incompatible rule; checked at distance >= 6 from O:
    the new rule followed AND the old one absent). Base rate of each rule = its compliance share in replies of
    every non-PERSIST conversation; a rule whose base rate exceeds 0.30 for any core baseline is dropped (proposal).
  OWN (48). References to the model's own earlier answer. Q at u2-u5 makes the model commit: pick 32 ("Pick one of
    these three names for my boat and tell me which: A, B or C."); list 16 ("Give me three ideas for X as a
    numbered list."). P at Q+4..Q+9 ("Which name did you go with?" / "What was the second idea on your list?").
    Gold = parsed from the model's OWN reply at Q (pick: exactly one offered option asserted by G-VAL; list: the
    2nd numbered item). A source reply that cannot be parsed scores the conversation 0 and is counted (source-fail
    rate reported). Lures: a new same-type value before Q (u1) and one after Q said by someone else ("My friend
    suggested D"); the after-lure frame-echoes the probe.
  TOPIC (48). Topic return after a digression. Three user projects stated in u1-u5, two explicitly closed ("that
    one's all sorted"), one left open with a pending decision; open-project position in thirds; 3-6 digression
    fillers; the probe asks to resume "the one I still haven't sorted out" without naming it. Gold = the open
    project's key noun; candidates = the three key nouns.
  ROLE (48). Role integrity. The user states name, job and town in u1-u3; in half the conversations the user also
    names the assistant. P "What's my name?" (u8-u12) and X "Tell me a little about yourself." (another turn >= u6).
    Pass iff P is right with no capture AND X asserts no user fact in the first person, does not claim the user's
    name, and (when the assistant was named) states the given name. A whole-conversation capture scan runs on every
    family as a diagnostic.
  LOOKUP (48). Reading from a supplied passage (LK skeleton, P-082). Two short fictional tables of the same shape
    (an old and a new rota, list or schedule; order balanced) in u1-u4; fillers; P asks a key of the named table
    (answerable), X asks a key absent from that table (must abstain, G-ABS). Unit passes only if both are right, so
    always-answer and always-abstain both score 0. Diagnostic conditions for later (not dev v1): shuffled table,
    no table, BM25-retrieved table.
  LOOP (40). Degeneration stress: open-ended continuation requests ("Tell me a short story about a lighthouse.",
    "Keep going.", "Now tell it from the keeper's side."); no requests to repeat or summarize. Score per
    conversation = share of its 12 replies that are not degenerate (s4 loop rule). The Level A loop rate is
    computed over EVERY reply of EVERY conversation, all families.
  K (knowledge-bearing, 32 + 32 one-turn controls; reported separately, P-045 design): a follow-up that needs a
    reference plus world knowledge ("I'm visiting Portugal." ... "What language do they speak there?") and the same
    question asked in one turn with the referent named. Reported: follow-up rate, control rate, their gap.
  Deferred from PLAN F1-F8 (decide before the lock): F6 sharded vs full, F7 persona, F8 social basics.

4. GRADERS (pure Python; no model)
  G-VAL strict value grader (closed candidate set). Right only if ALL hold (E004 gen_grade.py clauses 1-7 adapted
    to chat-length replies with E001 battery.py's sentence-scoped rules):
    v1 non-empty, not degenerate (loop rule below), stop reason is not the 256-token cap;
    v2 the gold is ASSERTED: some mention (whole word, case-insensitive; E004 value matching incl. "May", grey,
       number words) sits in a sentence that is not a question, not negated within 3 words before it, not hedged,
       not inside a hypothetical or conditional, not one of a list of alternatives ("X or Y", candidate bullets);
    v3 no other candidate is asserted; a stale value of the asked object may appear only inside a change phrase
       ("moved from X", "X was changed to") when the gold is asserted; any pool value NOT in the conversation fails
       (a guess); a reply mentioning every candidate fails (shotgun);
    v4 assistant voice: the gold's sentence does not bind a user fact to the first person ("My cat is Biscuit",
       "I'm Marcus": battery.py captures / FIRST_BIND, E004 clause 7 object words);
    v5 no role leak (no role tag at the start, no fake user turn inside) and not an echo (>= 80% of the reply's word
       4-grams copied from the probe question).
    Lenient (diagnostic only): the first candidate mentioned is the gold.
  G-ABS abstain grader: an abstain cue ("you haven't told me", "you didn't mention", "I don't know", "it isn't in
    the rota/list/note", "I don't have that") and no pool value mentioned at all (strict; a lenient twin allowing
    a candidate bound to its correct other holder is reported, since strict can fail a truthful reply).
  G-FMT format graders for PERSIST (caps ratio >= 0.9 over >= 5 letters; first word; closing phrase; one sentence;
    ends with "?"; word present), each also requires the reply to be fresh (battery.py fresh()).
  G-DYN for OWN: parse the source reply (single asserted option; numbered or bulleted list of exactly 3), then
    G-VAL with the parsed gold (pick) or content-word match (list: all content words of the gold item, <= 4 words,
    present; no content word unique to another item present).
  Loop rule (pre-registered exactly, per reply r at turn t):
    LOOP     some word 3-gram occurs >= 4 times in r (battery.degenerate); OR r, normalized, equals an earlier
             reply of the conversation; OR r has >= 12 words and >= 50% of its word 4-grams occur in ONE earlier
             reply of the conversation (self-copy).
    RUNAWAY  r stopped at the 256-token cap.   EMPTY no word characters.   LEAK a role tag or template marker
             inside r, or r continues into a fake user turn.
    Degenerate = any of the four. The Level A loop criterion counts LOOP only; the other three are reported beside.

5. CHEATERS AND ITEM GATES (run on the REAL dev items before any baseline is scored)
  Fake scripted responders (no model). Non-probe turns get a fixed "Okay, noted." unless the family needs a policy
  there (Q, I, O). Probe turns get a natural sentence built around the value picked by the rule:
    FIRST first in-context value of the probe's type     LAST latest one
    WORDING value after the longest word run shared with the question (E004 O4)
    OVERLAP statement with most content words shared with the question, latest wins (E004 O5, both variants)
    MARKER latest statement with a correction marker, else first (E004 O3)
    ECHO the question restated     DEFLECT "I'm not sure, could you remind me?"     SHOTGUN every candidate listed
    USERVOICE "My <object> is <gold>." (the gold in the user's voice)
    Family-specific: PERSIST never / once (only the next reply) / first-5 / obey-all-rules-ever; OWN picks by FIRST
    or LAST at Q too; LOOKUP always-answer (WORDING) and always-abstain; LOOP a scripted looper and a self-copier.
  IDEAL: the annotation's gold in a natural sentence, the right policy on every turn.
  Gates (script exits non-zero on any failure; tables written to logs/):
    G1 IDEAL scores 1.00 on every family and cell under the real graders.
    G2 every cheater: family score <= 0.40 on every scored family and <= 0.50 on every cell; USERVOICE, ECHO,
       DEFLECT and SHOTGUN score 0.00 on every value-graded unit (proposal). PERSIST and LOOP are gated by their
       own cheaters; a fixed reply repeated every turn fails freshness there after its first use.
    G3 no cheater meets any Level A criterion (CORR cells, BIND pair).
    G4 per item: at most 3 of the generic cheaters pass it; the per-item histogram is written. Items that fail are
       rebuilt, never deleted silently (count logged).
    G5 L2 on every item: no probe question, prefix or later user turn contains the probe's gold; fillers carry no
       name, digit or pool value.
    G6 grader fixtures and mutants (L3) all killed, none by a crash, on fixtures built from real items.
    G7 length budget of s1 on every conversation.
    G8 knowledge-free check: every gold of a knowledge-free probe occurs in a user turn (or is gold_fn on the
       model's own reply); K items are flagged knowledge-bearing.
    G9 every family's structural factors balanced exactly (positions, orders, cells, d bins).

6. METRICS AND COMPOSITES
  Unit scores in [0,1]: a conversation (mean of its graded probes) or a pair (BIND) or a both-right conversation
  (LOOKUP, RECALL abstain, ROLE). Family score F_f(m) = mean over units, then over the 3 sampling seeds.
  Level R composite R(m) = 100 x unweighted mean of F_f over the 10 scored knowledge-free families: RECALL, CORR,
    BIND, TWOHOP, PERSIST, OWN, TOPIC, ROLE, LOOKUP, LOOP (the list is frozen at the lock after the headroom rule).
    Paired difference D = R(Planck) - R(Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct). 10,000 bootstrap resamples: units resampled within
    each family (stratified), training seeds resampled, sampling seeds nested; percentile 95% CI. Level R holds when
    the CI's lower bound is >= -3 points, plus the OOD-H wording rule of PLAN s1 (proposal).
    Sensitivity row: the composite without families where Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct is <= 0.05.
  Level A (pooled over >= 3 training seeds; proposal thresholds): CORR U (U-diff + U-same) >= 0.80 AND C_noupd
    >= 0.80 AND C_twoslot >= 0.80; BIND pair rate >= 0.80; LOOP rate over all replies of all conversations <= 2%
    and no higher than Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct's; then the blind human test (PLAN s1).
  Tier 0 gate: T0 >= 0.90 (proposal) is required for any Level R or Level A claim; T0 failures are listed.
  Reported beside: per cell, RECALL by d bin, CORR by reference form, PERSIST by rule, OWN source-fail rate,
    lenient scores, likelihood rows, degenerate-type rates, capture rate, plain vs template render, greedy.

7. HEADROOM AND RE-ANCHOR
  Headroom rule (PLAN, D3): after the dev baselines, a family or Level A cell fails if every core baseline scores
    <= 0.05, or every core baseline scores >= 0.95 (template render, mean of 3 seeds). A failing family is rebuilt
    before the lock (harder or easier cells, same layout) or dropped from the composite with the reason in the
    prereg. So a family where every baseline sits at the floor cannot be "passed" by matching the floor.
  Re-anchor rule (PLAN, before the lock only): if LFM2-2.6B is below 0.80 on dev for a Level A cell, first audit
    the cell and its grader; if still below, that threshold becomes its dev rate rounded down to 0.05, floor 0.60.
